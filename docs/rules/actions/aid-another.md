# Aid Another
## Category
Special Attack / Combat Action

## Summary
In many circumstances, you can help an ally accomplish a task or perform better. However, additional help isn't always beneficial (e.g., Use Magic Device checks) and the DM can limit how aid another is used under specific conditions. Multiple helpers' bonuses stack.

## Mechanics

### 1. Aid Another in Combat
In melee combat, you can take a standard action to distract or interfere with an opponent, helping an ally attack or defend.
*   **Conditions:** You must be in a position to make a melee attack against an opponent that is engaging your ally in melee combat.
*   **Required Check:** Make an attack roll against AC 10.
*   **Success Outcome:** If you succeed, you grant your ally a +2 circumstance bonus. You must choose one of the following benefits:
    *   **Offense:** A +2 bonus on their next attack roll against that opponent.
    *   **Defense:** A +2 bonus to AC against that opponent's next attack.
    *   **Duration:** The bonus lasts until the beginning of your next turn.
*   **Base Attack Bonus Scaling:** If you have a Base Attack Bonus (BAB) of +5 or higher, the bonus you grant scales with your check result. For every 10 points your attack roll exceeds 10, the bonus increases by +1 (Bonus = `attack result / 10`, rounded down, + 1). E.g., a roll of 20 grants +3, and a roll of 30 grants +4.
*   **Attacks of Opportunity (AoO):** Aiding another in combat does not normally provoke. However, if you aid an ally who is performing an action that would normally provoke an AoO, the act of aiding another provokes attacks of opportunity in the same way. See [attacks-of-opportunity.md](./attacks-of-opportunity.md).

### 2. Aid Another for Skill Checks
You can assist an ally with a skill check, taking the same amount of time that the skill check normally requires.
*   **Required Check:** Make the same kind of skill check. You must roll the check; you cannot take 10 or 20.
*   **Success Outcome:** If your check result is 10 or higher, the ally you are helping receives a +2 circumstance bonus on their skill check.
*   **Expert Assistance Scaling:** If you have 5 or more ranks in the skill you are using to aid, the bonus you grant scales with your check result. For every 10 points your check result exceeds 10, the circumstance bonus increases by +1 (Bonus = `check result / 10`, rounded down, + 1).
*   **Restrictions:** In cases where a skill restricts who can achieve certain results, you cannot aid another if you couldn't attempt the task alone. E.g., a character without the trapfinding class feature cannot use aid another to help a rogue find a magic trap. See [use-skill.md](./use-skill.md).

### 3. Aid Another for Ability Checks
You can help an ally succeed on an ability check, taking the same amount of time that the ability check normally requires.
*   **Required Check:** Make the same kind of ability check. You must roll; you cannot take 10 or 20.
*   **Success Outcome:** If your check result is 10 or higher, your ally receives a +2 circumstance bonus. See [ability-scores.md](./ability-scores.md).

## Special Notes
*   **Stacking:** When multiple creatures aid the same ally, the circumstance bonuses they provide stack.
*   **Limits:** The DM may limit how many characters can aid a single ally at once based on physical space and logic (e.g., only a few people can fit around a door to force it open).
