# Use Feat

## Category
Miscellaneous Action

## Summary
Certain feats let you take special actions in combat. Other feats do not require actions themselves, but they give you a bonus when attempting something you can already do.

## Mechanics
The individual feat descriptions tell you what you need to know about them.

## Special Notes
Some feats are not meant to be used within the framework of combat.
