# Actions in Combat
## Category
Combat Basics

## Summary
During a combat round, a character can perform a variety of actions. This page summarizes the standard combat actions, their action types, whether they provoke attacks of opportunity (AoOs), and links to their detailed rules.

### Table Footnotes
1. **Aid Another:** If you aid someone performing an action that would normally provoke attacks of opportunity, then your aid another action also provokes attacks of opportunity. See [aid-another.md](./aid-another.md).
2. **Start/Complete Full-Round Action:** If you undertake an act that would normally provoke attacks of opportunity, then this standard action also provokes. You cannot use this to start or complete a full attack, charge, run, or withdraw. See [start-complete-full-round-action.md](./start-complete-full-round-action.md).
3. **Attack of Opportunity:** If you make an attack that would normally provoke attacks of opportunity (like an unarmed strike without the proper feat), then your attack of opportunity provokes attacks of opportunity. See [attacks-of-opportunity.md](./attacks-of-opportunity.md).
4. **Substitutions:** This attack form substitutes for a melee attack. As such, it can be used once in an attack or charge, or one or more times during a full attack. It can even be used as an attack of opportunity.
5. **Skills:** Disable Device, Heal, Open Lock, Search, and Use Rope provoke attacks of opportunity. Skills that involve movement provoke AoOs as appropriate for that movement.

## Special Notes
Even if an action does not normally provoke an attack of opportunity, you usually provoke if you move out of a threatened square during that action. You also provoke from an opponent when you enter that opponent's space.
