# Ready or Loose a Shield
## Category
Move Actions
## Summary
Strapping a shield to your arm to gain its bonus to AC, or unstrapping and dropping it to free your hand, is a move action.
## Mechanics
This action is required to either gain the benefits of a shield or to free the hand that holds it for other purposes.
- If you have a base attack bonus of +1 or higher, you can ready or loose a shield as a free action combined with a regular move.
- Dropping a carried (but not worn) shield is a free action.
## Special Notes
The ability to ready or loose a shield as a free action is a significant advantage for characters with a high base attack bonus, allowing for greater tactical flexibility.
