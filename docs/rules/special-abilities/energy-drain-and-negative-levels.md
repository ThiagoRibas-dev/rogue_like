# Energy Drain and Negative Levels

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes a supernatural ability to drain levels from victims, often by undead monsters.

## Mechanics
- **Attack:** Most energy drain attacks require a successful melee attack roll.
- **Negative Levels:** Each successful energy drain attack bestows one or more negative levels.
- **Penalties per Negative Level:**
    - –1 on all skill checks and ability checks.
    - –1 on attack rolls and saving throws.
    - –5 hit points.
    - –1 effective level (for die rolls or calculations using level).
    - Spellcasters lose access to one spell (highest level available) and one less spell slot at their highest spell level when preparing/regaining spells.
- **Duration & Removal:** Negative levels remain for 24 hours or until removed by a spell (e.g., `restoration`).
- **Fortitude Save:** After 24 hours, the afflicted creature must attempt a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 attacker’s HD + attacker’s Cha modifier) for each negative level.
    - **Success:** The negative level goes away with no harm.
    - **Failure:** The negative level goes away, but the creature’s level is also reduced by one.
- **Instant Death:** A character with negative levels at least equal to their current level, or drained below 1st level, is instantly slain.
- **Monster Creation:** Depending on the creature that killed them, they may rise the next night as a monster of that kind; otherwise, they rise as a wight.
- **Temporary Hit Points:** A creature gains 5 temporary hit points for each negative level it bestows (not if caused by a spell).
