# Low-Light Vision

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Allows characters to see twice as far as normal in dim light.

## Mechanics
- **Distance:** Can see twice as far as a normal human in conditions of dim light.
- **Color Vision:** Low-light vision is color vision.
- **Reading:** A spellcaster with low-light vision can read a scroll as long as there is a tiny source of light.
- **Moonlight:** Characters with low-light vision can see outdoors on a moonlit night as well as they can during the day.
