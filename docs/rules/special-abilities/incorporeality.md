# Incorporeality

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes creatures that lack physical bodies, being insubstantial and immune to nonmagical attacks.

## Mechanics
- **Harm:** Can be harmed only by other incorporeal creatures, magic weapons, or by spells, spell-like effects, or supernatural effects. Immune to all nonmagical attack forms.
- **Damage Chance:** Even when struck by magic or magic weapons, an incorporeal creature has a 50% chance to ignore any damage from a corporeal source (except for force effects or `ghost touch` weapons).
- **Immunities:** Immune to critical hits, extra damage from favored enemies, and sneak attacks.
- **Movement:** Move in any direction at will. Can pass through solid objects, although they cannot see when their eyes are within solid matter.
- **Senses:** Inaudible unless they decide to make noise. Hiding inside solid objects gives a +2 circumstance bonus on Listen checks.
- **Attacks:** Physical attacks of incorporeal creatures ignore material armor (even magic armor), unless it is made of force (e.g., `mage armor`) or has the `ghost touch` ability.
- **Environment:** Pass through and operate in water as easily as air. Cannot fall or take falling damage.
- **Physical Actions:** Corporeal creatures cannot trip or grapple incorporeal creatures.
- **Weight:** Have no weight and do not set off traps triggered by weight.
- **Traces:** Do not leave footprints, have no scent, and make no noise unless they manifest.
