# Fire Immunity

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
A creature with fire immunity never takes fire damage.

## Mechanics
- **Immunity:** Never takes fire damage.
- **Vulnerability:** Has vulnerability to cold, which means it takes half again as much (+50%) damage as normal from cold, regardless of whether a saving throw is allowed, or if the save is a success or failure.
