# Ability Score Loss

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes the effects of ability damage and ability drain, which reduce a character's ability scores.

## Mechanics
Various attacks cause ability score loss, either as temporary **ability damage** or permanent **ability drain**.

### Ability Damage
- Lost points return at a rate of 1 point per day per ability (or 2 points with complete bed rest).
- Spells like `lesser restoration` and `restoration` can offset ability damage.

### Ability Drain
- This loss is permanent.
- The `restoration` spell can restore points lost to ability drain.

### Effects of Ability Score 0
- **Strength 0:** Character cannot move and is helpless.
- **Dexterity 0:** Character cannot move and is helpless.
- **Constitution 0:** Character is dead.
- **Intelligence 0:** Character is unconscious in a coma-like stupor and helpless.
- **Wisdom 0:** Character is withdrawn into a deep sleep filled with nightmares and is helpless.
- **Charisma 0:** Character is withdrawn into a catatonic, coma-like stupor and is helpless.

A character’s ability score cannot drop below 0.

### Constitution Loss
If a character’s Constitution score drops, they lose 1 hit point per Hit Die for every point by which their Constitution modifier drops. A character's hit points cannot be reduced below 1 per Hit Die by Constitution loss.

## Special Notes
- The ability to drain ability scores is a supernatural ability.
- Some spells or abilities cause an effective ability score reduction, which is temporary and disappears when the effect ends.
