# Spell Resistance

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
An extraordinary ability to avoid being affected by spells.

## Mechanics
- **Caster Level Check:** To affect a creature with spell resistance, a spellcaster must make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level) at least equal to the creature’s spell resistance.
- **Automatic:** The possessor does not have to do anything special to use spell resistance.
- **Applicability:** Only spells and spell-like abilities are subject to spell resistance. Extraordinary and supernatural abilities are not.
- **Voluntary Lowering:** A creature can voluntarily lower its spell resistance as a standard action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity. It remains down until the creature’s next turn.
- **Self-Immunity:** A creature’s spell resistance never interferes with its own spells, items, or abilities.
- **No Stacking:** Spell resistance does not stack; it overlaps.

### When Spell Resistance Applies
- **Targeted Spells:** Applies if the spell is targeted at the creature.
- **Area Spells:** Applies if the resistant creature is within the spell’s area.
- **Effect Spells:** Most effect spells are not subject to spell resistance, unless they act upon a creature more or less directly (e.g., `web`).
- **Check Timing:** Check spell resistance when the creature is first affected by the spell. Check only once for any particular casting.
- **Indirect Effects:** Spell resistance does not apply if an effect fools the creature’s senses, reveals something about the creature, or if the creature is affected as a consequence of the spell acting on something else.
- **School Guidelines:**
    - **Abjuration:** Applies if the target is harmed, changed, or restricted.
    - **Conjuration:** Usually not subject to SR, unless it conjures energy.
    - **Divination:** Not subject to SR.
    - **Enchantment:** Typically subject to SR.
    - **Evocation:** Applies if it deals damage directly to the creature.
    - **Illusion:** Almost never subject to SR, unless it's a direct attack.
    - **Necromancy:** Most are subject to SR.
    - **Transmutation:** Subject to SR if they transform the target creature.

### Successful Spell Resistance
- Prevents a spell from affecting or harming the resistant creature.
- Never removes a magical effect from another creature or negates a spell’s effect on another creature.
- Prevents a spell from disrupting another spell.
