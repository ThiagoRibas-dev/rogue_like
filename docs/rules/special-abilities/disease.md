# Disease

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes how characters can contract and suffer from diseases, and how they can recover.

## Mechanics
When a character is injured by a contaminated attack, touches diseased matter, or consumes tainted food/drink, they must make an immediate Fortitude saving throw.
- **Success:** The disease has no effect.
- **Failure:** The character takes damage after an incubation period.
- **Repeated Damage:** Once per day afterward, a successful Fortitude saving throw avoids repeated damage. Two successful saving throws in a row indicate recovery.
- **Secret Rolls:** Fortitude saving throws can be rolled secretly.

### Disease Descriptions
Diseases have various symptoms and are spread through different vectors (ingested, inhaled, injury, contact).

| Demon fever | Injury 18 | 1 day | 1d6 Con² |
| Devil chills³ | Injury 14 | 1d4 days | 1d4 Str |
| Filth fever | Injury 12 | 1d3 days | 1d3 Dex, 1d3 Con |
| Mindfire | Inhaled 12 | 1 day | 1d4 Int |
| Mummy rot⁴ | Contact 20 | 1 day | 1d6 Con |
| Red ache | Injury 15 | 1d3 days | 1d6 Str |
| Shakes | Contact 13 | 1 day | 1d8 Dex |
| Slimy doom | Contact 14 | 1 day | 1d4 Con² |

¹ Each time the victim takes 2 or more damage from the disease, he must make another Fortitude save or be permanently blinded.
² When damaged, character must succeed on another saving throw or 1 point of damage is permanent drain instead.
³ The victim must make three successful Fortitude saving throws in a row to recover from devil chills.
⁴ Successful saves do not allow the character to recover. Only magical healing can save the character.

### Healing a Disease
- **Heal Skill:** Use of the Heal skill can help. If a healer makes a successful Heal check, the diseased character can use the healer’s result in place of their saving throw if it's higher. The character must be in the healer’s care and have rested for 8 hours.
- **Ability Score Recovery:** Characters recover points lost to ability score damage at a rate of 1 per day per ability, even while a disease is in progress.

## Special Notes
Diseases whose names are printed in italic in the table are supernatural; others are extraordinary.
