# Charm and Compulsion

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes two general types of enchantments that affect minds: charms and compulsions.

## Mechanics
### Charming
- **Effect:** Gives the charming character the ability to befriend and suggest courses of action to a minion. Servitude is not absolute or mindless.
- **Language:** A charmed creature doesn't gain magical ability to understand its new friend's language.
- **Allegiance:** Retains original alignment and allegiances, but regards the charming creature as a dear friend and gives great weight to suggestions.
- **Combat:** Fights former allies only if they threaten the new friend, using least lethal means.
- **Resistance:** Entitled to an opposed Charisma check to resist instructions that would make it do something it wouldn't normally do for a close friend. Success means it doesn't go along with the order but remains charmed.
- **Suicidal Commands:** Never obeys a command that is obviously suicidal or grievously harmful.
- **Breaking Free:** If commanded to do something violently opposed to its nature, the subject may attempt a new saving throw to break free entirely.
- **Attack:** If openly attacked by the charmer or their apparent allies, automatically freed.

### Compulsion
- **Effect:** Overrides the subject’s free will or changes how the subject’s mind works.
- **Obedience:** A charm makes the subject a friend; a compulsion makes the subject obey.

## Special Notes
Regardless of charm or compulsion, a character won't volunteer information or tactics not asked for.
