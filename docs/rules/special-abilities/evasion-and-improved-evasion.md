# Evasion and Improved Evasion

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Extraordinary abilities allowing the target of an area attack to avoid damage.

## Mechanics
### Evasion
- **Effect:** If subjected to an attack that allows a Reflex save for half damage, a character with evasion takes no damage on a successful save.
- **Movement:** Must have room to move to evade. A bound character or one squeezing cannot use evasion.
- **Reflexive:** Evasion is a reflexive ability; the character need not know the attack is coming.
- **Armor:** Rogues and monks cannot use evasion in medium or heavy armor. Some creatures with innate evasion do not have this limitation.

### Improved Evasion
- **Effect:** Like evasion, but even on a failed saving throw, the character takes only half damage.
