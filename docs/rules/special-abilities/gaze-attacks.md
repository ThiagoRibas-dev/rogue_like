# Gaze Attacks

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes attacks that affect targets by merely looking at them.

## Mechanics
- **Ability Type:** Gaze attacks not produced by a spell are supernatural.
- **Saving Throw:** Each character within range must attempt a saving throw (Fortitude or Will) each round at the beginning of their turn.
- **Averting Eyes:** An opponent can avert their eyes, looking at the creature’s body or shadow. This gives a 50% chance each round to avoid the saving throw, but the creature with the gaze attack gains concealment.
- **Closing Eyes:** An opponent can shut their eyes, turn their back, or wear a blindfold. They do not need to make a saving throw, but the creature with the gaze attack gains total concealment.
- **Active Gaze:** A creature can actively use its gaze as an attack action against a target within range, who must then attempt a saving throw (subject to the chances to avoid it if defending).
- **Immunity:** A creature is immune to its own gaze attack.
- **Visibility:** Limited visibility (dim light, fog) provides a percentage chance equal to the concealment miss chance to avoid the saving throw.
- **Invisibility:** Invisible creatures cannot use gaze attacks.
- **Darkvision:** Characters using darkvision are affected normally.
- **Control:** Unless specified otherwise, a creature can control its gaze and "turn it off".

## Special Notes
Looking at the creature’s image (in a mirror or illusion) does not subject the viewer to a gaze attack.
