# Fear

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes the effects of fear on characters, which can be shaken, frightened, or panicked.

## Mechanics
In most cases, a character makes a Will saving throw to resist this effect.

### Shaken
- **Effect:** –2 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks.

### Frightened
- **Effect:** Shaken, and flees from the source of fear as quickly as possible. Can choose the path of flight.
- **Action:** Once out of sight/hearing of the source, can act as they want. Can be forced to flee again if the source reappears.
- **Combat:** Can fight if unable to flee (though still shaken).

### Panicked
- **Effect:** Shaken, and runs away from the source of fear as quickly as possible. Path is random.
- **Action:** Flees from all other dangers. Cowers if prevented from fleeing.

## Special Notes
Fear effects are cumulative.
- A shaken character who is made shaken again becomes frightened.
- A shaken character who is made frightened becomes panicked.
- A frightened character who is made shaken or frightened becomes panicked.
