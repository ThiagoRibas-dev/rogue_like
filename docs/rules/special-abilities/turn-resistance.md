# Turn Resistance

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes how some creatures (usually undead) are less easily affected by the turning ability of clerics or paladins.

## Mechanics
- **Ability Type:** Turn resistance is an extraordinary ability.
- **Resolution:** When resolving a turn, rebuke, command, or bolster attempt, add the appropriate bonus to the creature’s Hit Dice total.
