# Resistance to Energy

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
An ability (usually extraordinary) to ignore some damage of a certain type each round.

## Mechanics
- **Definition:** Each resistance ability is defined by the energy type it resists and how many points of damage are resisted.
- **Source:** It doesn’t matter whether the damage has a mundane or magical source.
- **Spell Disruption:** When resistance completely negates the damage from an energy attack, the attack does not disrupt a spell.
- **Stacking:** This resistance does not stack with the resistance that a spell might provide.
