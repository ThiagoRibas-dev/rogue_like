# Fast Healing

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
An extraordinary ability allowing a creature to regain hit points at an exceptional rate.

## Mechanics
- **Rate:** At the beginning of each of the creature’s turns, it heals a certain number of hit points (defined in its description).
- **Nonlethal Damage:** A creature that has taken both nonlethal and lethal damage heals the nonlethal damage first.
- **Limitations:** Does not allow a creature to regrow or reattach lost body parts. Does not restore hit points lost from starvation, thirst, or suffocation. Does not increase hit points regained when a creature polymorphs.
