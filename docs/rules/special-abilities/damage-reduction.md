# Damage Reduction

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Allows creatures to ignore some damage from weapons or ignore blows.

## Mechanics
- **Numerical Part:** The amount of hit points the creature ignores from normal attacks.
- **Overcoming DR:** Usually overcome by special materials, magic weapons (+1 or higher enhancement bonus), certain weapon types (slashing, bludgeoning), or alignment-imbued weapons. A dash after the slash means it's effective against any attack that doesn't ignore DR.
- **Ammunition:** Ammunition from a +1 or higher projectile weapon is treated as magic for overcoming DR. Ammunition from an alignment weapon gains that alignment.
- **Negated Effects:** When DR completely negates damage, it also negates most special effects like injury poison, monk's stunning, and injury disease.
- **Ignored Effects:** Does not negate touch attacks, energy damage with an attack, or energy drains. Does not affect poisons or diseases delivered by inhalation, ingestion, or contact.
- **Spell Disruption:** Attacks dealing no damage due to DR do not disrupt spells.
- **Magic & Energy:** Spells, spell-like abilities, and energy attacks (even nonmagical fire) ignore damage reduction.
- **Stacking:** If a creature has DR from multiple sources, they do not stack; the creature gets the benefit of the best DR in a given situation.

## Special Notes
Characters can see that conventional attacks don't work against creatures with DR.
