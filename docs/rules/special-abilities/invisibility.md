# Invisibility

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes the state of being visually undetectable, and how other senses can be used to locate invisible creatures.

## Mechanics
- **Vision:** Invisibility makes a creature undetectable by vision, including darkvision.
- **Immunities:** Does not, by itself, make a creature immune to critical hits, but does make it immune to extra damage from a ranger’s favored enemy and from sneak attacks.
- **Detection:**
    - **Spot:** A creature can notice an active invisible creature within 30 feet with a DC 20 Spot check (DC 30 if holding still, DC 40 for inanimate/unliving). Pinpointing location is practically impossible (+20 DC).
    - **Listen:** A successful Listen check (vs. Move Silently) reveals the creature's presence. Beating the DC by 20 pinpoints the location.
    - **Groping:** A character can make a touch attack into two adjacent 5-foot squares as a standard action. If successful (with a 50% miss chance), it deals no damage but pinpoints the location.
    - **Being Struck:** If an invisible creature strikes a character, the struck character knows the location of the creature until it moves.
- **Attacking an Invisible Creature:**
    - **Pinpointed:** Attack normally, but the invisible creature benefits from full concealment (50% miss chance).
    - **Not Pinpointed:** Player chooses a space to attack. If the creature is there, attack normally (with 50% miss chance). If not, the attack misses.
- **Visible Objects:** If an invisible creature picks up a visible object, the object remains visible. They can hide small visible items on their person.
- **Tracks:** Invisible creatures leave tracks and can be tracked normally.
- **Other Senses:**
    - **Scent:** A creature with the scent ability can detect an invisible creature as it would a visible one.
    - **Blind-Fight Feat:** Roll miss chance twice; miss only if both rolls indicate a miss (or make one 25% miss chance roll).
    - **Blindsight:** Can attack and interact with creatures regardless of invisibility.
- **Light:** An invisible burning torch still gives off light, as does an invisible object with a `light` spell cast upon it.
- **Ethereal/Incorporeal:** Ethereal creatures are invisible but not materially present, so most senses don't help. Incorporeal creatures are often invisible; Spot and Listen checks can help, but Scent, Blind-Fight, and blindsight do not.
- **Gaze Attacks:** Invisible creatures cannot use gaze attacks.
- **Detect Spells:** Invisibility does not thwart detect spells.
