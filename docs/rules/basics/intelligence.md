# Intelligence (INT)

## Category
Ability Score

## Summary
Determines how well a character learns and reasons, affecting spells and skills.

## Mechanics
Apply Intelligence modifier to:
*   The number of languages your character knows at the start of the game.
*   The number of skill points gained each level (minimum 1).
*   Appraise, Craft, Decipher Script, Disable Device, Forgery, Knowledge, Search, and Spellcraft checks.
A wizard gains bonus spells based on her Intelligence score. The minimum Intelligence score needed to cast a wizard spell is 10 + the spell’s level.

## Special Notes
An animal has an Intelligence score of 1 or 2. A creature of humanlike intelligence has a score of at least 3.
