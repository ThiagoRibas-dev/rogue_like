# Constitution (CON)

## Category
Ability Score

## Summary
Represents a character’s health and stamina, increasing hit points.

## Mechanics
Apply Constitution modifier to:
*   Each roll of a Hit Die (minimum 1 hit point gained).
*   Fortitude saving throws.
*   Concentration checks.
If Constitution score changes enough to alter its modifier, hit points increase or decrease accordingly.

## Special Notes
Important for all classes due to its effect on hit points.
