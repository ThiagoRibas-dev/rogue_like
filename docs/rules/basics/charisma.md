# Charisma (CHA)

## Category
Ability Score

## Summary
Measures a character’s force of personality, persuasiveness, personal magnetism, and ability to lead.

## Mechanics
Apply Charisma modifier to:
*   Bluff, Diplomacy, Disguise, Gather Information, Handle Animal, Intimidate, Perform, and Use Magic Device checks.
*   Checks that represent attempts to influence others.
*   Turning checks for clerics and paladins attempting to turn undead.
Sorcerers and bards get bonus spells based on their Charisma scores. The minimum Charisma score needed to cast a sorcerer or bard spell is 10 + the spell’s level.

## Special Notes
Most important for paladins, sorcerers, and bards. Also important for clerics.
