# Alignment
## Category
Core Rule
## Summary
A creature's alignment represents its general moral and personal attitudes, defined by combinations of the lawful-chaotic axis with the good-evil axis.
## Mechanics
### Good vs. Evil
Good characters protect innocent life and make personal sacrifices to help others. Evil characters debase or destroy innocent life, hurting, oppressing, and killing others for fun or profit. Neutral characters have compunctions against killing the innocent but lack commitment to protect or help others, often committed by personal relationships. Animals and creatures incapable of moral action are neutral.
### Law vs. Chaos
Lawful characters tell the truth, keep their word, respect authority, honor tradition, and judge those who fall short of their duties. Chaotic characters follow their consciences, resent being told what to do, favor new ideas over tradition, and do what they promise if they feel like it. Neutral characters have a normal respect for authority, are honest but can be tempted into lying or deceiving others. Animals and creatures incapable of moral action are neutral.
### The Nine Alignments
*   **Lawful Good, "Crusader"**: Combines commitment to oppose evil with discipline. Tells the truth, keeps word, helps those in need, speaks out against injustice. Hates to see the guilty go unpunished.
*   **Neutral Good, "Benefactor"**: Does the best a good person can do, devoted to helping others. Works with authorities but is not beholden to them.
*   **Chaotic Good, "Rebel"**: Acts as conscience directs, with little regard for expectations. Makes own way, kind and benevolent. Believes in goodness but has little use for laws.
*   **Lawful Neutral, "Judge"**: Acts as law, tradition, or a personal code directs. Order and organization are paramount. May believe in personal order or strong government.
*   **Neutral, "Undecided"**: Does what seems like a good idea, without strong feelings on good vs. evil or law vs. chaos. Most lack conviction; some philosophically commit to neutrality as a balanced view.
*   **Chaotic Neutral, "Free Spirit"**: Follows whims, individualist. Values own liberty but doesn't strive to protect others' freedom. Avoids authority, resents restrictions, challenges traditions. Unpredictable but not totally random.
*   **Lawful Evil, "Dominator"**: Methodically takes what he wants within his code of conduct, without regard for whom it hurts. Cares about tradition, loyalty, and order, but not freedom, dignity, or life. Plays by rules without mercy.
*   **Neutral Evil, "Malefactor"**: Does whatever she can get away with, purely for self-interest. Sheds no tears for those killed. No love of order, no illusion that laws make her better.
*   **Chaotic Evil, "Destroyer"**: Driven by greed, hatred, and lust for destruction. Hot-tempered, vicious, arbitrarily violent, and unpredictable. Plans are haphazard, groups poorly organized.
## Special Notes
Alignment is a tool for developing a character's identity, not a restriction. Individuals vary from the norm, and few people are completely consistent. Being good or evil can be a conscious choice, but for most, it's an attitude. Devotion to law or chaos is often a personality trait rather than a choice. The first six alignments (Lawful Good through Chaotic Neutral) are standard for player characters; the three evil alignments are for monsters and villains.
