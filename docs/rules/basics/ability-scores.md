# Ability Scores

## Category
Basic Rule

## Summary
Describes the six core abilities that define a character's fundamental capabilities.

## Mechanics
Each ability, after changes made because of race, has a modifier ranging from –5 to +5. This modifier is applied to die rolls and other numbers related to that ability. A positive modifier is a bonus, and a negative modifier is a penalty.

### Abilities and Spellcasters
The ability governing bonus spells depends on the spellcaster type: Intelligence for wizards; Wisdom for clerics, druids, paladins, and rangers; or Charisma for sorcerers and bards. A spellcaster must also be of high enough class level to cast spells of a given level.

## Special Notes
When an ability score changes, all associated attributes change accordingly. Skill points are not retroactively gained for previous levels if Intelligence increases.
