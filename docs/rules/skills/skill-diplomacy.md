# Skill: Diplomacy
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Diplomacy skill represents your ability to influence others through etiquette, social grace, tact, and persuasion.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Time and Action
*   **Standard Persuasion:** Changing an NPC's attitude requires at least **1 minute** of interaction.
*   **Rushed Persuasion:** You can attempt to change an attitude as a **Full-Round Action**, but you take a **-10 penalty** on the check.
*   **Haggling:** Takes **1 minute**.
*   **Mediation:** Takes a significant period of negotiation (often hours or days), but can be rushed to **1 hour** by taking a **-10 penalty** on the check.

### Retry Limits
*   **Standard/Haggling Failure:** If your check fails to improve the NPC's attitude, they become firmly committed to their position. No retries are allowed for the same topic.
*   **Mediation Retries:** You can retry a mediation check as long as at least one of the parties remains unfriendly or better toward the other.

## NPC Attitudes and DCs

Use this table to determine the check DC required to move an NPC from their initial attitude to a new target attitude:

| Initial Attitude | Hostile | Unfriendly | Indifferent | Friendly | Helpful |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Hostile** | $< 20$ | 20 | 25 | 35 | 50 |
| **Unfriendly** | $< 5$ | 5 | 15 | 25 | 40 |
| **Indifferent** | — | $< 1$ | 1 | 15 | 30 |
| **Friendly** | — | — | $< 1$ | 1 | 20 |
| **Helpful** | — | — | — | $< 1$ | 1 |

### Attitude Definitions and Activities

*   **Hostile:** Will take risks to hurt or oppose you. (Actions: Attack, actively interfere, report you).
*   **Unfriendly:** Wishes you ill. (Actions: Mislead, spread gossip, avoid, watch suspiciously, insult).
*   **Indifferent:** Doesn't care either way. (Actions: Socially interact, trade, chatter).
*   **Friendly:** Wishes you well. (Actions: Chat, advise, offer minor/risk-free help, advocate).
*   **Helpful:** Will take risks to help you. (Actions: Protect, back you up, heal, give aid, donate items).

## Specialized Uses

### 1. Haggling (Lowering Prices)
You can attempt to lower the price of an item by negotiating with a merchant.
*   **Resolution:** Make a Diplomacy check. The DC is the typical DC for influencing the NPC plus the **seller's Diplomacy modifier**.
*   **Success (Helpful):** You receive a **10% discount** on the item.
*   **Failure (Unfriendly or worse):** The merchant takes offense and refuses to sell anything to you for at least 24 hours.

### 2. Mediation (Negotiating Peace)
You can act as a mediator to resolve disputes between two external groups or leaders.
*   **Resolution:** Make a Diplomacy check. The DC is the standard DC for influencing the target's attitude plus the **group leader's Diplomacy modifier**.
    *   *Cultural Penalty:* The DC increases by **+5** if the two parties are from different cultures or races.
*   **Success:** You successfully adjust each group's attitude toward the other party to **friendly or better**.

### 3. Opposed Diplomacy Checks
In formal debates, hearings before a judge, or high-stakes negotiations between advocates, participants roll opposed Diplomacy checks instead of target DCs. The character with the higher check result wins the argument or gains the negotiation advantage.
