# Skill: Listen
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Listen skill is used to perceive noises, hear whispered conversations, detect sneaking creatures, or identify the sound of approaching danger.

*   **Key Ability:** Wisdom (Wis)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### DCs by Sound Type

| Sound / Action | DC | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Battle / Screaming** | -10 | A heated combat nearby. |
| **People talking** | 0 | Normal conversation. |
| **Unseen creature walking** | 10 | A creature moving naturally in plain clothes. |
| **Whispered conversation** | 15 | Someone whispering to another. |
| **Key turning in a lock** | 20 | A subtle mechanical sound. |
| **Opposed Stealth** | Opposed | Opposed by the target's **Move Silently** check. |

### Listen Modifiers (Stacking)

| Condition | DC Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Distance** | +1 per 10 feet of distance |
| **Through a wooden door** | +5 |
| **Through a stone wall** | +15 |
| **Listener is distracted** | +5 |
| **Listener is asleep** | +10 |
| **Incorporeal hiding in solid object** | -2 (bonus to listener) |

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You hear the noise and can discern its general direction. If you beat the DC by **20 or more**, you can pinpoint the exact location of an unseen or invisible sound source.
*   **Failure:** You hear nothing. You can retry on a later round if the sound continues.

---

## Special Interactions
*   **Blindness:** Blinded creatures do not lose their ability to Listen. They often rely on Listen checks to locate opponents (see [conditions.md](./conditions.md) and [senses-and-perception.md](./senses-and-perception.md)).
*   **Deafness:** Deafened creatures automatically fail all Listen checks.
*   **Listen-to-Spot Synergy:** Hearing a sound can alert you to look in a specific direction, allowing you to make a Spot check with a $+2$ circumstance bonus to locate the source.
