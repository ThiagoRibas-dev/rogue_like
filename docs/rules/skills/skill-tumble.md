# Skill: Tumble
## Category
Skill Rules (St-Z) & Cross-Referencing

## Overview
The Tumble skill represents your ability to dive, roll, somersault, and slide, allowing you to move through threatened areas without provoking attacks of opportunity or stand up quickly in combat.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Tumble checks)

---

## Mechanics

### Actions and Movement
*   **Half Speed (Default):** While tumbling, you move at **half speed** (but only for the portion of movement spent tumbling through threatened squares).
*   **Full Speed (+10 DC):** You can move at your full speed while tumbling by adding **+10** to the DC of all checks.
*   **Running (+20 DC):** You can tumble while running by adding **+20** to the DC of all checks.

---

## Task DCs

| Task | Base DC | Failure Consequence |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Tumble past opponent** (avoid AoO) | 15¹ | You provoke attacks of opportunity from the opponent normally. |
| **Tumble through opponent's space** | 25¹ | You stop immediately before entering their space and provoke an AoO. |
| **Stand up from Prone** | 35 | Stands up as a **Free Action** (instead of a move action). *Note: Still provokes an AoO.* |

¹ *Add **+2 to the DC** for each additional opponent after the first you attempt to avoid during the same movement. If you pass multiple opponents at once, choose which one to check against first.*

---

## Treacherous Surface Modifiers

Obstructed or slippery ground increases the difficulty of tumbling:

| Surface Condition | DC Modifier | Examples |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Lightly slippery** | +2 | Wet tavern floor, damp stones. |
| **Sloped or angled** | +2 | Steep stairs, cavern ramp. |
| **Severely obstructed** | +5 | Cavern floor, heavy debris, dense undergrowth. |
| **Severely slippery** | +5 | Sheet of ice, grease spill. |
| **Deep Bog** | Impossible | You cannot tumble under any circumstances. |

---

## Synergy Benefits

Investing ranks in Tumble improves several combat and physical capabilities:

*   **To Balance:** 5+ ranks in Tumble grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Balance checks.
*   **To Jump:** 5+ ranks in Tumble grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Jump checks.
*   **Fighting Defensively:** 5+ ranks in Tumble increases the dodge bonus to AC when fighting defensively from **+2 to +3**.
*   **Total Defense:** 5+ ranks in Tumble increases the dodge bonus to AC when using the Total Defense action from **+4 to +6**.
