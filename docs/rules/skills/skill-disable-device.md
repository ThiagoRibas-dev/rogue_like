# Skill: Disable Device
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Disable Device skill represents your ability to jam locks, sabotage mechanical devices, disarm traps, or dismantle complex machinery.

*   **Key Ability:** Intelligence (Int)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
    *   *Exception:* Untrained characters can attempt simple, non-hazardous tasks (such as jamming a basic lock), but cannot disarm complex mechanical traps or any magic traps.
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Secret Checks
Disable Device checks are made **secretly by the DM**, so you do not know if you succeeded or if you failed and are about to trigger a hazard.

### DCs and Time Requirements

The time required to disable a device depends on its complexity:

| Device Complexity | DC | Time Required | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Simple** | 10 | 1d4 rounds | Jamming a basic lock. |
| **Tricky** | 15 | 1d4 rounds | Sabotaging a wagon wheel to fall off. |
| **Difficult** | 20 | 2d4 rounds | Disarming a standard trap. |
| **Wicked** | 25 | 2d4 rounds | Disarming a complex mechanical trap or clockwork engine. |
| **Magic Trap**¹ | 25 + spell level | 2d4 rounds | Disarming a *glyph of warding* or *fire trap*. |

¹ *Requires the Trapfinding class feature.*

### DC Modifiers

| Desired Modification | DC Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Leave no trace of tampering** | +5 |
| **Accelerate task to 1 round** | +20 |

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You disable or disarm the device.
*   **Failure by 4 or Less:** You fail to disable the device, but nothing happens. You can try again.
*   **Failure by 5 or More:** Something goes wrong.
    *   *If the device is a trap:* You immediately spring it.
    *   *If the device is a sabotage attempt:* You believe the device is disabled, but it still functions normally.

---

## Special Actions

### Bypassing Traps
If you have the **Trapfinding** class feature and beat a trap's DC by **10 or more**, you can:
1.  Study the trap and bypass it without disarming it.
2.  Bypass the trap along with your companions without triggering it.
3.  **Insert Bypass Element:** Intentionally add a bypass mechanism allowing you and your companions to easily avoid triggering the trap in the future.
