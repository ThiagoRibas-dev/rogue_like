# Skill: Speak Language
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
Unlike other skills, Speak Language functions more like an automatic proficiency than a check-based skill. Each rank purchased grants fluency in one additional language.

*   **Key Ability:** None (no ability score modifier applies)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### How It Works
*   **No Checks:** Speak Language is never rolled. You either know a language (and speak it fluently) or you do not.
*   **Buying Languages:** Each rank of Speak Language grants **one additional language**. Cross-class ranks cost 2 skill points but still grant only one language.
*   **Starting Languages:** All characters begin play speaking their native tongue and any bonus languages granted by their Intelligence modifier or race (at no skill point cost).

> **Druidic** is a secret language known only to Druids. Non-Druids cannot purchase it with Speak Language ranks. Druids gain it automatically at 1st level and are forbidden from teaching it to non-Druids.

## Reading and Writing
*   All characters who know a language can speak it. **Literacy** (reading and writing) is assumed for all player character classes except Barbarians, who must spend 2 skill points to learn to read and write.
*   Knowing a language's spoken form does not automatically grant knowledge of its written script (though they are usually grouped together for game purposes unless the DM rules otherwise).
