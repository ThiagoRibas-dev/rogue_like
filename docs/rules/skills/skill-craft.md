# Skill: Craft
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Craft skill represents your ability to create, manufacture, or repair physical objects. Crafting is a downtime activity that allows characters to fabricate weapons, armor, tools, and alchemical items.

*   **Key Ability:** Intelligence (Int)
*   **Trained Only:** No (anyone can attempt standard crafting, though alchemical items require training)
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

To craft an item, you must pay for raw materials and dedicate time to make weekly or daily progress checks.

### Step-by-Step Crafting Process

1.  **Determine Price:** Find the item's price in **silver pieces** (1 gp = 10 sp).
2.  **Determine DC:** Find the item's Craft DC (see table below).
3.  **Pay for Materials:** Pay **one-third (1/3)** of the item's total price in raw materials.
4.  **Perform Work:** Work for one week (or one day) and make a Craft check.
5.  **Calculate Progress:**
    *   *If the check succeeds:* Multiply your **check result** by the **item's DC**.
        *   If the product (Result $\times$ DC) **equals or exceeds** the item's price in silver pieces (sp), the item is complete.
        *   If the product is less than the price, it represents progress. Record the product. In subsequent weeks, repeat the check and add the new products to the total. When the accumulated total equals or exceeds the price in sp, the item is complete.
    *   *If the check fails by 4 or less:* You make no progress this week/day.
    *   *If the check fails by 5 or more:* You ruin half of the raw materials. You must pay half of the original material cost (1/6 of the item's price) to replace the ruined materials before continuing.

### Daily Crafting Option
Instead of weekly checks, you can work day-by-day:
*   Divide the item's price in silver pieces (sp) by **7**. This is your daily progress target.
*   Make a check at the end of the day. If it succeeds, multiply the check result by the DC. Compare this product to the daily progress target.

## Craft DCs

### 1. Armor and Shields
*   **Shields, Light Armor:** DC 10
*   **Medium Armor:** DC 15
*   **Heavy Armor:** DC 15

### 2. Weapons and Ammunition
*   **Simple Weapon, Ammunition:** DC 12
*   **Martial Weapon:** DC 15
*   **Exotic Weapon:** DC 15
*   **Bow:** DC 12
*   **Composite Bow:** DC 15
*   **Composite Bow (with Strength Rating):** DC 15 + 2 per point of Strength rating (e.g., a Bow with +4 Str has a DC of 23).

### 3. Miscellaneous Items
*   **Locks:** 
    *   *Simple:* DC 20
    *   *Average:* DC 25
    *   *Good:* DC 30
    *   *Amazing:* DC 35
*   **Traps:** Varies by trap level (typically DC 20 to 30; see [skill-disable-device.md](./skill-disable-device.md)).

### 4. Alchemical Items (Trained Only)
You must be a spellcaster or possess the *Alchemy* class feature to craft alchemical items:
*   **Acid:** DC 15
*   **Smokestick / Tindertwig:** DC 20
*   **Alchemist's Fire / Thunderstone:** DC 20
*   **Antitoxin:** DC 25

---

## Repairing Damaged Items
You can use the Craft skill to repair a damaged object (see [objects.md](./objects.md)):
*   **Cost:** Pay **one-fifth (1/5)** of the item's total price in raw materials.
*   **Action:** Make a Craft check against the item's standard craft DC.
*   **Success:** A single successful check completes the repair.
*   **Failure:** Standard failure rules apply (failing by 5+ ruins half the repair materials).
