# Skill: Disguise
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Disguise skill is used to alter your appearance, allowing you to blend into crowds, impersonate others, or pass yourself off as another race, age, or gender.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Time and Preparation
*   **Work Time:** Creating a disguise requires **1d3 $\times$ 10 minutes** of preparation.
*   **Materials:** Requires appropriate makeup, props, and clothing.
*   **Secret Check:** The DM rolls your Disguise check secretly when the disguise is made. Only one roll is made per disguise.

### Disguise Modifiers

| Appearance Alteration | Check Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Minor details only** | +5 |
| **Different gender** | -2 |
| **Different race** | -2 |
| **Different age category**¹ | -2 per step |
| **Form-altering magic**² | +10 |

¹ *Steps: Young (under adulthood), Adulthood, Middle Age, Old, Venerable. A 20-year-old disguised as a venerable elder takes a -6 penalty (-2 per step).*
² *Spells like alter self, disguise self, polymorph, or shapechange. True seeing bypasses the magic bonus but does not penetrate the mundane part of the disguise.*

### Size and Weight Limits
A mundane disguise cannot alter your apparent height or weight by more than **one-tenth (1/10)** of your original size. You cannot disguise yourself as a different size category without magic.

---

## Detection

### Observers and Spot Checks
Your Disguise check result acts as the DC for observers' opposed **Spot** checks:
*   **Casual Observers:** If you do not draw attention to yourself, observers do not make Spot checks.
*   **Meeting Observers:** Observers make a Spot check immediately upon meeting you, and another check each hour thereafter.
*   **Suspicious Observers:** Observers who have reason to suspect you are automatically considered to be taking 10 on their Spot check.
*   **Groups:** If you casually interact with many different creatures in a short time, the DM rolls a single Spot check once per hour using the average Spot modifier of the group.

### Familiarity Modifiers
If you are impersonating a specific individual, observers who know that person receive a bonus to their Spot check and are automatically considered suspicious:

| Familiarity Level | Spot Check Bonus |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Recognizes on sight** | +4 |
| **Friends or associates** | +6 |
| **Intimate** (close family, spouse, sibling) | +10 |

---

## Disguising Objects
You can also use this skill to disguise the appearance of objects (e.g., hiding a mechanism or altering a lock's look):
*   **Limits:** An object's disguise cannot alter its apparent size or weight by more than 1/10 unless specialized accessories are used (requiring a separate Craft check).
*   **Resolution:** Your Disguise check is opposed by the **Search** checks of anyone examining the object or searching the area.
