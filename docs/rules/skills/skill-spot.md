# Skill: Spot
## Category
Skill Rules (St-Z) & Cross-Referencing

## Overview
The Spot skill represents visual perception, allowing you to notice sneaking creatures, spot hidden objects, read lips, or notice subtle details in your surroundings.

*   **Key Ability:** Wisdom (Wis)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Requirements
*   **Opposed Check:** When looking for a hiding creature, your Spot check is opposed by the target's **Hide** check (see [skill-hide.md](./skill-hide.md)).
*   **Passive vs. Active:** The DM might make a passive Spot check for you when something enters your field of view, or you can spend an action to actively scan an area.

### Visual DCs and Modifiers

| Condition / Task | DC / Modifier | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Spot a visible creature in plain sight** | 0 | The base starting difficulty. |
| **Read Lips** | 15 | Requires training. Read 10 minutes of conversation. Fail = no read. Fail by 5+ = false info. |
| **Notice a disguise** | Opposed Disguise | See [skill-disguise.md](./skill-disguise.md). |
| **Distance** | +1 DC per 10 feet | The most common penalty applied to Spot checks. |
| **Poor Visibility** | +5 to +10 DC | Dust, mist, or low light (see [weather.md](./weather.md)). |
| **Spotter is distracted** | +5 DC | Actively engaged in combat, casting, or another task. |

---

## Read Lips (Trained Only)
You can use Spot to understand what someone is saying by watching their mouth:
*   **Requirements:** You must be within **30 feet** of the speaker, have a clear line of sight, and speak the language they are using (see [skill-speak-language.md](./skill-speak-language.md)).
*   **Action:** Concentration for **10 minutes**.
*   **DC:** **15** (increases for fast talkers or obscured views).
*   **Outcome:**
    *   *Success:* You understand the general content of the conversation during that period.
    *   *Failure:* You do not understand.
    *   *Failure by 5 or more:* You misinterpret the conversation, getting incorrect information.

---

## Special Interactions
*   **Invisibility:** You cannot spot an invisible creature visually. However, a successful DC 20 Spot check can detect a general "shimmer" or disturbances in the environment, indicating the presence of an active invisible creature, though you cannot pinpoint its square (see [senses-and-perception.md](./senses-and-perception.md)).
*   **Blindness:** Blinded creatures automatically fail all Spot checks (see [conditions.md](./conditions.md)).
