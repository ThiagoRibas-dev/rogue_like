# Skill: Escape Artist
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Escape Artist skill is used to slip out of ropes, manacles, or other bindings, squeeze through extremely tight spaces, or escape from grapple holds.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Escape Artist checks)

---

## Mechanics

### DC and Time Requirements

| Restraint Type | DC | Standard Action Time | Quick Escape Time¹ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Ropes / Bindings** | Opposed Use Rope + 10 | 1 minute | 5 rounds |
| **Manacles (Standard)** | 30 | 1 minute | 5 rounds |
| **Manacles (Tight)** | 35 | 1 minute | 5 rounds |
| **Net / Entangling Spell**² | 20 | Full-Round Action | Standard Action |
| **Grapple or Pin** | Opposed Grapple Check | Standard Action | Move Action |
| **Narrow Space** (Tight Squeeze) | 30 | 1 minute per 5 feet | 30 seconds (5 rounds) per 5 feet |

¹ *Quick Escape increases the DC of the check by **+10** (or imposes a **-10 penalty**).*
² *Spell description details take precedence (e.g., animate rope, entangle).*

### Squeezing through Narrow Spaces
*   A narrow space is defined as any gap that is less than half as wide as the space you normally occupy (e.g., less than 2-1/2 feet wide for a Medium creature).
*   If you fail, you cannot move through the space. If you fail by 5 or more, you get stuck and must make a new check to free yourself.

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You escape the restraint or successfully squeeze through the narrow space.
*   **Failure:** You remain restrained or stuck.
*   **Retry:** You can usually attempt to escape again by taking the required amount of time, except when stuck in a narrow space where getting unstuck requires a new check.
