# Skill: Sense Motive
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Sense Motive skill represents sensitivity to body language, speech habits, social subtext, and gut instinct. It is used to detect lies, read social situations, intercept secret messages, and assess the threat level of an opponent.

*   **Key Ability:** Wisdom (Wis)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### 1. Assess Opponent
You can size up how dangerous a visible target within 30 feet is.
*   **Action:** **Standard Action**.
*   **Check:** Opposed by the target's **Bluff** check.
*   **Bonus:** +2 circumstance bonus if you have seen the target in combat before.

| Outcome | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **You succeed** | You know the target's approximate threat category (actual ± one tier; 50% chance of either). |
| **You win by 10+** | You know the exact threat category. |
| **Target wins** | No useful information. |
| **Target wins by 5–9** | You believe the target is one category stronger or weaker than they truly are (50% chance). |
| **Target wins by 10+** | Your assessment is off by two threat categories (50% chance either direction). |

**Threat Categories:**

| Category | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A pushover** | Target's CR is 4+ lower than your HD/level. |
| **Easy** | Target's CR is 1–3 lower than your HD/level. |
| **A fair fight** | Target's CR equals your HD/level. |
| **A tough challenge** | Target's CR is up to 3 higher than your HD/level. |
| **A dire threat** | Target's CR is 4+ higher than your HD/level. |

> [!NOTE]
> A target particularly vulnerable to your attack style is considered one category less challenging; one particularly resistant to your attacks is considered one category more challenging.

---

### 2. Discern Secret Message
You can detect hidden messages being transmitted via the Bluff skill.
*   **Action:** No action (reactive, happens when you observe a Bluff interaction).
*   **Check:** Opposed by the transmitter's **Bluff** check.
*   **Penalty:** -2 for each piece of context about the message you are missing.

| Outcome | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Beat DC by 5+** | You intercept and fully understand the secret message. |
| **Beat DC by 1–4** | You know *something* hidden is being communicated, but not the content. |
| **Fail by 4 or less** | You detect nothing. |
| **Fail by 5+** | You infer false information about the conversation. |

---

### 3. Hunch
You get a gut feeling about a social situation.
*   **Action:** **1 minute**.
*   **DC:** **20**.
*   **Success:** You sense that something is wrong (e.g., an impostor) or that someone is trustworthy.
*   **Failure:** No information. You cannot retry.

---

### 4. Sense Enchantment
You observe a creature's behavior for signs of magical domination or compulsion.
*   **Action:** **1 minute** of observation.
*   **DC:** **25** normally; **DC 15** if the target is *dominated* (very limited behavior).
*   **Success:** You identify that an enchantment effect is influencing the target.
*   **Failure:** No information. You cannot retry.
