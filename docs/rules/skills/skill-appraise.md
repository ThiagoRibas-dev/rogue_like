# Skill: Appraise
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Appraise skill is used to evaluate the value, quality, and authenticity of objects, works of art, or materials.

*   **Key Ability:** Intelligence (Int)
*   **Trained Only:** No (anyone can attempt an Appraise check)
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Time
*   **Standard Appraisal:** Appraising an item takes **1 minute**.
*   **Quick Appraisal:** You can attempt to appraise an item in **1 round**, but this increases the check DC by **+5**.
*   **Complex Appraisals:** Evaluating large or complex works, such as an entire poem or a massive tapestry, requires taking whatever time is necessary to read or observe the work; this cannot be done quickly.

### DC by Item Classification

The difficulty of appraising an item depends on its rarity and quality:

| Item/Work Classification | DC | Example Items |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Common Items and Works** | 12 | Mundane gear, livestock, raw materials, trade goods, spices, foodstuffs. Created by craftsmen with a modifier $< +5$. |
| **Good/Rare/Exotic Items** | 15 | Fine clothing, gems, rare artwork, pure gold bars, spell components, jewelry. Created by craftsmen with a modifier of $+5$ to $+19$. |
| **Unique Items and Works** | 20 | Masterpiece artwork, extremely rare jewels, lavish/complex religious items. Created by craftsmen with a modifier of $+20$ or higher. |

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You correctly estimate the item's market value.
*   **Failure (Normal Check on Common Items):** You misjudge the value by $2d6+3 \times 10\%$ of its actual value (rolled by the DM secretly).
*   **Failure (Valuable Items or Quick Check):** You fail to estimate the item's value and learn nothing.
*   **Retry:** You cannot attempt a normal Appraise check on the same object again. If you fail a quick appraisal, you can attempt a normal 1-minute appraisal, but you cannot try to appraise it quickly again.

---

## Special Interactions
*   **Elven Items:** Appraising elven craftsmanship or items might grant a $+2$ synergy bonus if the character has 5 or more ranks in a related Craft skill.
*   **Magical Items:** Appraising magical items only reveals their physical construction and value, not their magical properties. For magical properties, see [magic-items-general.md](./magic-items-general.md).
