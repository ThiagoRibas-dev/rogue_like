# Skill: Jump
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Jump skill is used to clear pits, cross chasms, leap over obstacles, jump up onto ledges, or safely jump down from heights.

*   **Key Ability:** Strength (Str)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Jump checks)

---

## Mechanics

### Action and Movement
*   **Action:** Jumping is performed as part of a move action.
*   **Limit:** The distance of your jump cannot exceed your remaining movement for the round.

### Base Speed Modifiers
Jump distances assume a base land speed of 30 feet. If your speed differs, modify your check:
*   **Speed $> 30$ feet:** You gain a **+4 bonus** on the check for every 10 feet of speed above 30.
*   **Speed $< 30$ feet:** You take a **-6 penalty** on the check for every 10 feet of speed below 30.

### 1. Long Jump (Horizontal)
Leaping horizontally across a gap or pit:
*   **Running Jump:** Requires moving at least 20 feet in a straight line prior to the leap.
    *   **DC = Distance to clear (in feet)**. (e.g., clearing a 15-foot pit is DC 15).
*   **Standing Jump:** If you do not have a 20-foot running start, the **DC is doubled** (e.g., clearing a 15-foot pit is DC 30).
*   *Edge Grab:* If you fail a long jump but land within 1 or 2 feet of the edge, you can attempt a **DC 15 Reflex save** to grab the far edge.

### 2. High Jump (Vertical)
Leaping vertically to reach a ledge or grab a rope:
*   **Running Jump:** Requires moving at least 20 feet in a straight line prior to the leap.
    *   **DC = 4 $\times$ Height to clear (in feet)**. (e.g., jumping 4 feet high is DC 16).
*   **Standing Jump:** If you do not have a 20-foot running start, the **DC is doubled** (e.g., jumping 4 feet high is DC 32).
*   **Vertical Reach:** You can reach higher than your jump height.
    *   *Standard Reach:* A Medium humanoid can reach $8$ feet high from a standing position.
    *   *Jump Reach:* Your total vertical reach during a jump is equal to your height $+$ your jump height $+ 1.5 \times \text{your height}$ (typically about $8$ feet for a Medium creature plus the jump height).

### 3. Hop Up
You can hop up onto an elevated surface (such as a table or boulder) up to waist-high as part of your movement:
*   **DC:** **10**.
*   *Standing Start:* If you have no running start, the DC is **20**.

### 4. Jump Down
You can deliberately leap down from a height to reduce impact damage (see [natural-hazards.md](./natural-hazards.md)):
*   **DC:** **15**.
*   **Success:** Treat the fall as being **10 feet shorter** than it actually is for the purpose of calculating falling damage.

---

## Synergy Bonuses
*   **Tumble:** If you have 5 or more ranks in Tumble, you receive a **+2 synergy bonus** on Jump checks (see [skill-tumble.md](./skill-tumble.md)).
