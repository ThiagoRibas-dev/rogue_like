# Skill: Sleight of Hand
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Sleight of Hand skill represents your ability to conceal objects on your person, palm small items, pickpocket, or perform feats of legerdemain that require manual dexterity and misdirection.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
    *   *Exception:* Untrained characters may attempt a Dexterity check to hide an object on their person or for any Sleight of Hand task with a DC of 10 or lower.
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Sleight of Hand checks)

---

## Mechanics

### Action
*   **Standard Action** by default. Can be attempted as a **Move Action** at a -20 penalty.

### Observation and Opposition
*   **Under Observation:** When performing a Sleight of Hand check while being watched, your check is opposed by the observer's **Spot** check. A successful Spot check does *not* prevent the act — it merely means the observer notices what you're doing.
*   **Frisk Search:** If you have hidden an object on your person and someone frisks you, your Sleight of Hand check is opposed by the searcher's **Search** check, at a **+4 bonus** for the searcher (it is easier to find something than to hide it).

### Pickpocketing
Attempting to take something from another creature requires:
*   **DC:** **20**.
*   **Opposition:** The target makes a **Spot** check opposed by your same Sleight of Hand check result.
*   **On Target Success:** The target notices the attempt regardless of whether you got the item.
*   **Limits:** The DM determines what is appropriate to pickpocket. Coin purses, loose keys, and tiny weapons are fair game. Securely worn items (bracers, rings, armor) are not.
*   **Aware Target:** If the target knows you are trying to steal from them, use **Disarm** (see [disarm.md](./disarm.md)) or **Grapple** (see [grapple.md](./grapple.md)) rules instead.

## Advanced Techniques

### Dagger Surprise
You can produce a concealed dagger at a decisive moment to catch an opponent flat-footed:
*   **Requirements:** A sheathed dagger on your person, the **Quick Draw** feat, and an empty off-hand.
*   **Setup:** You must fight the same opponent for at least **2 consecutive rounds** so they grow accustomed to your empty off-hand.
*   **Execution:** On the **3rd round**, use Quick Draw to draw the dagger as a free action. Make a Sleight of Hand check opposed by the opponent's **Spot** check.
*   **Success:** The opponent is treated as **flat-footed** for your next attack with that dagger.
*   **Once per Encounter:** The same opponent cannot be caught with this trick twice in the same encounter.

### Unobtrusive Spellcasting
As part of casting a spell, you can try to hide your verbal and somatic components:
*   **Check:** Your Sleight of Hand check is opposed by each observer's **Spot** check.
*   **Success:** Observers do not notice you casting the spell.
*   **Failure:** Observers notice, but the spell is not prevented.

### Performance (Legerdemain)
You can use Sleight of Hand in place of Perform to entertain an audience through juggling, card tricks, or feats of legerdemain. Use your Sleight of Hand check result in place of a Perform check for purposes of earning income.
