# Skill: Search
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Search skill represents active, methodical investigation of an area, object, or surface to find hidden doors, concealed traps, secret compartments, or traces of passage.

*   **Key Ability:** Intelligence (Int)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Action and Area
*   **Action:** **Full-Round Action** per 5-foot square examined.
*   **Range:** You can only Search a 5-foot square you are adjacent to or within (i.e., you must be close to the area being searched).
*   **Passive Search:** Creatures cannot passively detect traps or secret doors; a Search check must be actively declared.

### DCs by Task

| Task | DC | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Notice a secret door or simple trap** | 20 | Standard secret doors, pressure plates, tripwires. |
| **Find a magic trap** | 25 + spell level | Requires the *Trapfinding* class feature. |
| **Find a footprint** | Varies | Locates the sign of passage; does not allow tracking (see [skill-survival.md](./skill-survival.md) for tracking). |
| **Find a hidden object** | 10–25 | DM assigns DC based on how well it is concealed. |
| **Detect an obscured forgery or disguised item** | Opposed Forgery / Disguise | See [skill-forgery.md](./skill-forgery.md) and [skill-disguise.md](./skill-disguise.md). |

### Trapfinding Requirement
Only creatures with the *Trapfinding* class feature (Rogues and certain other classes) can:
*   Make a Search check to locate **magic traps** (DC 25 + spell level).
*   Use Disable Device to **disarm** a magic trap (see [skill-disable-device.md](./skill-disable-device.md)).

Without Trapfinding, a character can only find and disarm mechanical traps.

---

## Taking 10 and Taking 20
*   **Taking 10:** Allowed when not threatened or distracted. A routine, methodical search.
*   **Taking 20:** Allowed but takes 20× the time (20 full-round actions per 5-foot square). Usually impractical in a dungeon under pressure, but effective for clearing a room during downtime.

---

## Synergy Bonus
*   **5+ ranks in Search:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Survival checks to follow tracks (see [skill-survival.md](./skill-survival.md)).
