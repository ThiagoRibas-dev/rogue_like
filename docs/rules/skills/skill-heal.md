# Skill: Heal
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Heal skill is used to stabilize dying companions, treat diseases and poisons, speed up natural recovery, and treat environmental wounds.

*   **Key Ability:** Wisdom (Wis)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### First Aid (Stabilization)
*   **Action:** **Standard Action** (provokes attacks of opportunity).
*   **DC:** **15**.
*   **Success:** Stabilizes a dying character (see [injury-and-death.md](./injury-and-death.md)), stopping their round-by-round hit point loss. It also stops bleeding.
*   **Failure:** The target continues to lose hit points normally.

### Long-Term Care
*   **Action:** Requires **8 hours** of light effort per day.
*   **DC:** **15**.
*   **Success:** The patients double their natural healing rates for hit points and ability damage (see [healing.md](./healing.md)):
    *   *Natural Healing:* Patient heals $2$ HP per character level/HD per day of rest ($4$ HP per level/HD for full bed rest).
    *   *Ability Damage:* Patient heals $2$ points of ability damage per affected score per day of rest ($4$ points for full bed rest).
*   *Capacity:* A healer can care for up to 6 patients at once. The healer cannot perform long-term care on themselves.

### Treat Caltrop, Spike, or *Spike Stones* Wounds
*   **Action:** **10 minutes** of treatment.
*   **DC:** **15**.
*   **Success:** Removes the speed reduction penalty imposed by stepping on caltrops or magical spikes, allowing the patient to move at normal speed again.

### Treat Poison
*   **Action:** **Standard Action** (taken immediately after the patient is poisoned).
*   **DC:** **Poison's Fortitude Save DC**.
*   **Success:** The patient receives a **+4 competence bonus** on their next saving throw against the poison's secondary damage.

### Treat Disease
*   **Action:** **10 minutes** of treatment.
*   **DC:** **Disease's Fortitude Save DC**.
*   **Success:** The patient receives a **+4 competence bonus** on their next saving throw against the disease.
