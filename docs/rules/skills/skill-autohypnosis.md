# Skill: Autohypnosis
## Category
Psionic Skills

## Overview
You have trained your mind to gain mastery over your body and the mind's own deepest capabilities.

*   **Key Ability:** Wisdom (Wis)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

## Task Details

### Ignore Caltrop Wound
If you are wounded by stepping on a caltrop, your speed is normally reduced to one-half normal. A successful Autohypnosis check removes this movement penalty. The wound doesn't go away—it is just ignored through self-persuasion.

### Memorize
You can attempt to memorize a long string of numbers, a long passage of verse, or some other particularly difficult piece of information (but not magical or exotic scripts). 
*   **Capacity:** Each successful check allows you to memorize a single page of text (up to 800 words), numbers, diagrams, or sigils. 
*   **Recall:** You always retain this information, but you must make another successful Autohypnosis check to recall it later.

### Resist Dying
If you have negative hit points and are losing hit points (1 per round or 1 per hour), you can substitute a DC 20 Autohypnosis check for your d% stabilization roll. 
*   **Success:** You stop losing hit points.
*   **Failure:** You can substitute this check for the d% roll in later rounds if you fail.

### Resist Fear
In response to any fear effect, you make a saving throw normally. If you fail the saving throw, you can make an Autohypnosis check on your **next round** even while overcome by fear. 
*   **Success:** If your check meets or beats the DC for the fear effect, you shrug off the fear. 
*   **Failure:** The fear affects you normally, and you gain no further attempts against that specific effect.

### Tolerate Poison
You can choose to substitute an Autohypnosis check for a saving throw against any standard poison's **secondary** damage or effect. 
*   *Note:* This skill has no effect on the initial saving throw against poison.

### Willpower
If reduced to 0 hit points (disabled), you can make an Autohypnosis check to take a strenuous action without suffering the normal consequences. 
*   **Action:** You must make a check for *each* strenuous action you want to take. 
*   **Success:** You take the normal action without taking 1 point of damage. 
*   **Failure:** There is no direct penalty—you can simply choose not to take the strenuous action. If you take it anyway, you drop to -1 hit points and begin dying, as normal.

## Synergy and Feats

*   **Autonomous Feat:** Grants a **+2 bonus** on Autohypnosis checks.
*   **5+ ranks in Autohypnosis:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Knowledge (psionics) checks.
*   **5+ ranks in Concentration:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Autohypnosis checks.
