# Creature Size

Creature size affects many aspects of combat and movement, including Armor Class, attack rolls, grapple checks, hide checks, carrying capacity, space, and reach.

## Little Creatures (Tiny, Diminutive, and Fine)

Creatures smaller than Small occupy less than one 5-foot square on the battlefield.

*   **Multiple Creatures per Square:** Up to 4 Tiny creatures, 25 Diminutive creatures, or 100 Fine creatures can occupy a single 5-foot square.
*   **Melee Reach of 0 Feet:** These creatures cannot reach into adjacent squares. They must enter an opponent's square to make a melee attack.
    *   *Attacks of Opportunity:* Entering an opponent's square to attack provokes an attack of opportunity from the defender.
    *   *Attacking Small Creatures:* Sighted opponents can attack into their own square normally without penalty to strike these small intruders.
*   **Threatening Squares:** Because they have 0 reach, little creatures do **not** threaten adjacent squares. Creatures can move past them without provoking attacks of opportunity. However, they *do* threaten the square they currently occupy.
*   **Flanking:** Little creatures cannot flank opponents.

---

## Big Creatures (Large, Huge, Gargantuan, and Colossal)

Creatures larger than Medium occupy multiple 5-foot squares and have natural reach.

*   **Natural Reach:** Big creatures threaten all squares within their natural reach.
*   **Approaching Big Creatures:** When a Medium or smaller creature approaches a creature with greater than 5-foot reach, it must enter and leave threatened squares to get into melee range, provoking attacks of opportunity unless using Tumble or another ability.
*   **Reach Weapons:** If a Large or larger creature wields a reach weapon, it doubles its natural reach. However, it cannot attack targets within its natural reach or closer.
    *   *Example:* A Large (Tall) creature has a natural reach of 10 feet. Wielding a reach weapon allows it to attack targets at 15 to 20 feet, but it cannot attack targets at 10 feet or 5 feet.
