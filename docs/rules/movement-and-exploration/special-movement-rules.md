# Special Movement Rules

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Movement

## Summary

This rule covers specific situations that affect movement, such as accidentally ending movement in an illegal space, the cumulative effects of double movement costs, and the minimum movement allowed despite penalties.

## Mechanics

### Accidentally Ending Movement in an Illegal Space

-   If a character ends movement in a space where they are not allowed to stop, place their miniature in the last legal position occupied, or the closest legal position if one is nearer.

### Double Movement Cost

-   When movement is hampered, its cost usually doubles (e.g., 2 squares for difficult terrain, 3 for diagonal difficult terrain).
-   If movement cost is doubled twice, each square counts as 4 squares (or 6 for diagonal).
-   If movement cost is doubled three times, each square counts as 8 squares (or 12 for diagonal), and so on. This is an exception to the general rule that two doublings are equivalent to a tripling.

### Minimum Movement

-   Despite movement penalties, you can take a full-round action to move 5 feet (1 square) in any direction, including diagonally.
-   This rule does not allow movement through impassable terrain or when all movement is prohibited.
-   Such movement provokes attacks of opportunity as normal (it is not a 5-foot step).
