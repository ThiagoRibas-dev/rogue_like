# Speed
## Category
Combat Statistics

## Summary
Speed represents how far a creature can move in a round while performing other actions. Your speed depends primarily on your race, the armor you are wearing, and the load you carry.

## Mechanics

### 1. Base Land Speed
Your base land speed is your unarmored, unencumbered speed.
*   **30 feet (6 squares):** Humans, Elves, Half-Elves, Half-Orcs.
*   **20 feet (4 squares):** Dwarves, Gnomes, Halflings.

### 2. Armor and Encumbrance Adjustments
Wearing medium or heavy armor, or carrying a medium or heavy load, slows you down.
*   **Dwarf Exception:** Dwarves are naturally sturdy; their speed remains 20 feet even when wearing medium or heavy armor or carrying a medium or heavy load.

#### Table: Speed Adjustments
| Base Speed | Adjusted Speed (Medium/Heavy Armor or Medium/Heavy Load) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **20 ft. (4 squares)** | 15 ft. (3 squares) |
| **30 ft. (6 squares)** | 20 ft. (4 squares) |
| **40 ft. (8 squares)** | 30 ft. (6 squares) |
| **50 ft. (10 squares)** | 35 ft. (7 squares) |
| **60 ft. (12 squares)** | 40 ft. (8 squares) |

*For heavier base speeds, the formula generally reduces speed by approximately 1/3 (rounded to the nearest 5-foot increment).*

### 3. Encumbrance Limits
*   **Medium Load:** Reduces speed as shown in the table above.
*   **Heavy Load:** Reduces speed as shown in the table above, and limits your maximum Dexterity bonus to AC to +1.
*   See [ability-scores.md](./ability-scores.md) for carrying capacity weight thresholds.

### 4. Speed Modifiers and Stacking
*   **Applying Modifiers:** Always apply any bonuses or penalties to speed *before* adjusting for armor or encumbrance.
*   **Stacking:** Speed bonuses of different types (e.g., a Barbarian's Fast Movement untyped bonus, a Monk's enhancement bonus, and an *expeditious retreat* spell's enhancement bonus) stack. Bonuses of the same type (such as two enhancement bonuses) do not stack; only the highest applies.

---

## Combat Action Speed Multipliers
*   **Single Move (Hustle):** Move up to your speed as a move action.
*   **Double Move:** Move up to twice your speed in a round by using both your move and standard actions. See [tactical-movement.md](./tactical-movement.md).
*   **Running:** Move up to **quadruple (x4) your speed** as a full-round action.
    *   *Armor Penalty:* If you are running in heavy armor, you can only move up to **triple (x3) your speed**.
    *   *Condition:* You lose your Dexterity bonus to AC while running.
