# Weather

Weather plays a major role in outdoor adventuring. Bad weather slows travel, hinders navigation, reduces visibility, and deals environmental damage.

---

## Predicting and Surviving Weather

### Survival Checks
*   **Predicting Weather:** A creature can predict the weather for the next 24 hours with a successful DC 15 Survival check. For every 5 points by which the check result exceeds 15, they can predict the weather 24 hours further into the future.
*   **Surviving Severe Weather:** A successful DC 15 Survival check grants a +2 bonus on all Fortitude saves against severe weather while moving up to half overland speed, or a +4 bonus if remaining stationary. The user grants this same bonus to one additional creature for every point by which the Survival check exceeds 15.

## Temperature Dangers

Prolonged exposure to extreme heat or cold deals damage and imposes status conditions.

### Temperature Bands
*   **Moderate and Warm (41° F to 90° F):** Unharmful to most creatures.
*   **Cold (1° F to 40° F) / Hot (91° F to 110° F):** Requires hourly saves.
*   **Severe Cold (-19° F to 0° F) / Severe Heat (111° F to 140° F):** Requires saves every 10 minutes.
*   **Extreme Cold (-50° F to -20° F) / Extreme Heat (141° F to 180° F):** Deals automatic damage plus saves every 10 minutes.
*   **Unearthly Cold (-51° F or lower) / Unearthly Heat (181° F to 210° F):** Deals automatic damage per minute or round.
*   **Burning Heat (211° F or higher):** Deals massive automatic damage; ordinary heat protection is useless.

### Temperature Penalties & Statuses
*   **Fatigue & Exhaustion:** Taking any damage from temperature exposure makes a creature **fatigued**. If they take more temperature damage while fatigued (or gain fatigue from any other source), they become **exhausted**.
*   **Lethal Damage Conversion:** An unconscious creature takes all temperature damage as lethal damage.
*   **Modifiers:**
    *   *Heavy Clothing/Armor:* Imposes a -4 penalty on saving throws against heat-related damage.
    *   *Wet:* Imposes a -10 penalty on saving throws against cold-related damage.

## Protection Adaptations

A creature's level of protection is determined by combining its base protection and any modifiers.

### Protection against Cold
*   **Base Level 0:** No cold adaptations.
*   **Base Level 1:** Non-arctic animal with fur; monsters native to cold terrain.
*   **Base Level 2:** Arctic animal with fur; monsters native to the arctic.
*   **Base Level 3:** *Endure elements* spell; Resistance to cold 5 or more.
*   **Modifiers:**
    *   `+1` Cold weather outfit.
    *   `+3` Improvised shelter (snow cave, den).

### Protection against Heat
*   **Base Level 0:** No heat adaptations; non-desert cold-blooded animal or vermin.
*   **Base Level 1:** Monsters native to hot climates; desert animal or vermin.
*   **Base Level 2:** Monsters native to deserts.
*   **Base Level 3:** *Endure elements* spell; Resistance to fire 5 or more.
*   **Modifiers:**
    *   `+1` Desert outfit.
    *   `+3` Improvised shelter (tent, sand cave, windbreak).

### Recovery
Temperature-related nonlethal damage cannot be healed naturally or magically until the creature has cooled down (heat) or warmed up (cold). A *heal* spell bypasses this temperature requirement.

## Wind Effects

Wind extinguishes flames, makes ranged attacks difficult, and can knock down or blow away creatures based on their size.

| Wind Force | Wind Speed | Ranged Attacks | Siege Weapons | Listen Penalty | Flame Effects | Size of Creature Checked | Size of Creature Knocked Down | Size of Creature Blown Away |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Light** | 0-10 mph | Normal | Normal | Normal | Normal | — | — | — |
| **Moderate** | 11-19 mph | Normal | Normal | Normal | 50% candle snuff | — | — | — |
| **Strong** | 20-30 mph | -2 | Normal | -2 | Snuffs candles/torches | Tiny | — | — |
| **Severe** | 31-50 mph | -4 | Normal | -4 | 50% lantern snuff | Small | Tiny | — |
| **Windstorm** | 51-74 mph | Impossible | -4 | -8 | 75% lantern snuff | Medium | Small | Tiny |
| **Hurricane** | 75-174 mph | Impossible | -8 | Auto-fail | Snuffs all flames | Large | Medium | Small |
| **Tornado** | 175+ mph | Impossible | Impossible | Auto-fail | Snuffs all flames | Huge | Large | Medium or smaller |

*Note: Flying or airborne creatures are treated as one size category smaller than their actual size for wind effects.*

### Wind Condition Statuses
*   **Checked:** Creatures cannot move forward against the wind. Flying creatures are blown back $1d6 \times 5$ feet.
*   **Knocked Down:** Land creatures are knocked prone. Flying creatures are blown back $1d6 \times 10$ feet.
*   **Blown Away:** Land creatures are knocked prone and rolled $1d4 \times 10$ feet per round (taking 1d4 nonlethal damage per 10 feet). Flying creatures are blown back $2d6 \times 10$ feet per round (taking 2d6 nonlethal damage from battering).
*   **Tornado Vortex:** Creatures coming into contact with a tornado funnel are picked up and whirled for 1d10 rounds, taking 6d6 points of damage per round, and then violently expelled (falling damage applies).

## Precipitation

Most precipitation lasts for 2d4 hours. Sleet and snow occur only when the temperature is 30° F or lower.
*   **Fog:** Thin fog obscures all sight beyond 60 feet (darkvision included). Creatures separated by >30 feet have concealment; at 60 feet they have total concealment. Thick fog obscures all sight beyond 5 feet (creatures 5 feet away have concealment; others have total concealment).
*   **Hail:** Sound imposes a -4 penalty on Listen checks. 5% chance of large hail dealing 1 point of bludgeoning damage per storm to unprotected creatures. Lasts 1d20 minutes.
*   **Rain:** Reduces visibility ranges by half (-4 on Spot/Search). Has same effect on flames, ranged attacks, and Listen checks as severe wind.
    *   *Downpour:* Grants concealment to creatures 5 or more feet apart. Can cause floods.
*   **Sleet:** Frozen rain. Reduces visibility by half (-4 Spot/Search). Same wind effects as severe wind, but has a 75% chance to extinguish protected flames.
*   **Snow:** Reduces visibility by half (-4 Spot/Search). Wind effects of severe wind. Leaves 1d6 inches of snow.
    *   *Heavy Snow:* Grants concealment to creatures 5 or more feet apart. Has the flame extinguishing effect of moderate wind. Leaves 1d4 feet of snow. Strong winds can create drifts $1d4 \times 5$ feet deep. 10% chance of lightning.

## Storms

Storms last for $2d4-1$ hours. Wind speeds are severe (30-50 mph). Combined effects of wind and precipitation reduce visibility ranges by three-quarters (-8 on Spot, Search, and Listen). Ranged attacks are impossible (siege weapons take -4). Unprotected flames are extinguished; protected flames have a 50% chance to be doused.

*   **Duststorm:** Smothers unprotected flames and chokes protected flames. Leaves 1d6 inches of dust/sand.
*   **Sandstorm:** Reduces visibility to $1d10 \times 5$ feet (-4 penalty on Listen, Search, Spot). Deals 1d3 points of nonlethal damage per hour to creatures in the open.
*   **Snowstorm:** Same as storm, plus leaves 1d6 inches of snow.
*   **Thunderstorm:** Features lightning. One bolt strikes per minute for 1 hour. Any creature in the open (or under conductive cover) has a 1-in-20 chance (d20 roll of 20) of being struck each minute, taking 5d8 electricity damage (Reflex DC 15 half). 10% chance of a tornado.

### Powerful Storms
Wind speeds exceed 50 mph. Visibility is reduced to zero (Spot, Search, Listen, and ranged attacks are impossible). Unprotected flames are extinguished; protected flames have a 75% chance of being doused.
*   **Windstorm:** Lasts 1d6 hours. Little or no precipitation, but causes storm-force wind damage.
*   **Greater Duststorm:** Deals 1d3 points of nonlethal damage per round to unprotected creatures in the open. Poses a suffocation hazard, though a mouth scarf delays checks until a number of rounds equal to $10 \times$ Constitution score. Leaves $2d3-1$ feet of dust.
*   **Blizzard:** Combination of high winds and heavy snow. Lasts 1d3 days.
*   **Hurricane:** Heavy floods, torrential downpours, and hurricane-force winds. Lasts up to a week (major impact in a 24-to-48-hour period).
