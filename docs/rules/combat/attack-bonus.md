# Attack Bonus
## Category
Combat Statistics
## Summary
Your attack bonus determines your proficiency in striking opponents with weapons.
## Mechanics
Your attack bonus is calculated differently for melee and ranged weapons:
- **Melee Weapon:** Base attack bonus + Strength modifier + size modifier
- **Ranged Weapon:** Base attack bonus + Dexterity modifier + size modifier + range penalty

## Special Notes
The size modifier is a crucial factor in determining your attack bonus, reflecting the ease or difficulty of hitting targets based on your size.
