# How Combat Works
## Category
Combat Basics
## Summary
Combat is cyclical, with all combatants acting in turn in a regular cycle of rounds, following a specific sequence from surprise to regular turns.
## Mechanics
The combat sequence is as follows:
1. Each combatant starts flat-footed. Once a combatant acts, they are no longer flat-footed.
2. Determine awareness: If some but not all combatants are aware, a surprise round occurs. Aware combatants roll initiative and take one action (standard or move) in initiative order. Unaware combatants do not act. If no one or everyone is aware, there is no surprise round.
3. Combatants who have not yet rolled initiative do so. All combatants are now ready for their first regular round.
4. Combatants act in initiative order (highest to lowest).
5. After everyone has had a turn, the combatant with the highest initiative acts again, and steps 4 and 5 repeat until combat ends.
## Special Notes
This section outlines the fundamental flow of combat, including the concept of flat-footed, surprise rounds, and initiative order.
