# Two-Weapon Fighting
## Category
Combat Basics

## Summary
If you wield a second weapon in your off hand, you can gain one extra attack with that weapon when you make a full attack. However, doing so is extremely difficult and imposes penalties on all attacks made during the turn.

## Mechanics

### 1. Two-Weapon Fighting Penalties
Normally, fighting with two weapons is difficult. You take a **-6 penalty** on attacks with your primary hand and a **-10 penalty** on attacks with your off hand.
You can reduce these penalties in two ways (which stack):
1.  **Light Off-Hand Weapon:** If your off-hand weapon is light, the penalties are reduced by 2 each (to -4 primary / -8 off-hand).
2.  **Two-Weapon Fighting Feat:** The Two-Weapon Fighting feat lessens the primary hand penalty by 2 and the off-hand penalty by 6.

#### Two-Weapon Fighting Penalties Table
| Circumstances | Primary Hand Penalty | Off Hand Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Normal Penalties** | -6 | -10 |
| **Off-hand weapon is light** | -4 | -8 |
| **Two-Weapon Fighting feat** | -4 | -4 |
| **Light off-hand & Two-Weapon Fighting feat** | -2 | -2 |

*Note: An unarmed strike is always considered a light weapon for two-weapon fighting.*

### 2. Full Attack Requirement
You only gain the extra attack from two-weapon fighting when making a **Full Attack** action. If you make a single attack (such as a standard action attack, charge attack, or attack of opportunity), you cannot make an off-hand attack, but you do not suffer Two-Weapon Fighting penalties. See [full-attack.md](./full-attack.md).

### 3. Double Weapons
You can use a double weapon to make an extra attack with the off-hand end of the weapon as if you were fighting with two weapons. The penalties apply as if the off-hand end of the weapon were a light weapon, and you must hold the double weapon in two hands to do so. See [equipment.md](./equipment.md).

### 4. Ranged & Thrown Weapons
The same rules and penalties apply when you throw or fire a weapon from each hand, provided neither weapon requires two hands to fire (e.g., throwing two daggers or firing two hand crossbows).
*   **Weight Classification for Thrown/Ranged:**
    *   **Light:** Medium weapons that weigh 1 pound or less (e.g., darts, shurikens) are treated as light off-hand weapons.
    *   **One-Handed:** Medium weapons that weigh more than 1 pound (e.g., throwing axes, javelins, short bows, light crossbows, hand crossbows) are treated as one-handed weapons.
    *   **Size Scaling:** The same weight designations apply proportionately to weapons sized for larger or smaller creatures.

### 5. Natural Weapons Interaction
Natural attacks (claws, bites, slams, etc.) are **not** subject to Two-Weapon Fighting rules or penalties.
*   If a creature uses natural attacks exclusively, it can make all of its natural attacks during a full attack without TWF penalties (primary attacks at full bonus, secondary attacks at a -5 penalty).
*   If a creature combines manufactured weapons and natural weapons, it resolves its manufactured weapon attacks normally (including TWF penalties if using two manufactured weapons). All of its natural attacks are treated as secondary natural attacks, which do not benefit from TWF feat reductions: they take a **-5 penalty** on attack rolls (or -2 with Multiattack) and add only **1/2 Strength modifier** to damage. See [special-attacks.md](./special-attacks.md).
