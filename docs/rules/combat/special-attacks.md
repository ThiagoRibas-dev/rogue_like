# Special Attacks
## Category
Combat Actions

## Summary
Special attacks encompass combat maneuvers available to players (such as disarming or tripping) and extraordinary or supernatural attacks utilized by monsters (such as swallowing whole or breathing poison).

---

## 1. Tactical Combat Maneuvers (Player & Creature Options)
These standard combat maneuvers can be attempted by any character. They are detailed in their respective rules files:

*   **[Aid Another](./aid-another.md):** Grant a +2 bonus (or higher) to an ally's attack or AC.
*   **[Bull Rush](./bull-rush.md):** Push an opponent backward 5 feet or more.
*   **[Charge](./charge.md):** Move up to double speed and make a single attack with a +2 bonus, taking a -2 AC penalty.
*   **[Disarm](./disarm.md):** Disarm an opponent of their weapon or shield.
*   **[Feint](./feint.md):** Use a Bluff check in combat to deny your opponent's Dexterity bonus to AC.
*   **[Grapple](./grapple.md):** Wrestle with one or more opponents, holding them stationary.
*   **[Overrun](./overrun.md):** Plow past or over an opponent as part of your movement.
*   **[Sunder](./sunder.md):** Strike and damage an opponent's weapon, shield, or worn object.
*   **[Throw Splash Weapon](./throw-splash-weapon.md):** Throw a container of volatile liquid (e.g., alchemist's fire) at a target or grid intersection.
*   **[Trip](./trip.md):** Knock an opponent prone.
*   **[Turn or Rebuke Undead](./turn-or-rebuke-undead.md):** Channel positive or negative energy to drive off, destroy, awe, or command undead and deathless.
*   **[Two-Weapon Fighting](./two-weapon-fighting.md):** Wield a weapon in each hand to make an extra attack during a full attack.

## 2. Monster & Creature Special Attacks (Rules Compendium)
These special attacks are typically extraordinary (Ex) or supernatural (Su) abilities possessed by specific monsters or characters with racial templates.

### Constrict (Ex)
A creature can crush a grappled opponent, dealing bludgeoning damage.
*   **Trigger:** Successful grapple check made to deal damage.
*   **Damage:** Deals the damage listed in the creature's description. If the creature also has *Improved Grab*, it deals this constrict damage in addition to the natural weapon damage used to grab. See [grapple.md](./grapple.md).

### Improved Grab (Ex)
A creature can immediately grab and hold a target upon hitting with a natural weapon.
*   **Trigger:** A successful hit with a designated natural weapon (usually bite or claw).
*   **Mechanic:** Attempts to start a grapple as a free action without provoking an AoO. No initial melee touch attack is required. It normally works only against targets at least one size category smaller than the creature.
*   **Body Part Option:** The creature can choose to grapple normally (pulling the target into its space) or grapple using only the body part that grabbed (taking a **-20 penalty** on grapple checks but not counting as grappled itself—allowing it to keep its Dex bonus to AC, threaten its area, and attack other foes). See [grapple.md](./grapple.md).

### Pounce (Ex)
When a creature charges, it can make a **Full Attack** at the end of the charge instead of a single attack (even if restricted to a single action on its turn).
*   All attacks receive the $+2$ charge attack bonus.
*   If the creature successfully grabs a target during the pounce, and has the *Rake* ability, it can immediately make its rake attacks. See [charge.md](./charge.md).

### Rake (Ex)
A creature gains extra natural attacks when grappling.
*   **Mechanics:** Grants two additional claw attacks against a grappled opponent. These attacks do not take the standard -4 penalty for natural attacks in a grapple, and they use the creature's normal attack bonus.
*   **Requirements:** The creature must begin its turn grappling to rake. It cannot start a grapple and rake on the same turn unless it has *Pounce* or a similar ability. See [grapple.md](./grapple.md).

### Rend (Ex)
A creature tears the flesh of an opponent by hitting with multiple natural attacks.
*   **Trigger:** Hits the same target with the specified natural attacks (usually two claws) in the same round.
*   **Damage:** Automatically deals extra damage equal to the natural weapon's damage + 1.5 times the creature's Strength modifier.

### Swallow Whole (Ex)
A creature can swallow a grappled opponent.
*   **Trigger:** The creature begins its turn with an opponent held in its mouth (via Improved Grab). It makes a new opposed grapple check.
*   **Success:** The opponent is swallowed and deals normal bite damage. The target is considered grappled, but the swallowing creature is not.
*   **Being Swallowed:** A swallowed creature takes damage each round (usually bludgeoning and acid) inside the stomach.
*   **Cutting Free:** A swallowed creature can try to cut its way free using a light slashing or piercing weapon to deal a specified amount of damage to the stomach (AC $= 5 + 1/2\ \text{Natural Armor bonus}$). If they cut free, they escape. Alternatively, they can win an opposed grapple check to escape back into the creature's mouth. See [grapple.md](./grapple.md).

### Trample (Ex)
As a full-round action, a creature can move up to twice its speed and run over opponents at least one size category smaller than itself.
*   **Mechanic:** The creature simply moves through the targets' spaces.
*   **Target Reaction:** Opponents run over can either:
    1.  Take an Attack of Opportunity at a **-4 penalty**, OR
    2.  Attempt a Reflex save (DC $= 10 + 1/2\ \text{Creature's HD} + \text{Str modifier}$) for half damage.
*   **Damage:** Automatically deals bludgeoning damage (listed in description) to those who do not successfully save.

### Aligned Strike (Su)
Attacks are treated as aligned (chaotic, evil, good, or lawful) for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

### Epic Strike (Su)
Natural attacks are treated as having a $+6$ enhancement bonus for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

### Magic Strike (Su)
Natural attacks are treated as magic for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

### Poison (Ex) / Disease (Ex or Su) / Paralysis (Ex or Su)
Special venomous, infectious, or paralyzing attacks that require a Fortitude save to resist. See [saving-throws.md](./saving-throws.md).
