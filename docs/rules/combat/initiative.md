# Initiative
## Category
Combat Statistics
## Summary
Initiative determines the order in which combatants act during a battle.
## Mechanics
### Initiative Checks
At the start of a battle, each combatant makes an initiative check, which is a Dexterity check (1d20 + Dexterity modifier). Characters act in order, counting down from highest result to lowest. This order is maintained in subsequent rounds unless a character's initiative changes due to special actions.
### Ties
If two or more combatants have the same initiative check result, they act in order of total initiative modifier (highest first). If a tie still exists, the tied characters roll again to determine who goes first among them.
### Flat-Footed
At the start of a battle, before your first regular turn in the initiative order, you are flat-footed. While flat-footed, you cannot use your Dexterity bonus to AC (if any). Barbarians and rogues with the uncanny dodge extraordinary ability are exceptions. A flat-footed character also cannot make attacks of opportunity.
### Inaction
Even if you cannot take actions, you retain your initiative score for the duration of the encounter.
## Special Notes
Initiative is crucial for establishing the turn order and understanding the initial state of combat.
