# Dying (-1 to -9 Hit Points)

## Category
Injury and Death

## Summary
When your character’s current hit points drop to between –1 and –9 inclusive, he’s dying.

## Mechanics
- **Actions:** A dying character immediately falls unconscious and can take no actions.
- **Hit Point Loss:** A dying character loses 1 hit point every round. This continues until the character dies or becomes stable.
