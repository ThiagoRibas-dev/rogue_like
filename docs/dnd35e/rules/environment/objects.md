# Objects

When attempting to break an object, you can either attack it with a weapon (dealing damage to reduce its hit points) or break it with sheer strength (making a Strength check).

---

## Attacking Objects

To destroy an object by dealing damage, you must hit it and overcome its hardness. Attacking a carried or worn object is done via a **Sunder** attempt, which provokes an attack of opportunity from the defender.

### Armor Class (AC) of Objects
*   **Carried or Worn Object AC:** $10 + \text{size modifier} + \text{Dex modifier of carrying creature}$.
*   **Unattended Object AC:** $10 + \text{size modifier} + \text{Dex modifier (-5 for Dex 0)} - 2 = 3 + \text{size modifier}$.
    *   *Automatic Hit:* Taking a full-round action to line up a shot results in an automatic hit with a melee weapon, or a +5 bonus on attack rolls with a ranged weapon.

| Object Size | Example | AC Modifier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Colossal** | Castle wall | -8 |
| **Gargantuan** | Narrow side of a barn | -4 |
| **Huge** | Wagon | -2 |
| **Large** | Big door | -1 |
| **Medium** | Chest | +0 |
| **Small** | Chair | +1 |
| **Tiny** | Book | +2 |
| **Diminutive** | Scroll | +4 |
| **Fine** | Ring | +8 |

### Hardness & Hit Points
*   **Hardness:** Subtract this number from all damage dealt to the object. Only damage exceeding the hardness reduces the object's hit points.
*   **Hit Points (HP):** The object is fully functional until its hit points reach 0, at which point it is destroyed.
*   **Magic Item Enhancement:** Each +1 of enhancement bonus adds $+2$ to the hardness of armor, a weapon, or a shield, and $+10$ to its hit points.

---

## Special Considerations for Damage

Apply all divisions or multiplications of damage *before* subtracting the object's hardness.

*   **Energy Attacks:**
    *   *Acid and Sonic:* Deal full damage to most objects.
    *   *Fire and Electricity:* Deal half damage to most objects.
    *   *Cold:* Deals one-quarter damage to most objects.
*   **Ranged Weapon Damage:** Objects take half damage from ranged weapons, unless the weapon is a siege engine.
*   **Ineffective Weapons:** The DM can rule that certain weapons cannot effectively deal damage to certain objects (e.g., trying to cut a stone wall with a whip).
*   **Immunities:** Objects are immune to **nonlethal damage** and to **critical hits** (even animated objects are immune as they are constructs).
*   **Vulnerabilities:** The DM can rule that certain attacks are especially successful, dealing double damage or ignoring hardness entirely.

---

## Saving Throws for Objects

*   **Unattended Nonmagical Items:** Never make saving throws. They automatically fail all saves.
*   **Attended Items (attended by a creature):** Use the carrying creature's saving throw modifiers.
*   **Magic Items (unattended):** Always receive saving throws. Their Fortitude, Reflex, and Will saving throw bonuses are equal to $2 + 1/2 \times \text{caster level}$.
*   **Magic Items (attended):** Use the owner's saving throw bonus or the item's own saving throw bonus, whichever is higher.

---

## Disguising Objects

You can use the **Disguise** skill to temporarily change the appearance of an object (Search checks oppose this check).
*   **Time Required:** $1d8 \times 10$ minutes for Small to Large objects.
*   **Limitations:** The object's size or weight cannot apparently change by more than one-tenth of the original unless specially crafted accessories are used (requiring a separate Craft check).

## Substance Hardness & Hit Points

The table below lists the hardness and hit points per inch of thickness for common substances.

| Material | Hardness | HP per Inch of Thickness |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paper or Cloth** | 0 | 2 |
| **Rope (1-inch diam.)** | 0 | 2 |
| **Ice** | 0 | 3 |
| **Leather or Hide** | 2 | 5 |
| **Wood** | 5 | 10 |
| **Stone** | 8 | 15 |
| **Iron or Steel** | 10 | 30 |
| **Mithral** | 15 | 30 |
| **Adamantine** | 20 | 40 |

### Shield and Weapon Hardness (Medium-sized)
*   **Light wooden shield:** Hardness 5, 7 HP
*   **Heavy wooden shield:** Hardness 5, 15 HP
*   **Light steel shield:** Hardness 10, 10 HP
*   **Heavy steel shield:** Hardness 10, 20 HP
*   **Tower shield:** Hardness 5, 20 HP
*   *Note: Divide HP by 2 for each size category the item is smaller than Medium; multiply by 2 for each category larger than Medium.*
*   *Note: Armor has hit points equal to its armor bonus $\times$ 5.*

## Breaking Objects (Strength Checks)

When a creature tries to break an object with sudden force instead of dealing damage, resolve it with a Strength check.

| Task | Strength Check DC |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Break open a small chest** | 17 |
| **Break down simple wooden door (1 in. thick)** | 13 |
| **Break down strong wooden door (2 in. thick)** | 18 |
| **Break down glass door (6 in. thick)** | 18 |
| **Break down ice door (1-1/2 ft. thick)** | 25 |
| **Break down door barred with iron** | 25 |
| **Burst a strand of rope** | 23 *(add Use Rope check result to DC if bound)* |
| **Burst manacles** | 26 |
| **Burst chain** | 26 |
| **Burst masterwork manacles** | 28 |
| **Burst iron bands** | 30 |
| **Break down stone door (4 in. thick)** | 28 |
| **Break down iron door (2 in. thick)** | 28 |

### DC Modifiers
*   **Hold Portal spell:** $+5$ to the DC.
*   **Stability/Size:** Size modifiers apply to the Strength check when attempting to push or break large obstacles.
