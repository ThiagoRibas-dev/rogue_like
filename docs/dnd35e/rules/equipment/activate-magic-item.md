# Activate Magic Item

Activating a magic item is a standard action unless the item description indicates otherwise. The consequences and requirements of activation depend entirely on the item's activation type.

---

## Activation Methods

*   **Command Word:** Speak the activation word. Takes standard action (or spell casting time), no AoO. Blocked by silence.
*   **Continuous:** Continuous effects that don't need activation. Donning the item takes a standard action (or longer) and provokes AoOs.
*   **Manipulation:** Physical activation (like drinking a potion or using a rod). Takes standard action, **provokes attacks of opportunity**, and can be disrupted.
*   **Mental:** Purely mental activation. Standard action, no AoO. Usable while grappled/silenced.
*   **Spell Completion:** Reading a scroll. Takes spell casting time, **provokes attacks of opportunity**, and requires concentration.
*   **Spell Trigger:** Activating a wand or staff. Takes spell casting time, no AoO. Requires the spell to be on your class list.

For complete rules on identifying items, body slots, activation types (including scrolls, wands, staffs, potions, and oils), and Use Magic Device checks, see **[magic-items-general.md](./magic-items-general.md)**.
