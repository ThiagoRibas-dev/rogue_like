# Move 5 Feet through Difficult Terrain

## Category
Full-Round Action

## Summary
In some situations, your movement may be so hampered that you don’t have sufficient speed even to move 5 feet (a single square). In such a case, you may spend a full-round action to move 5 feet (1 square) in any direction, even diagonally.

## Mechanics
- **Action Type:** Full-Round Action
- **Movement:** 5 feet (1 square)
- **Attacks of Opportunity:** This movement provokes attacks of opportunity normally.

## Special Notes
Even though this looks like a 5-foot step, it’s not.
