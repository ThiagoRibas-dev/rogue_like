# Measuring Distance

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Movement

## Summary

This rule explains how to measure distance on the battle grid, particularly focusing on diagonal movement and determining the closest square or creature.

## Mechanics

### Diagonals

-   When measuring distance, the first diagonal square counts as 1 square, the second counts as 2 squares, the third as 1, the fourth as 2, and so on.
-   You cannot move diagonally past a corner (even with a 5-foot step).
-   You can move diagonally past a creature (including an opponent) or other impassable obstacles like pits.

### Closest Creature

-   If two squares or creatures are equally close to a location, randomly determine which one counts as closest by rolling a die.
