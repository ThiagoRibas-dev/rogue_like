# Movement, Position, and Distance

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Core Mechanics

## Summary

This rule outlines the standards for character and creature movement on a battle grid, including how to measure distance and the space creatures occupy. It establishes the 30mm scale for miniatures and the 5-foot square standard for the battle grid.

## Mechanics

### Miniatures and Scale

-   **Scale:** Miniatures are on a 30mm scale, where a 6-foot-tall human is represented by a 30mm figure.
-   **Grid:** The battle grid consists of 1-inch squares, each representing a 5-foot-by-5-foot area.
