# Moving through a Square

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Movement

## Summary

This rule defines how characters and creatures can move through squares occupied by others, distinguishing between friendly characters, opponents, and helpless creatures, and noting exceptions for very small or very large creatures.

## Mechanics

### Moving through a Square

-   **Friend:** You can move through a square occupied by a friendly character, unless you are charging. A friendly character does not provide cover when you move through their square.
-   **Opponent:** You cannot move through a square occupied by an opponent, unless the opponent is helpless.
    -   You can move through a square occupied by a helpless opponent without penalty.
    -   Some very large helpless creatures may still present an obstacle, with each square moved through counting as 2 squares.
-   **Ending Movement:** You cannot end your movement in the same square as another creature unless it is helpless.

### Special Cases

-   **Overrun:** You can attempt to move through a square occupied by an opponent during your movement (see Overrun).
-   **Tumbling:** A trained character can attempt to tumble through a square occupied by an opponent (see the Tumble skill).
-   **Very Small Creature:** A Fine, Diminutive, or Tiny creature can move into or through an occupied square. This provokes attacks of opportunity.
-   **Creature Three Sizes Larger or Smaller:**
    -   Any creature can move through a square occupied by a creature three size categories larger than it.
    -   A big creature can move through a square occupied by a creature three size categories smaller than it.
-   **Designated Exceptions:** Some creatures, particularly those that completely fill their squares, cannot be moved past even with the Tumble skill or similar special abilities.
