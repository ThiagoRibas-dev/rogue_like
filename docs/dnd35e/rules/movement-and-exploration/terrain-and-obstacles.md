# Terrain and Obstacles

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Movement

## Summary

This rule describes how different types of terrain and obstacles affect movement during combat, including difficult terrain, various obstacles, and rules for squeezing through narrow spaces.

## Mechanics

### Difficult Terrain

-   **Movement Cost:** Each square of difficult terrain counts as 2 squares of movement. Diagonal moves into difficult terrain count as 3 squares.
-   **Running/Charging:** You cannot run or charge across difficult terrain.
-   **Mixed Terrain:** If you occupy squares with different terrain types, you move only as fast as the most difficult terrain allows.
-   **Exceptions:** Flying and incorporeal creatures are not hampered by difficult terrain.

### Obstacles

-   **Hampered Movement:** Obstacles that hamper but don't completely block movement cause each obstructed square or obstacle between squares to count as 2 squares of movement. This cost must be paid in addition to moving into the square on the other side. If you lack sufficient movement, you cannot cross. Some obstacles may also require a skill check.
-   **Blocking Movement:** Some obstacles completely block movement, preventing characters from moving through them.
-   **Exceptions:** Flying and incorporeal creatures can avoid most obstacles.

### Squeezing

-   **Narrow Spaces:** You can squeeze through or into a space at least half as wide as your normal space.
-   **Movement Cost:** Each move into or through a narrow space counts as if it were 2 squares.
-   **Penalties:** While squeezed, you take a -4 penalty on attack rolls and a -4 penalty to AC.
-   **Large Creatures:** When a Large creature (normally 4 squares) squeezes into a 1-square-wide space, its miniature occupies two squares, centered on the line between them. Larger creatures are centered similarly.
-   **Ending Movement:** A creature can squeeze past an opponent but cannot end its movement in an occupied square.
-   **Very Narrow Spaces:** To squeeze through or into a space less than half your space's width, you must use the Escape Artist skill. You cannot attack while using Escape Artist to squeeze, take a -4 penalty to AC, and lose any Dexterity bonus to AC.
