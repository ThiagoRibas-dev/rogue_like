# Light and Darkness

Adventurers require light to navigate dark and dangerous locales. Creatures without special senses (like darkvision) are severely hampered by the absence of light.

---

## Light Levels and Mechanics

D&D 3.5e defines three primary light levels, each with distinct mechanical effects:

### 1. Bright Light
*   **Visibility:** Most creatures see clearly.
*   **Stealth:** A creature cannot use the Hide skill in an area of bright light unless it is invisible, has concealment, or has cover.

### 2. Shadowy Illumination
*   **Visibility:** Most creatures see dimly.
*   **Concealment:** Creatures within shadowy illumination have **concealment** (20% miss chance, see [concealment.md](concealment.md)) against attackers who do not possess low-light vision, darkvision, or other sensory overrides.

### 3. Darkness
*   **Visibility:** In complete darkness, creatures without special senses (like darkvision or blindsight) are effectively **blinded** (see [conditions.md](conditions.md)).
*   **Concealment:** Blinded creatures suffer a -2 penalty to AC, lose their Dex bonus to AC, move at half speed, and suffer a -4 penalty on Search checks and most Strength- and Dexterity-based skill checks. All opponents have **total concealment** (50% miss chance) against them.

## Light Sources and Illumination

A light source provides bright illumination out to a specified radius, and shadowy illumination for an equal distance beyond the bright radius. 

### Mundane Light Sources

| Item | Bright Illumination | Shadowy Illumination | Duration | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Candle** | — | 5 feet | 1 hour | Does not provide bright illumination, only shadowy. |
| **Everburning Torch** | 20 feet | 40 feet | Permanent | Magical item. |
| **Lamp (common)** | 15 feet | 30 feet | 6 hours / pint of oil | Burn oil. |
| **Lantern (bullseye)** | 60-foot cone | 120-foot cone | 6 hours / pint of oil | Projects light in a cone rather than a radius. |
| **Lantern (hooded)** | 30 feet | 60 feet | 6 hours / pint of oil | Can be hooded to shut out light. |
| **Sunrod** | 30 feet | 60 feet | 6 hours | Alchemical item. |
| **Torch** | 20 feet | 40 feet | 1 hour | Mundane. |

### Magical Light Sources

| Spell | Bright Illumination | Shadowy Illumination | Duration | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Continual Flame** | 20 feet | 40 feet | Permanent | 2nd-level spell. |
| **Dancing Lights** | 20 feet (each) | 40 feet (each) | 1 minute | Up to four lights. |
| **Daylight** | 60 feet | 120 feet | 30 minutes | 3rd-level spell. |
| **Light** | 20 feet | 40 feet | 10 minutes | 0-level spell. |

## Distant Light

In open spaces, a light source can be spotted from much farther away than its radius of illumination. An observer can notice a distant light source by making a **DC 20 Spot check**.

*   **Observer in Complete Darkness:** Can spot a light source at a distance equal to **20 times** its radius of illumination (e.g., a torch's 40-foot shadowy radius can be spotted up to 800 feet away).
*   **Observer in Dim Light:** Can spot a light source at a distance equal to **10 times** its radius of illumination (e.g., 400 feet for a torch).
*   **Automatic Failure:** An observer who fails the Spot check automatically notices the light source at half of the range.
*   **Looking Into Light:** Observers situated outside the illuminated radius of a light source can see objects or creatures within the illuminated area perfectly fine, making normal Spot checks to do so.
