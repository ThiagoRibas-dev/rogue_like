# Hit Points
## Category
Combat Statistics
## Summary
Hit points measure how hard you are to kill.
## Mechanics
When your hit point total reaches 0, you’re disabled. When it reaches –1, you’re dying. When it gets to –10, you’re dead.
## Special Notes
Hit points represent both the ability to take physical punishment and the ability to turn a serious blow into a less serious one.
