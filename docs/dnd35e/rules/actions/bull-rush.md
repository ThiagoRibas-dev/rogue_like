# Bull Rush

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Special Attack

## Summary

This rule describes the bull rush special attack, allowing a character to push an opponent straight back instead of dealing damage.

## Mechanics

### Initiating a Bull Rush

-   **Action Type:** Standard action (an attack) or part of a charge.
-   **Target:** You can only bull rush an opponent who is one size category larger than you, the same size, or smaller (see [creature-size.md](./creature-size.md)).
-   **Movement:** First, you move into the defender's space.
    -   This provokes an attack of opportunity from each opponent that threatens you, including the defender.
    -   If you have the Improved Bull Rush feat, you do not provoke an attack of opportunity from the defender.
    -   Any attack of opportunity made by anyone other than the defender against you during a bull rush has a 25% chance of accidentally targeting the defender instead.
    -   Any attack of opportunity by anyone other than you against the defender likewise has a 25% chance of accidentally targeting you. (Roll to see if the attack went astray after making the attack roll.)
-   **Required Check:** You and the defender make opposed Strength checks.
    -   **Modifiers:**
        -   You add a +4 bonus for each size category you are larger than Medium, or a -4 penalty for each size category you are smaller than Medium (see [creature-size.md](./creature-size.md) for size details).
        -   You get a +2 bonus if you are charging.
        -   The defender gets a +4 bonus if they have more than two legs or are otherwise exceptionally stable.

### Bull Rush Results

-   **Success Outcome:** If you beat the defender's Strength check result, you push them back 5 feet.
    -   If you wish to move with the defender, you can push them back an additional 5 feet for each 5 points by which your check result is greater than the defender's check result.
    -   You cannot exceed your normal movement limit.
    -   The defender provokes attacks of opportunity if moved. You also provoke attacks of opportunity if you move with them. Neither of you provoke attacks of opportunity from each other.
-   **Failure Outcome:** If you fail to beat the defender's Strength check result, you move 5 feet straight back to where you were before you moved into their space. If that space is occupied, you fall prone in that space.
