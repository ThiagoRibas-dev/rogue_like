# Use Special Ability
## Category
Standard Actions
## Summary
Using a special ability is usually a standard action, but the action type is defined by the ability's description.
## Mechanics
### Spell-Like Abilities
Using a spell-like ability is similar to casting a spell. It requires concentration and provokes attacks of opportunity. If your concentration is broken, the attempt fails but counts as if you had used the ability. The casting time is typically 1 standard action unless otherwise noted.
- **Using on the Defensive:** You can attempt to use a spell-like ability on the defensive with a Concentration check (DC 15 + spell level). If the check fails, you can’t use the ability, but it still counts as if you had used it.
### Supernatural Abilities
Using a supernatural ability is usually a standard action. Its use cannot be disrupted, does not require concentration, and does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
### Extraordinary Abilities
Using an extraordinary ability is usually not an action, as most are reactive. Those that are actions are typically standard actions that cannot be disrupted, do not require concentration, and do not provoke attacks of opportunity.
## Special Notes
The type of special ability (spell-like, supernatural, or extraordinary) determines its mechanics in combat.
