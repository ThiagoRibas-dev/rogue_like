# Overrun

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Special Attack

## Summary

This rule describes the overrun special attack, allowing a character to attempt to move through an opponent's square, potentially knocking them prone.

## Mechanics

### Overrun Attempt

-   **Action Type:** Standard action taken during your move.
-   **Target:** You can only overrun an opponent who is one size category larger than you, the same size, or smaller (see [creature-size.md](./creature-size.md)).
-   **Frequency:** You can make only one overrun attempt per round.
-   **Step 1: Attack of Opportunity:** Since you begin the overrun by moving into the defender's space, you provoke an attack of opportunity from the defender.
-   **Step 2: Opponent Avoids?:** The defender has the option to simply avoid you. If they do, they suffer no ill effect, and you may keep moving. The overrun attempt doesn't count against your actions this round (except for any movement required to enter the opponent's square). If your opponent doesn't avoid you, move to Step 3.
-   **Step 3: Opponent Blocks?:** If your opponent blocks you, make a Strength check opposed by the defender's Dexterity or Strength check (whichever ability score has the higher modifier).
    -   **Modifiers:**
        -   A combatant gets a +4 bonus on the check for every size category they are larger than Medium or a -4 penalty for every size category they are smaller than Medium (see [creature-size.md](./creature-size.md) for details).
        -   The defender gets a +4 bonus on their check if they have more than two legs or are otherwise more stable than a normal humanoid.
    -   **Success Outcome:** If you win, you knock the defender prone.
    -   **Failure Outcome:** If you lose, the defender may immediately react and make a Strength check opposed by your Dexterity or Strength check (including the size modifiers noted above, but no other modifiers) to try to knock you prone.
-   **Step 4: Consequences:**
    -   If you succeed in knocking your opponent prone, you can continue your movement as normal.
    -   If you fail and are knocked prone in turn, you have to move 5 feet back the way you came and fall prone, ending your movement there.
    -   If you fail but are not knocked prone, you have to move 5 feet back the way you came, ending your movement there. If that square is occupied, you fall prone in that square.

### Special Cases

-   **Improved Overrun:** If you have the Improved Overrun feat, your target may not choose to avoid you.
-   **Mounted Overrun (Trample):** If you attempt an overrun while mounted, your mount makes the Strength check to determine the success or failure of the overrun attack (and applies its size modifier, rather than yours). If you have the Trample feat and attempt an overrun while mounted, your target may not choose to avoid you, and if you knock your opponent prone with the overrun, your mount may make one hoof attack against your opponent.
