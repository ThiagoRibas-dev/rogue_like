# Feint

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Special Attack

## Summary

This rule describes the feint special attack, a standard action that allows a character to negate an opponent's Dexterity bonus to AC for their next melee attack.

## Mechanics

### Feint Action

-   **Action Type:** Standard action.
-   **Required Check:** Make a Bluff check opposed by a Sense Motive check by your target. The target may add their base attack bonus to this Sense Motive check.
-   **Success Outcome:** If your Bluff check result exceeds your target's Sense Motive check result, the next melee attack you make against the target does not allow them to use their Dexterity bonus to AC (if any). This attack must be made on or before your next turn.
-   **Attacks of Opportunity:** Feinting in combat does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Penalties for Nonhumanoids

-   **Nonhumanoid:** When feinting against a nonhumanoid, you take a -4 penalty.
-   **Animal Intelligence:** Against a creature of animal Intelligence (1 or 2), you take a -8 penalty.
-   **Nonintelligent Creature:** It is impossible to feint against a nonintelligent creature.

### Feinting as a Move Action

-   With the Improved Feint feat, you can attempt a feint as a move action instead of as a standard action.
