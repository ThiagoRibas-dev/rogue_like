# Standard Actions
## Category
Actions in Combat
## Summary
A standard action allows you to do something, most commonly make an attack or cast a spell.
## Mechanics
### Attack
Making an attack is a standard action.
- **Melee Attacks:** With a normal melee weapon, you can strike any opponent within 5 feet (adjacent). Some melee weapons have reach (e.g., 10 feet), but cannot strike adjacent foes.
- **Unarmed Attacks:** Similar to melee weapons, but attacking unarmed provokes an attack of opportunity from an armed target before your attack. Unarmed attacks do not provoke from other foes or unarmed foes. An unarmed character cannot make attacks of opportunity unless "armed" (e.g., monk, Improved Unarmed Strike feat, touch attack spell, natural physical weapons).
  - **Unarmed Strike Damage:** Medium characters deal 1d3 nonlethal damage (+Str modifier). Small characters deal 1d2, Large characters 1d4. Unarmed strikes count as light weapons.
  - **Dealing Lethal Damage:** You can declare an unarmed strike lethal before the attack roll, taking a -4 penalty. With Improved Unarmed Strike, you can deal lethal damage without penalty.
- **Ranged Attacks:** With a ranged weapon, you can shoot or throw at any target within maximum range and line of sight. Thrown weapons have a maximum of five range increments; projectile weapons have ten.
  - **Attack Rolls:** 1d20 + attack bonus. Hit if result equals or beats target's AC.
  - **Automatic Misses and Hits:** Natural 1 is always a miss. Natural 20 is always a hit and a threat (possible critical hit).
  - **Damage Rolls:** If attack hits, roll weapon damage (plus modifiers). Damage reduces target's current hit points.
  - **Multiple Attacks:** Requires a full attack action.
  - **Shooting or Throwing into a Melee:** -4 penalty on attack roll if target is engaged in melee with a friendly character. Penalty is avoided if target is at least 10 feet away from nearest friendly character. Precise Shot feat negates this penalty.
- **Fighting Defensively as a Standard Action:** Take a -4 penalty on all attacks in a round to gain a +2 dodge bonus to AC for the same round.
- **Critical Hits:** Natural 20 on attack roll is a threat. Make a critical roll (another attack roll with same modifiers). If critical roll hits AC, original hit is a critical hit. Critical hits multiply damage (roll damage multiple times and total; extra damage dice are not multiplied). Default threat range is 20, multiplier x2. Increased threat range means lower numbers can threaten, but are not automatic hits unless they hit AC. Increased critical multiplier means better than double damage. Spells requiring an attack roll can crit; spells not requiring an attack roll cannot.

### Cast a Spell
Most spells require 1 standard action. You can cast before or after a move action. You retain Dexterity bonus to AC while casting.
- **Spell Components:**
  - **Verbal (V):** Requires speaking in a firm voice. Gagged or in *silence* area prevents casting. Deafened spellcaster has 20% chance to spoil spell with verbal component.
  - **Somatic (S):** Requires free gestures with at least one hand. Cannot cast while bound, grappling, or with both hands full.
  - **Material (M), Focus (F), Divine Focus (DF):** Requires proper materials. Preparing unlisted materials is a free action if you have a spell component pouch.
  - **Experience Point (XP):** Entails an XP cost. Cannot restore lost XP. Cannot spend so much XP that you lose a level. XP expended whether casting succeeds or fails.
- **Concentration:** Required to cast. If concentration is broken (e.g., by damage), make a Concentration check (DC depends on threat) or lose the spell. Lost prepared spells are gone; at-will spells count against daily limit.
- **Concentrating to Maintain a Spell:** Standard action, does not provoke attacks of opportunity. Anything that breaks casting concentration breaks maintenance. If concentration breaks, spell ends.
- **Casting Time:** Most are 1 standard action, taking effect immediately.
- **Attacks of Opportunity:** Generally, casting a spell provokes attacks of opportunity. If damaged, make a Concentration check (DC 10 + damage taken + spell level) or lose the spell. Free action spells do not provoke.
- **Casting on the Defensive:** Does not provoke attacks of opportunity, but requires a Concentration check (DC 15 + spell level). Failure means losing the spell.
- **Touch Spells in Combat:** Cast spell, then touch subject in same round or later. Automatically touch one friend or yourself. To touch opponent, make an attack roll.
  - **Touch Attacks:** Armed attack, does not provoke attacks of opportunity (though casting does). Can score critical hits. Opponent's AC against touch attack does not include armor, shield, or natural armor bonus. Size, Dexterity, and deflection bonuses apply.
  - **Holding the Charge:** If not discharged in casting round, you can hold the charge indefinitely. Can make touch attacks round after round. Touching anything (even unintentionally) discharges spell. Casting another spell dissipates touch spell. Can make normal unarmed/natural weapon attack while holding charge; if it hits, deal normal damage and spell discharges. If it misses, still holding charge.
- **Dismiss a Spell:** Standard action, does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Activate Magic Item
Standard action (unless item specifies otherwise).
- **Spell Completion Items:** Equivalent to casting a spell. Requires concentration, provokes attacks of opportunity. Can be lost if concentration broken. Can attempt to activate on defensive.
- **Spell Trigger, Command Word, or Use-Activated Items:** Does not require concentration, does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Use Special Ability
Usually a standard action, but defined by the ability.
- **Spell-Like Abilities:** Like casting a spell. Requires concentration, provokes attacks of opportunity, can be disrupted. If concentration broken, attempt fails but counts as used. Casting time is 1 standard action unless noted.
  - **Using on the Defensive:** Can attempt on defensive (DC 15 + spell level Concentration check). Failure means inability to use, but still counts as used.
- **Supernatural Abilities:** Usually standard action. Cannot be disrupted, no concentration, no attacks of opportunity.
- **Extraordinary Abilities:** Usually not an action (reactive). If an action, usually standard, cannot be disrupted, no concentration, no attacks of opportunity.

### Total Defense
Standard action. Gain +4 dodge bonus to AC for 1 round. AC improves at start of action. Cannot combine with fighting defensively or Combat Expertise. Cannot make attacks of opportunity.

### Start/Complete Full-Round Action
Standard action to start a full-round action, completed in next round with another standard action. Cannot be used for full attack, charge, run, or withdraw.
## Special Notes
Standard actions form the backbone of most combat turns, allowing for attacks, spellcasting, and various other tactical maneuvers.
