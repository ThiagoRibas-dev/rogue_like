# Charge
## Category
Special Attack / Combat Action

## Summary
Charging is a special full-round action that allows you to move up to twice your speed and attack in a single turn. It carries strict movement restrictions, grants a bonus on the attack roll, and temporarily lowers your AC.

## Mechanics

### 1. Initiating a Charge
*   **Action Type:** Full-round action (or standard action if restricted; see below).
*   **Line of Sight:** You must have line of sight to the target at the start of your turn to charge them.
*   **Movement Restrictions:**
    *   **Distance:** You must move at least 10 feet (2 squares) and can move up to double your speed.
    *   **Direct Path:** You must move along a straight line directly toward the closest space from which you can attack the target.
    *   **Clear Path:** The path must be clear. If any line from your starting space to your ending space passes through a square that contains a creature (even an ally, unless they are helpless), blocks movement, or is difficult terrain, you cannot charge.
    *   **Ending Space:** You cannot charge if your ending space is blocked or occupied.
    *   **5-Foot Step:** You cannot take a 5-foot step in the same round you charge.
    *   **Attacks of Opportunity:** You provoke attacks of opportunity as normal for your movement.

### 2. Resolving a Charge
*   **The Attack:** After moving, you can make a single melee attack. You cannot make a full attack unless you have a special ability that allows it (such as Pounce).
*   **Bonuses:** You receive a **+2 bonus** on your attack roll.
*   **AC Penalty:** You take a **-2 penalty** to your AC until the start of your next turn.
*   **Bull Rush Option:** If you attempt a bull rush as your charge attack, you gain a **+2 bonus** on the opposed Strength check. See [bull-rush.md](./bull-rush.md).

### 3. Specialized Movement during a Charge
*   **Balancing:** You can make a Balance check to charge across a precarious surface.
    *   *Check Penalty:* You take a -5 penalty on the Balance check for each multiple of your speed (or fraction thereof) that you charge.
    *   *Frequency:* Make one Balance check for each multiple of your speed (or fraction thereof) that you charge. Failing a check ends your movement and prevents the charge attack. See [use-skill.md](./use-skill.md).
*   **Jumping:** You can make a long jump to clear an obstacle during a charge, provided you continue to meet all other charge criteria before, during, and after the jump.
*   **Tumbling:** You can tumble during a charge to avoid provoking attacks of opportunity, provided you continue to meet all other charge criteria before, during, and after tumbling. See [use-skill.md](./use-skill.md).
*   **Flying (Dive Attacks):** A flying creature can make a dive attack instead of a normal charge.
    *   *Requirements:* Must move a minimum of 30 feet and descend at least 10 feet.
    *   *Restrictions:* Can make only claw or talon attacks.
    *   *Benefit:* The claw or talon attacks deal double damage.

### 4. Restricted Actions (Standard Action Charge)
If you are unable to take a full round's worth of actions on your turn (such as during a surprise round or under a slow effect), you can charge as a standard action.
*   *Movement Limit:* You can move only up to your speed (instead of double).
*   All other restrictions and benefits of a charge still apply. See [surprise.md](./surprise.md).

## Weapon Interactions
*   **Lances:** A lance deals double damage when wielded by a mounted character during a charge. See [mounted-combat.md](./mounted-combat.md).
*   **Setting against a Charge:** Certain piercing weapons (like spears and tridents) deal double damage when readied (set) and used against a charging creature. See [ready.md](./ready.md).
