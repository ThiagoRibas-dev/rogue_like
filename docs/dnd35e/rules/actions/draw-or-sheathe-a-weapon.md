# Draw or Sheathe a Weapon
## Category
Move Actions
## Summary
Drawing a weapon to use in combat or sheathing it to free a hand requires a move action.
## Mechanics
This action applies to weapons and weapon-like objects, such as wands, carried in easy reach. If an item is stored in a pack or is otherwise out of easy reach, retrieving it is treated as retrieving a stored item.
- If you have a base attack bonus of +1 or higher, you may draw a weapon as a free action combined with a regular move.
- If you have the Two-Weapon Fighting feat, you can draw two light or one-handed weapons in the time it would normally take to draw one.
- Drawing ammunition for a ranged weapon (arrows, bolts, sling bullets, or shuriken) is a free action.
## Special Notes
The ability to draw a weapon as a free action is a significant advantage for characters with a high base attack bonus.
