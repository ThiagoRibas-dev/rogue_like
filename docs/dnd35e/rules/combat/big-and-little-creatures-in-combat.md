# Big and Little Creatures in Combat

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Combat Mechanics

## Summary

This rule outlines special considerations for creatures smaller than Small or larger than Medium regarding their space, reach, and interactions in combat.

## Mechanics

### Tiny, Diminutive, and Fine Creatures

-   **Space:** These very small creatures take up less than 1 square of space.
    -   Four Tiny creatures, twenty-five Diminutive creatures, or one hundred Fine creatures can fit into a single square.
-   **Natural Reach:** Typically have a natural reach of 0 feet, meaning they cannot reach into adjacent squares.
    -   They must enter an opponent's square to attack in melee, which provokes an attack of opportunity from the opponent.
    -   You can attack into your own square to attack such creatures normally.
-   **Threatened Squares:** They do not threaten squares around them, so you can move past them without provoking attacks of opportunity.
-   **Flanking:** They cannot flank an enemy.

### Large, Huge, Gargantuan, and Colossal Creatures

-   **Space:** These very large creatures take up more than 1 square.
-   **Natural Reach:** Typically have a natural reach of 10 feet or more, allowing them to reach targets not in adjacent squares.
    -   Unlike reach weapons, creatures with greater than normal natural reach (more than 5 feet) still threaten adjacent squares.
    -   They usually get an attack of opportunity if you approach them, as you must enter and move within their reach before attacking (not provoked by a 5-foot step).
-   **Reach Weapons:** Large or larger creatures using reach weapons can strike up to double their natural reach but cannot strike at their natural reach or less.

### Table: Creature Size and Scale

| Creature Size | Space¹ | Natural Reach¹ |
| :------------ | :----- | :------------- |
| Fine          | 1/2 ft. | 0              |
| Diminutive    | 1 ft.  | 0              |
| Tiny          | 2-1/2 ft. | 0              |
| Small         | 5 ft.  | 5 ft.          |
| Medium        | 5 ft.  | 5 ft.          |
| Large (tall)  | 10 ft. | 10 ft.         |
| Large (long)  | 10 ft. | 5 ft.          |
| Huge (tall)   | 15 ft. | 15 ft.         |
| Huge (long)   | 15 ft. | 10 ft.         |
| Gargantuan (tall) | 20 ft. | 20 ft.         |
| Gargantuan (long) | 20 ft. | 15 ft.         |
| Colossal (tall) | 30 ft. | 30 ft.         |
| Colossal (long) | 30 ft. | 20 ft.         |

¹ These values are typical for creatures of the indicated size. Some exceptions exist.
