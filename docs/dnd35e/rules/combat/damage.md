# Damage and Damage Types
## Category
Combat Statistics

## Summary
When an attack succeeds, it deals damage, reducing the target's current hit points. Damage is categorized by its type (melee/weapon damage, energy damage, and precision damage), which determines how it interacts with immunities, resistances, and damage reduction (DR).

## Mechanics

### 1. General Damage Rules
*   **Minimum Damage:** If penalties (such as from low Strength) reduce your damage roll to less than 1, a successful hit still deals **1 point of damage**.
*   **Strength Modifiers:**
    *   **Melee / Thrown / Sling:** Add your Strength modifier to the damage roll.
    *   **Off-Hand Weapon:** Add only **1/2 your Strength bonus** to off-hand damage. See [two-weapon-fighting.md](./two-weapon-fighting.md).
    *   **Two-Handed Wielding:** Add **1.5 times your Strength bonus** to the damage roll (does not apply to light weapons wielded with two hands).
    *   **Bows:** Apply your Strength penalty to damage rolls on normal bows. You do not apply your Strength bonus unless you are using a Composite Bow designed for your Strength rating.
*   **Multiplying Damage:** When damage is multiplied (e.g., on a critical hit), roll the damage dice and add all modifiers multiple times.
    *   *Multiple Multipliers:* If damage is multiplied more than once (e.g., a critical hit with a weapon set against a charge), each multiplier applies to the base damage. E.g., two $\times 2$ multipliers combine to a $\times 3$ multiplier.
    *   *Exceptions:* Extra damage dice that are not part of the weapon's base damage (such as precision damage or flaming weapon dice) are **never multiplied**.

## 2. Damage Types

### Weapon Damage
Manufactured and natural melee/ranged weapons deal one of three types of physical damage:
*   **Bludgeoning (B)**
*   **Piercing (P)**
*   **Slashing (S)**
Some weapons can deal multiple types (e.g., piercing or slashing), or both simultaneously.

### Energy Damage
Spells, magical weapons, and elemental effects deal one of five energy types:
*   **Acid**
*   **Cold**
*   **Electricity**
*   **Fire**
*   **Sonic**

### Positive and Negative Energy
*   **Positive Energy:** Typically heals living creatures and damages undead.
*   **Negative Energy:** Typically heals undead and damages living creatures.
*   *Note: Positive and negative energy are damage types, but they are not considered "energy types" for effects like Resist Energy spells.*

## 3. Precision Damage
Precision damage represents hitting a vital spot. It includes class features like **Sneak Attack**, **Skirmish**, and **Sudden Strike**.

*   **Optimal Wielding:** Precision damage only applies if you are wielding your weapon optimally. If you take a -4 penalty to deal nonlethal damage with a lethal weapon, you cannot deal precision damage.
*   **Ranged Limit:** Ranged attacks can only deal precision damage if the target is within **30 feet** or less.
*   **Immune Targets:** Precision damage only affects living creatures with discernible anatomies. You cannot deal precision damage to:
    *   Constructs, Undead, Deathless, Oozes, Plants, and Incorporeal creatures.
    *   Any creature immune to critical hits.
*   **Concealment Block:** Any degree of concealment (even shadowy illumination) completely foils your ability to deal precision damage. See [concealment.md](./concealment.md).
*   **Full Attacks:** Precision damage applies to all attacks in a full attack that meet the requirements. If conditions change mid-attack (e.g., you are no longer flanking), subsequent attacks do not receive precision damage. See [full-attack.md](./full-attack.md).
*   **Standard / Swift Multi-Attacks:** Attacks that grant multiple hits during an action other than a full attack (such as a *Manyshot* standard action, or a swift action *scorching ray*) apply precision damage **only to the first hit**.
*   **Damage Multiplication:** Precision damage dice are never multiplied on a critical hit.
