# Attack Roll
## Category
Combat Statistics
## Summary
An attack roll represents your attempt to strike your opponent on your turn in a round.
## Mechanics
When you make an attack roll, you roll a d20 and add your attack bonus. Other modifiers may also apply. If your result equals or beats the target’s Armor Class, you hit and deal damage.
### Automatic Misses and Hits
- A natural 1 (the d20 comes up 1) on an attack roll is always a miss.
- A natural 20 (the d20 comes up 20) is always a hit. A natural 20 is also a threat—a possible critical hit.
## Special Notes
The attack roll is a fundamental mechanic for determining success in combat.
