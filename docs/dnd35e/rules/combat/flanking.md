# Flanking
## Category
Combat Mechanics

## Summary
Flanking represents positioning yourself and an ally on opposite sides of a defender, forcing them to divide their attention. It grants a **+2 circumstance bonus** on melee attack rolls.

## Mechanics

### 1. Determining Flanking
To determine if you and an ally flank an opponent:
*   **Opposite Borders/Corners:** You and your ally must be on the defender's opposite borders or opposite corners.
*   **The Center Line:** Trace an imaginary line between the centers of the squares occupied by you and your ally. If this line passes through opposite borders (including corners of those borders) of the defender's space, the defender is flanked.
*   **Threat Requirement:** Both you and your ally must actively threaten the defender. If either of you cannot attack the defender (e.g., due to being unarmed, holding a ranged weapon, or having a reach of 0 feet), you cannot flank.

### 2. Large and Larger Flankers
If a flanker occupies more than 1 square (i.e., a Large or larger creature), they count as flanking if **any square** they occupy satisfies the center-line flanking condition.

### 3. Reach of 0 Feet
Creatures with a natural reach of 0 feet (such as Tiny or smaller creatures) cannot threaten squares adjacent to them. Consequently, they cannot flank or help an ally flank. See [big-and-little-creatures-in-combat.md](./big-and-little-creatures-in-combat.md).

---

## Tactical Benefits of Flanking
*   **Melee Attack Bonus:** Flanking grants you a **+2 circumstance bonus** on melee attack rolls against the flanked target.
*   **Sneak Attack (Rogue):** A Rogue can make a sneak attack against an opponent they are flanking, as the opponent is distracted by the ally.
*   **Special Abilities:** Certain class features or feats (such as *Outflank* or *Vexing Flanker*) can increase the bonuses or benefits of flanking.
