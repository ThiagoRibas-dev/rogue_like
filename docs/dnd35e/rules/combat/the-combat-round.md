# The Combat Round
## Category
Actions in Combat
## Summary
Each round represents 6 seconds in the game world, providing an opportunity for each character to take an action.
## Mechanics
Each round's activity begins with the character with the highest initiative result and then proceeds in order. The same initiative order is used for each round of combat. When a character's turn comes up, they perform their entire round's worth of actions (with exceptions for Attacks of Opportunity and Special Initiative Actions).
For most purposes, there is no relevance to the end or beginning of a round. A round can be a segment of game time starting with the first character to act and ending with the last, or it can mean a span of time from one round to the same initiative count in the next round. Effects that last a certain number of rounds end just before the same initiative count that they began on.
## Special Notes
Understanding the combat round is fundamental to managing actions and effects in D&D combat.
