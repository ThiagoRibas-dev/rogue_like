# Surprise
## Category
Combat Basics
## Summary
When a combat starts, if you are not aware of your opponents and they are aware of you, you’re surprised.
## Mechanics
### Determining Awareness
Awareness can vary among combatants and may require Listen, Spot, or other checks.
### The Surprise Round
If some but not all combatants are aware of their opponents, a surprise round occurs before regular rounds. Aware combatants roll initiative and each take a standard action (or free actions) in initiative order. If no one or everyone is surprised, no surprise round occurs.
### Unaware Combatants
Combatants unaware at the start of battle do not act in the surprise round. They are flat-footed and lose any Dexterity bonus to AC.
## Special Notes
Surprise rounds can significantly impact the initial moments of combat, granting an advantage to aware combatants.
