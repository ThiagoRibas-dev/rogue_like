# Cover
## Category
Combat Mechanics

## Summary
Cover provides physical protection from attacks and hides you from sight. It grants bonuses to AC and Reflex saves, enables Hide checks, and prevents attacks of opportunity.

## Mechanics

### 1. Determining Cover
To determine if a target has cover:
*   **Ranged Attack (and Non-Adjacent Melee/Reach):** Choose any corner of your square. Trace lines from this corner to all four corners of the target's square. If any of these lines pass through a square or border that blocks line of effect or provides cover, or through a square occupied by a creature, the target has cover.
*   **Melee Attack (Adjacent Target):** The target has cover if any line from your square to the target's square intersects a wall (including a low wall or partition).
*   **Big Creatures:** A creature larger than 5 feet (1 square) determines cover slightly differently.
    *   *Attacking:* A large creature can choose **any square** it occupies to determine if a target has cover against its melee attacks.
    *   *Defending:* When attacking a large creature in melee, you can choose **any square** it occupies to determine if it has cover against you. See [big-and-little-creatures-in-combat.md](./big-and-little-creatures-in-combat.md).

### 2. Standard Cover Effects
Standard cover provides the following benefits:
*   **AC Bonus:** A **+4 cover bonus** to AC.
*   **Reflex Save Bonus:** A **+2 cover bonus** on Reflex saves against attacks that originate or burst from a point on the other side of the cover.
    *   *Exception:* Spread effects (like a *fireball* spell) extend around corners and negate this Reflex save bonus.
*   **No Attacks of Opportunity:** You cannot make attacks of opportunity against an opponent who has cover relative to you. See [attacks-of-opportunity.md](./attacks-of-opportunity.md).
*   **Hide Checks:** You can use cover to make a Hide check. Without cover, you usually need concealment to hide. See [use-skill.md](./use-skill.md).

### 3. Obstacles & Tactical Limits
*   **Striking Through Narrow Openings:** The DM can impose penalties or restrictions based on cover geometry. E.g., to attack through an arrow slit, you must use a long piercing weapon (like an arrow or spear); a slashing weapon like a battleaxe cannot reach through.

## Different Forms of Cover
Multiple forms of cover **do not stack**; you only benefit from the single best form of cover available.

### 1. Improved Cover
Peering around a corner, through a narrow arrow slit, or behind a crenellation.
*   **AC Bonus:** +8 cover bonus to AC.
*   **Reflex Save Bonus:** +4 cover bonus on Reflex saves.
*   **Evasion Benefit:** Grants the benefits of **Improved Evasion** against any attack to which the Reflex save bonus applies.
*   **Hide Bonus:** Grants a **+10 bonus** on Hide checks.

### 2. Limited Cover
Standing in the same square as a partial obstacle (e.g., a tree trunk or pillar).
*   **AC Bonus:** +2 cover bonus to AC.
*   **Reflex Save Bonus:** +1 cover bonus on Reflex saves.
*   *Note: This does not affect your fighting space; you use the object's protection dynamically.*

### 3. Soft Cover
Creatures (including allies or enemies) standing in the path of your ranged attack.
*   **AC Bonus:** +4 cover bonus to AC.
*   **Save/Hide Restriction:** Soft cover provides **no bonus** on Reflex saves, and you **cannot** use soft cover to make Hide checks.

### 4. Low Obstacles
A wall or barrier no higher than half of your height.
*   **Distance Limit:** Provides cover only if you are within 30 feet (6 squares) of the obstacle.
*   **Attacker Proximity:** If the attacker is closer to the obstacle than the target, the attacker can ignore the cover.

### 5. Total Cover
When you lack line of effect to a target.
*   **Attack Limit:** You cannot make any direct attack against a target with total cover.
*   *Spread exception:* A spread effect can still reach a target behind total cover if the effect's area is large enough to travel around the barrier.
