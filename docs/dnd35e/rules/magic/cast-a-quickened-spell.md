# Cast a Quickened Spell

## Category
Free Action

## Summary
You can cast a quickened spell or any spell whose casting time is designated as a free action as a free action.

## Mechanics
- **Action Type:** Free Action
- **Required Check:** None
- **Success Outcome:** You cast the spell.
- **Restrictions:** Only one such spell can be cast in any round, and such spells don’t count toward your normal limit of one spell per round.
- **Attacks of Opportunity:** Casting a spell with a casting time of a free action doesn’t incur an attack of opportunity.

## Special Notes
See the Quicken Spell feat for more information.
