# Cast a Spell (Full-Round)

## Category
Full-Round Action

## Summary
A spell that takes 1 round to cast is a full-round action.

## Mechanics
- **Action Type:** Full-Round Action
- **Casting Time:** 1 round. The spell comes into effect just before the beginning of your turn in the round after you began casting the spell. You then act normally after the spell is completed.
- **Concentration:** When you begin a spell that takes 1 round or longer to cast, you must continue the invocations, gestures, and concentration from one round to just before your turn in the next round (at least). If you lose concentration after starting the spell and before it is complete, you lose the spell.
- **Attacks of Opportunity:** You only provoke attacks of opportunity when you begin casting a spell, even though you might continue casting for at least one full round. While casting a spell, you don’t threaten any squares around you.

## Special Notes
- **Casting a Metamagic Spell:** Sorcerers and bards must take more time to cast a metamagic spell (one enhanced by a metamagic feat) than a regular spell. If a spell’s normal casting time is 1 standard action, casting a metamagic version of the spell is a full-round action for a sorcerer or bard. For spells with a longer casting time, it takes an extra full-round action to cast the metamagic spell.
- **Spontaneous Casting:** Clerics must take more time to spontaneously cast a metamagic version of a cure or inflict spell. Spontaneously casting a metamagic version of a spell with a casting time of 1 standard action is a full-round action, and spells with longer casting times take an extra full-round action to cast.
