# Wisdom (WIS)

## Category
Ability Score

## Summary
Describes a character’s willpower, common sense, perception, and intuition.

## Mechanics
Apply Wisdom modifier to:
*   Will saving throws.
*   Heal, Listen, Profession, Sense Motive, Spot, and Survival checks.
Clerics, druids, paladins, and rangers get bonus spells based on their Wisdom scores. The minimum Wisdom score needed to cast a cleric, druid, paladin, or ranger spell is 10 + the spell’s level.

## Special Notes
Most important for clerics and druids, also important for paladins and rangers.
