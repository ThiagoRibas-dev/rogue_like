# Nonabilities

Some creatures do not possess certain ability scores. These creatures do not have an ability score of 0—they lack the ability score altogether. 

The modifier for any nonability is **+0**. Other mechanical effects of nonabilities are detailed below.

---

## Strength (Str)

A creature has no Strength score if it has no physical body (such as a ghost) or is entirely incapable of movement and exerting physical force. Any creature that can physically manipulate other objects has at least 1 point of Strength.

*   **Checks:** Automatically fails all Strength checks.
*   **Combat:** If the creature can attack, it applies its Dexterity modifier (instead of Strength) to its melee attack rolls. It cannot exert physical force.

---

## Dexterity (Dex)

A creature has no Dexterity score if it is entirely incapable of movement (such as a shrieker). Any creature that can move has at least 1 point of Dexterity.

*   **Checks and Saves:** Automatically fails all Dexterity checks and Reflex saving throws.
*   **Initiative:** If the creature can perform actions (such as casting spells), it applies its Intelligence modifier (instead of Dexterity) to initiative checks.

---

## Constitution (Con)

A creature has no Constitution score if it has no body (such as a shadow or ghost) or lacks a metabolism (such as undead, constructs, or deathless). Any living creature has at least 1 point of Constitution.

*   **Saves:** Immune to any effect that requires a Fortitude save, unless the effect also works on objects or is harmless.
*   **Checks:** Automatically fails all Constitution checks.
*   **Immunities:** Immune to **fatigue**, **exhaustion**, **ability damage**, **ability drain**, and **energy drain**.
*   **Concentration:** Uses its **Charisma modifier** (instead of Constitution) to make Concentration checks (see [skill-concentration.md](./skill-concentration.md)).

---

## Intelligence (Int)

A creature has no Intelligence score if it is mindless—an automaton operating solely on instincts or programmed instructions (such as zombies, skeletons, vermin, or animated objects). Any creature that can think, learn, or remember has at least 1 point of Intelligence.

*   **Spells/Abilities:** Immune to all mind-affecting spells and abilities (charms, compulsions, phantasms, patterns, and morale effects).
*   **Checks:** Automatically fails all Intelligence checks.
*   **Feats and Skills:** Mindless creatures do not gain feats or skills, though they can possess racial bonus feats or racial skill modifiers.

---

## Wisdom (Wis)

Anything without a Wisdom score is an **object**, not a creature. Any creature capable of perceiving its environment in any fashion (including through sight, sound, or magic) has at least 1 point of Wisdom. 
*   An object that lacks a Wisdom score also lacks a Charisma score.

---

## Charisma (Cha)

Any creature capable of telling the difference between itself and other creatures/objects has at least 1 point of Charisma. 
*   An object that lacks a Charisma score also lacks a Wisdom score.
