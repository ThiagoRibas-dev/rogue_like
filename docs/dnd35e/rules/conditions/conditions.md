# Conditions

## Category
Basic Rule

## Summary
Describes various conditions that can affect characters and creatures, and their effects.

## Mechanics
If more than one condition affects a character, apply them all. If certain effects can’t combine, apply the most severe effect.

- **Ability Damaged:** The character has temporarily lost 1 or more ability score points. Lost points return at a rate of 1 per day unless noted otherwise.
- **Ability Drained:** The character has permanently lost 1 or more ability score points. Can only be regained through magical means.
- **Blinded:** Cannot see. –2 penalty to AC, loses Dexterity bonus to AC, moves at half speed, –4 penalty on Search and most Str/Dex-based skill checks. Vision-based checks automatically fail. Opponents have total concealment (50% miss chance).
- **Blown Away:** Knocked down and rolls 1d4 x 10 feet, taking 1d4 nonlethal damage per 10 feet. Flying creatures are blown back 2d6 x 10 feet and take 2d6 nonlethal damage.
- **Checked:** Prevented from forward motion. Checked creatures on the ground stop; flying creatures move back.
- **Confused:** Actions determined by d% roll: 01–10, attack caster; 11–20, act normally; 21–50, do nothing but babble; 51–70, flee from caster; 71–100, attack nearest creature.
- **Cowering:** Frozen in fear, can take no actions. –2 penalty to AC, loses Dexterity bonus to AC.
- **Dazed:** Unable to act normally. Can take no actions, but no penalty to AC.
- **Dazzled:** Unable to see well. –1 penalty on attack rolls, Search, and Spot checks.
- **Dead:** Hit points at –10, Constitution at 0, or killed outright. Soul leaves the body.
- **Deafened:** Cannot hear. –4 penalty on initiative checks, automatically fails Listen checks, 20% chance of spell failure with verbal components.
- **Disabled:** At 0 hit points, or negative but stable and conscious. May take a single move or standard action each round (not both). Moves at half speed. Standard actions deal 1 point of damage.
- **Dying:** Unconscious and near death, with –1 to –9 current hit points. Can take no actions. 10% chance to become stable each round, otherwise loses 1 hit point.
- **Energy Drained:** Gains one or more negative levels. Dies if negative levels equal or exceed Hit Dice.
- **Entangled:** Impeded movement. Moves at half speed, cannot run or charge, –2 penalty on attack rolls, –4 penalty to Dexterity. Must make a Concentration check (DC 15 + spell level) to cast a spell.
- **Exhausted:** Moves at half speed, –6 penalty to Strength and Dexterity. Becomes fatigued after 1 hour of rest.
- **Fascinated:** Entranced. Takes no actions other than to pay attention to the fascinating effect. –4 penalty on reactive skill checks. Any potential threat allows a new saving throw. Obvious threats automatically break the effect.
- **Fatigued:** Can neither run nor charge, –2 penalty to Strength and Dexterity. Becomes exhausted by anything that would cause fatigue. No longer fatigued after 8 hours of rest.
- **Flat-Footed:** Has not yet acted in combat. Loses Dexterity bonus to AC and cannot make attacks of opportunity.
- **Frightened:** Flees from the source of fear. –2 penalty on all attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks.
- **Grappling:** Engaged in hand-to-hand struggle. Can take limited actions, does not threaten any squares, loses Dexterity bonus to AC against opponents not being grappled.
- **Helpless:** Paralyzed, held, bound, sleeping, unconscious, etc. Treated as having Dexterity 0 (–5 modifier). Melee attacks get a +4 bonus. Ranged attacks get no bonus. Rogues can sneak attack. A coup de grace can be delivered.
- **Incorporeal:** Having no physical body. Immune to nonmagical attacks.
- **Invisible:** Visually undetectable. +2 bonus on attack rolls against sighted opponents, ignores opponents’ Dexterity bonuses to AC.
- **Knocked Down:** Knocked prone by wind. Flying creatures are blown back 1d6 x 10 feet.
- **Nauseated:** Unable to attack, cast spells, concentrate, or do anything requiring attention. Can only take a single move action per turn.
- **Panicked:** Drops everything and flees at top speed from the source of fear along a random path. –2 penalty on all saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks. Cowers if cornered.
- **Paralyzed:** Frozen in place, unable to move or act. Effective Dexterity and Strength of 0, helpless. Can take purely mental actions.
- **Petrified:** Turned to stone, considered unconscious.
- **Pinned:** Held immobile in a grapple.
- **Prone:** On the ground. –4 penalty on melee attack rolls, cannot use ranged weapons (except crossbows). +4 bonus to AC against ranged attacks, –4 penalty to AC against melee attacks.
- **Shaken:** –2 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks.
- **Sickened:** –2 penalty on all attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks.
- **Stable:** Dying but has stopped losing hit points. Unconscious.
- **Staggered:** Nonlethal damage equals current hit points. May take a single move or standard action each round.
- **Stunned:** Drops everything held, can’t take actions, –2 penalty to AC, loses Dexterity bonus to AC.
- **Turned:** Affected by turn undead. Flees for 10 rounds. Cowers if unable to flee.
- **Unconscious:** Knocked out and helpless.
