# Helpless Defenders

This material is Open Game Content, and is licensed for public use under the terms of the Open Game License v1.0a.

## Category

Combat Mechanics

## Summary

This rule defines the conditions and consequences for a helpless opponent, including how they are affected by regular attacks and the special action of a coup de grace.

## Mechanics

### Helpless Condition

A helpless opponent is bound, sleeping, paralyzed, unconscious, or otherwise at your mercy.

### Regular Attack against a Helpless Defender

-   **Melee Attacks:** A helpless character takes a -4 penalty to AC against melee attacks.
-   **Ranged Attacks:** There is no penalty to AC against ranged attacks.
-   **Dexterity Bonus:** A helpless defender cannot use any Dexterity bonus to AC. Their Dexterity score is treated as 0, and their Dexterity modifier to AC as -5.
-   **Sneak Attack:** Rogues can sneak attack helpless or pinned defenders.

### Coup de Grace

-   **Action:** As a full-round action, you can deliver a coup de grace to a helpless opponent with a melee weapon. You can also use a bow or crossbow if you are adjacent to the target.
-   **Attack Roll:** You automatically hit and score a critical hit.
-   **Fortitude Save:** If the defender survives the damage, they must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + damage dealt) or die.
-   **Sneak Attack:** A rogue gets their extra sneak attack damage against a helpless opponent when delivering a coup de grace.
-   **Attacks of Opportunity:** Delivering a coup de grace provokes attacks of opportunity from threatening opponents.
-   **Immunity to Critical Hits:** You cannot deliver a coup de grace against a creature immune to critical hits.
-   **Total Concealment:** Delivering a coup de grace against a creature with total concealment requires two consecutive full-round actions (one to "find" the creature, one to deliver the coup de grace).
