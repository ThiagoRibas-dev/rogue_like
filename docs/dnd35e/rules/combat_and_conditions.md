# Combat System & Status Conditions

## 1. Combat Sequence
Combat is structured in rounds representing 6 seconds of game time.
1.  **Surprise Round:** If some (but not all) combatants are aware of their opponents, they act in initiative order during a surprise round, restricted to a single Standard or Move action.
2.  **Initiative:** All participants roll $d20 + \text{Dexterity Modifier} + \text{Misc Modifiers}$ (e.g., Improved Initiative). This determines the turn order for the rest of combat.
3.  **Combat Rounds:** In initiative order, each combatant takes their turn. A standard turn allows one Standard action and one Move action, or one Full-Round action, plus any number of Free/Swift actions.

## 2. Actions in Combat

| Action Type | Description | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Action** | A single main action, usually an attack or spell. | Melee/Ranged Attack, Cast a Spell (1 standard action casting time), Use Special Ability, Aid Another. |
| **Move Action** | Move up to your speed or perform an equivalent action. | Walk, Draw/Stow Weapon, Stand Up, Open/Close Door, Retrieve Stored Item. |
| **Full-Round Action** | Consumes your entire turn. You can still take a 5-foot step. | Full Attack, Charge, Run, Withdraw, Cast a 1-round spell. |
| **Free Action** | Takes negligible time. Can be performed during other actions. | Speak (briefly), Drop an Item, Cast a Quickened Spell. |
| **Swift Action** | Consumes a small amount of effort; limited to one per turn. | Cast a Swift spell (e.g., *Lesser Restoration, Mass*). |
| **Immediate Action** | A swift action that can be performed out-of-turn (e.g., in response to an attack). | Cast *Delay Death* or *Protective Interposition*. Counts as your swift action for the next turn if used out-of-turn. |
| **Not an Action** | A minor activity that requires no action. | 5-foot Step, Delay. |

## 3. Attacks of Opportunity (AoO)
An Attack of Opportunity is a single free melee attack made against an opponent who lets their guard down within a square you threaten.
*   **Threatened Squares:** All squares you can attack with a melee weapon (usually adjacent squares; 10 ft. or more for reach weapons).
*   **Common Triggers:**
    *   Moving out of a threatened square (unless using a 5-ft step or Withdraw action).
    *   Casting a spell (unless casting defensively by passing a Concentration check).
    *   Making a ranged attack or loading a crossbow.
    *   Standing up from prone.
    *   Drinking a potion or retrieving an item.
*   **Limits:** Standard combatants are limited to one AoO per round. The *Combat Reflexes* feat allows additional AoOs equal to the character's Dex modifier.

## 5. Status Conditions

*   **Ability Damaged:** Temporary loss of ability points. Recovers at 1 point per day. At 0 Str/Dex = Helpless/Paralyzed. At 0 Con = Dead. At 0 Int/Wis/Cha = Unconscious.
*   **Ability Drained:** Permanent loss of ability points; only restorable by magic.
*   **Blinded:** Cannot see. Takes a -2 penalty to AC, loses Dex bonus to AC, moves at half speed, and takes a -4 penalty on Search, Spot, and most Str/Dex-based checks. Opponents gain total concealment (50% miss chance).
*   **Confused:** Acts randomly: (01–10) Attack caster; (11–20) Act normally; (21–50) Babble incoherently; (51–70) Flee from caster; (71–100) Attack nearest creature.
*   **Cowering:** Frozen in fear. Cannot take actions. Takes a -2 penalty to AC and loses Dex bonus to AC.
*   **Dazed:** Cannot take actions. No penalty to AC.
*   **Dazzled:** -1 on attack rolls, Search, and Spot checks due to bright light.
*   **Deafened:** Cannot hear. -4 on initiative checks, auto-fails Listen checks, and has a 20% spell failure chance for spells with verbal components.
*   **Disabled:** At exactly 0 HP. Can take only a single Move or Standard action per turn. Strenuous activity deals 1 damage (making you Dying).
*   **Dying:** At -1 to -9 HP. Unconscious. Loses 1 HP per round unless stabilized (10% natural recovery chance, or a DC 15 Heal check).
*   **Entangled:** Moves at half speed, cannot run/charge, takes -2 on attack rolls and -4 to Dex. Casting spells requires a Concentration check (DC 15 + spell level).
*   **Exhausted:** Moves at half speed, takes -6 penalty to Str and Dex. Cured by 1 hour of rest to become Fatigued.
*   **Fatigued:** Cannot run or charge, takes -2 to Str and Dex. Cured by 8 hours of rest.
*   **Flat-Footed:** Has not acted yet in combat. Loses Dex bonus to AC, and cannot make attacks of opportunity.
*   **Frightened:** -2 penalty on attack rolls, saves, and checks. Must flee from the source of fear.
*   **Grappling:** Immobile in a grapple. Loses Dex bonus to AC against non-grappling attackers. Limited to specific grapple actions.
*   **Helpless:** Paralyzed, bound, sleeping, or unconscious. Dex is treated as 0 (-5 modifier). Melee attacks gain +4 attack bonus. Subject to a coup de grace.
*   **Incorporeal:** No physical body. Immune to non-magical attacks. 50% chance to ignore damage from corporeal sources (except force effects). Attacks ignore physical armor.
*   **Invisible:** Visually undetectable. +2 on attacks, opponents lose Dex bonus to AC, and has a 50% miss chance against all attacks.
*   **Nauseated:** Extreme stomach distress. Unable to attack, cast spells, or concentrate. Can only take a single Move action per turn.
*   **Panicked:** Drops everything and flees randomly. Cannot take other actions. -2 on all saves and checks. If cornered, cowers.
*   **Paralyzed:** Str and Dex are treated as 0. Helpless. Can take only purely mental actions.
*   **Petrified:** Turned to stone. Treated as unconscious and helpless.
*   **Pinned:** Immobile in a grapple (held down). Cannot move or attack, loses Dex to AC, and is flat-footed.
*   **Prone:** Lying down. Takes -4 penalty on melee attacks. Gains +4 AC vs. ranged attacks, but takes -4 AC vs. melee attacks. Standing up is a Move action that provokes an AoO.
*   **Shaken:** Vexed by fear. Takes -2 penalty on attack rolls, saves, skill checks, and ability checks.
*   **Sickened:** -2 penalty on all attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, saves, skill checks, and ability checks.
*   **Staggered:** Nonlethal damage equals current HP (or when recovering from dying). Can take only a single Move or Standard action per turn.
*   **Stunned:** Drops held items, can't take actions, takes a -2 penalty to AC, and loses Dex bonus to AC.
*   **Unconscious:** At -1 to -9 HP, or when nonlethal damage exceeds current HP. Helpless.
