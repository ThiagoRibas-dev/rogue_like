# Mounted Combat
## Category
Combat Basics

## Summary
Riding a mount into battle grants tactical advantages, such as increased speed, higher ground bonuses, and a shield in the form of your mount. However, combat while mounted requires coordination and skill (using the Ride skill).

## Mechanics

### 1. General Mounted Rules
*   **Initiative:** Your mount acts on your initiative count as you direct it. You move at its speed, but the mount uses its actions to move.
*   **Space:** You share your mount's space completely. If multiple riders are on the same mount, all riders share its space.
*   **Controlling Your Mount:**
    *   **War-Trained Mounts:** (e.g., warhorses, warponies, griffons) Do not require checks to control in battle. You can direct them to attack as a free action; if you do so, you can still make your own attacks normally.
    *   **Nonwar Mounts:** (e.g., standard riding horses or ponies) Are frightened by combat. Each round you remain mounted in battle, you must make a **DC 20 Ride check** as a move action to control them. If you succeed, you can take a standard action. If you fail, the move action becomes a full-round action, and you can take no other actions that turn. See [use-skill.md](./use-skill.md).

### 2. Melee Combat while Mounted
*   **Higher Ground:** If your target is on foot and smaller than your mount, you gain a **+1 bonus** on melee attack rolls.
*   **Melee Attacks & Movement:** If your mount moves more than 5 feet, you can only make a **single melee attack** on your turn (you must wait until the mount reaches the target). You cannot make a melee full attack.
*   **Charging:** If your mount charges, you also take the AC penalty associated with a charge ($-2$). Your attack roll at the end of the charge receives the $+2$ charge bonus.
    *   *Lances:* Wielding a lance while mounted during a charge deals **double damage** (triple damage if you possess the Spirited Charge feat). See [charge.md](./charge.md).

### 3. Ranged Combat while Mounted
Unlike melee, you can make a full attack with a ranged weapon while your mount is moving, and you can take your own move actions normally (e.g., loading a crossbow).
*   **Ranged Attack Penalties:**
    *   *Mount moves up to its speed:* **No penalty**.
    *   *Mount takes a double move:* **-4 penalty** on attack rolls.
    *   *Mount is running (quadruple speed):* **-8 penalty** on attack rolls.
*   **Attack Timing:** For a double move or running mount, you make your attack rolls when the mount has completed half its movement, measuring range from that midpoint.

### 4. Casting Spells while Mounted
*   **Normal Cast:** You can cast spells normally if your mount moves up to its speed either before or after you cast.
*   **Casting while Moving (Double Move):** If your mount moves both before and after you cast, you must make a **Concentration check (DC 10 + spell level)** due to the vigorous motion, or lose the spell. See [cast-a-spell.md](./cast-a-spell.md).
*   **Casting while Running:** If your mount is running, you must make a **Concentration check (DC 15 + spell level)** due to the violent motion, or lose the spell.

### 5. Overrunning while Mounted
*   **Strength Check:** Your mount makes the opposed Strength check to determine the success or failure of the overrun, applying its size modifier rather than yours.
*   **Trample Attack:** If your mount knocks the defender prone during the overrun, it can make a single melee attack against the prone target using its front hooves/natural weapons (if it has any). This attack cannot be a special attack. See [overrun.md](./overrun.md).

## Ride Skill Maneuvers in Combat
These maneuvers require Ride checks. See [use-skill.md](./use-skill.md) for more details.

*   **Using Mount as Cover (No Action):** You can drop down alongside your mount to gain cover (+4 AC, +2 Reflex).
    *   *Check:* Make a Ride check before the enemy's attack. If successful, you gain cover against that opponent (or all opponents on the same initiative).
    *   *Restrictions:* You cannot use your mount as cover while attacking, casting spells, or charging.
*   **Guide with Knees (No Action):** Guide your mount without hands.
    *   *Check:* **DC 5 Ride check** at the start of your turn. Success allows you to use both hands to fight (wielding a shield, two-handed weapon, or two weapons). Failure means you must use one hand to control the mount.
*   **Leap (No Action):** Force your mount to jump an obstacle.
    *   *Check:* Roll against the jump DC using your Ride modifier or the mount's Jump modifier (whichever is lower). Failure means you fall off and take at least 1d6 falling damage.
*   **Stay in Saddle (No Action):** Avoid falling when your mount bolts/rears or when you take damage (DC 5).
    *   *Rider Unconscious:* If you are knocked unconscious, you have a **50% chance** to stay in the saddle (**75%** if using a military saddle). Otherwise, you fall and take 1d6 damage.
*   **Soft Fall (No Action):** Avoid damage if your mount falls.
    *   *Check:* **DC 15 Ride check**. Success means you take no damage. Failure means you take 1d6 falling damage.
*   **Spur Mount (Move Action):** Increase your mount's speed.
    *   *Check:* **DC 15 Ride check**. Success increases the mount's speed by +10 feet for 1 round, but deals 1 point of damage to the mount. Each consecutive round of spurring deals double the damage of the previous round (1, 2, 4, 8, 16...).
*   **Swim while Mounted (No Action):**
    *   *Check:* Make a Ride check each round your mount swims. **DC 10** (calm water), **DC 15** (rough water/underwater), or **DC 20** (stormy water). Failure halves your mount's swim speed and gives it a -5 Swim penalty. Failing by 5 or more unsaddles you.
*   **Fast Mount or Dismount (Free Action):**
    *   *Check:* **DC 20 Ride check** (requires a move action available this round). Failure means mounting/dismounting remains a move action.
