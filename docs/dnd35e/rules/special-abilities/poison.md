# Poison

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes how characters can be poisoned and the effects of various poisons.

## Mechanics
When a character is poisoned, they must make a Fortitude saving throw.
- **Failure:** Takes the poison’s initial damage (usually ability damage).
- **Success:** Avoids initial damage, but typically faces more damage 1 minute later, which can also be avoided with a successful Fortitude saving throw.
- **Dose:** One dose affects a single target. A poisoned weapon or object retains venom until it scores a hit or is touched.
- **Ability Type:** Poisonous effects are almost always extraordinary, though supernatural and spell-like poisons are possible.

### Types of Poisons
Poisons are divided into four basic types by delivery method:
- **Contact:** Touching the poison necessitates a saving throw. Can be delivered via weapon or touch attack. Can affect creatures even if they have sufficient damage reduction to avoid damage from the attack.
- **Ingested:** Used outside of combat (e.g., administering to an unconscious creature, duping someone).
- **Inhaled:** Usually in fragile vials. Thrown as a ranged attack (range increment 10 ft.). One dose fills a 10-foot cube. Each creature in the area must save. Holding breath is ineffective.
- **Injury:** Delivered through a wound. If a creature has sufficient damage reduction to avoid damage from the attack, the poison does not affect it.

### Perils of Using Poison
- 5% chance of exposing oneself when applying poison to a weapon or readying it.
- Natural 1 on an attack roll with a poisoned weapon requires a DC 15 Reflex save or accidental self-poisoning.

### Poison Immunities
- Creatures with natural poison attacks are immune to their own poison.
- Nonliving creatures (constructs, undead) and creatures without metabolisms (elementals) are always immune.
- Oozes, plants, and certain outsiders are also immune, though special poisons could harm them.
