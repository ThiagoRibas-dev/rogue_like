# Scent

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
An extraordinary ability to detect approaching enemies, sniff out hidden foes, and track by sense of smell.

## Mechanics
- **Range:** Detect opponents by smell, generally within 30 feet. Upwind, the range is 60 feet; downwind, 15 feet.
- **Strong Scents:** Strong scents (smoke, rotting garbage) can be detected at twice the normal ranges.
- **Overpowering Scents:** Overpowering scents (skunk musk, troglodyte stench) can be detected at three times these ranges.
- **Detection:** The creature detects another creature’s presence but not its specific location. Noting the direction of the scent is a move action. If it moves within 5 feet of the scent’s source, the creature can pinpoint that source.
- **Tracking:** A creature with the Track feat and the scent ability can follow tracks by smell, making a Wisdom check to find or follow a track (typical DC 10 for a fresh trail). The DC increases by 2 for each hour the trail is cold. The ability otherwise follows the rules for the Track feat. Creatures tracking by scent ignore the effects of surface conditions and poor visibility.
- **Identification:** Creatures with the scent ability can identify familiar odors just as humans do familiar sights.
- **Water:** Water ruins a trail for air-breathing creatures. Water-breathing creatures with scent can use it in the water easily.
- **Masking:** False, powerful odors can mask other scents, spoiling the ability to detect or identify creatures (base Survival DC to track becomes 20).
