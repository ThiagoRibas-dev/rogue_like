# Level Loss

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes the effects of losing a character level.

## Mechanics
- **Hit Die:** A character who loses a level instantly loses one Hit Die.
- **Reduced Abilities:** The character’s base attack bonus, base saving throw bonuses, and special class abilities are reduced to the new, lower level.
- **Lost Gains:** The character loses any ability score gain, skill ranks, and any feat associated with the level. If the exact ability score or skill ranks are unknown, lose 1 point from the highest ability score or ranks from the highest-ranked skills.
- **Companions:** If a familiar or companion creature has abilities tied to a character who has lost a level, the creature’s abilities are adjusted to fit the character’s new level.
- **Experience Points:** The victim’s experience point total is immediately set to the midpoint of the previous level.
