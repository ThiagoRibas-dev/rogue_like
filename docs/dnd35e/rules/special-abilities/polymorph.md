# Polymorph

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes how magic can cause creatures and characters to change their shapes.

## Mechanics
- **Mind:** Polymorphed creatures retain their own minds but have new physical forms.
- **Slaying/Bane Weapons:** Slaying or bane weapons designed for a specific creature type still affect those creatures even if polymorphed. A creature polymorphed into a different type is not subject to slaying/bane effects directed at that type.
- **Ranger's Favored Enemy:** A ranger's favored enemy bonus is denied if the foe polymorphs into another form.
- **Dwarf's Bonus:** A dwarf's bonus for fighting giants is based on shape and size; they do not gain a bonus against a giant polymorphed into something else, but do gain the bonus against any creature polymorphed into a giant.
