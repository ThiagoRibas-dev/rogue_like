# Psionics

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes special mental abilities possessed by various creatures, generated from the power of the mind.

## Mechanics
- **Ability Type:** These are spell-like abilities.
- **Source:** Generated from the power of the mind alone; no other outside magical force or ritual is needed.
- **Saving Throws:** Psionic attacks almost always allow Will saving throws to resist them.
- **Variety:** Not all psionic attacks are mental attacks. Some abilities allow reshaping the body, healing wounds, or teleportation. Some psionic creatures can see into the future, past, and present (in far-off locales) and read minds.
