# Gaseous Form

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
A supernatural or spell-like ability to take the form of a cloud of vapor or gas.

## Mechanics
- **Movement:** Can't run, but can fly. Can move through cracks but not solid matter.
- **Actions:** Can't attack physically or cast spells with verbal, somatic, material, or focus components. Lose supernatural abilities (except the ability to assume gaseous form).
- **Defense:** Gain damage reduction 10/magic. Spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities affect them normally. Lose all benefit of material armor (including natural armor), though size, Dexterity, deflection, and force armor bonuses still apply.
- **Immunities:** Do not need to breathe and are immune to attacks involving breathing (e.g., poison gas).
- **Environment:** Can't enter water or other liquids. Affected by winds, but cannot be dispersed or damaged by them.
- **Detection:** Discerning a creature in gaseous form from natural mist requires a DC 15 Spot check. Creatures in gaseous form attempting to hide in mist, smoke, or other gas gain a +20 bonus.

## Special Notes
Creatures in gaseous form are not ethereal or incorporeal.
