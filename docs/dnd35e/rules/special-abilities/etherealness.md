# Etherealness

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes the state of being on the Ethereal Plane, where creatures are invisible, inaudible, insubstantial, and scentless to those on the Material Plane.

## Mechanics
- **Interaction with Material Plane:** Ethereal creatures are invisible, inaudible, insubstantial, and scentless to creatures on the Material Plane. They can see and hear into the Material Plane in a 60-foot radius, though material objects block sight and sound. They cannot affect the Material Plane, not even magically.
- **Interaction with Ethereal Plane:** Ethereal creatures interact with other ethereal creatures and objects normally.
- **Force Effects:** Force effects originating on the Material Plane extend onto the Ethereal Plane (e.g., `wall of force`, `magic missile`). Gaze effects and abjurations also extend from the Material to the Ethereal Plane.
- **Movement:** Ethereal creatures move in any direction at will and do not need to walk on the ground. Material objects don’t block them. They do not fall or take falling damage.
- **Water:** Ethereal creatures pass through and operate in water as easily as air.

## Special Notes
- `See invisibility` and `true seeing` reveal ethereal creatures.
- Ghosts have a power called manifestation that allows them to appear on the Material Plane as incorporeal creatures, but they are still on the Ethereal Plane.
