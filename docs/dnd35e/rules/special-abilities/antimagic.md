# Antimagic

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
An antimagic field spell or effect cancels magic altogether within its area.

## Mechanics
- **No Magic:** No supernatural ability, spell-like ability, or spell works in an area of antimagic. Extraordinary abilities still function.
- **Suppression, Not Dispelling:** Antimagic suppresses magic, it does not dispel it. Once the effect is no longer in the area, the magic returns.
- **Spell Areas:** If a spell's area includes both an antimagic area and a normal area, it functions in the normal area. If the spell's center is in the antimagic area, the spell is suppressed.
- **Creatures:** Golems, elementals, outsiders, and corporeal undead still function in an antimagic area, but their magical abilities are suppressed. Summoned or conjured creatures of any type, as well as incorporeal undead, wink out if they enter an antimagic area and reappear when the field is gone.
- **Magic Items:** Continuous effects of magic items do not function in an antimagic area, but their effects are not canceled.
- **Stacking:** Two antimagic areas do not stack or cancel each other.

## Special Notes
- `Wall of force`, `prismatic wall`, and `prismatic sphere` are not affected by antimagic.
- `Break enchantment`, `dispel magic`, and `greater dispel magic` do not dispel antimagic.
- `Mage’s disjunction` has a 1% chance per caster level of destroying an antimagic field.
