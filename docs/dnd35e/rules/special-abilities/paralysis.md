# Paralysis

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Immobilizes victims through magical means (or toxins, discussed in Poison).

## Mechanics
- **Movement & Actions:** A paralyzed character cannot move, speak, or take any physical action. They are rooted to the spot, frozen and helpless.
- **Mental Actions:** May take purely mental actions, such as casting a spell with no components.
- **Flying/Swimming:** A winged creature flying in the air that becomes paralyzed falls. A swimmer cannot swim and may drown.
