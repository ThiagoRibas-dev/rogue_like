# Breath Weapon

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes a creature's ability to expel something from its mouth as an attack.

## Mechanics
- **Action:** Typically a standard action.
- **Attack Roll:** No attack roll is necessary. The breath fills its stated area.
- **Saving Throw:** Any character caught in the area must make the appropriate saving throw or suffer the full effect. A successful save may result in half damage or a reduced effect.
- **Ability Type:** Breath weapons are supernatural abilities except where noted.
- **Immunity:** Creatures are immune to their own breath weapons.
- **Breathing:** Creatures unable to breathe can still use breath weapons.
