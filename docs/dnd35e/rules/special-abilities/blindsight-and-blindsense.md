# Blindsight and Blindsense

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Describes extraordinary abilities that allow creatures to perceive without vision.

## Mechanics
### Blindsight
- **Definition:** Extraordinary ability to use a nonvisual sense (or combination) to operate effectively without vision.
- **Irrelevance:** Makes invisibility and concealment (even magical darkness) irrelevant.
- **Limitations:** Never allows distinguishing color or visual contrast; cannot read; does not subject to gaze attacks.
- **Penalties:** Blinding attacks do not penalize. Deafening attacks thwart if reliant on hearing.
- **Environment:** Works underwater but not in a vacuum.
- **Effects:** Negates displacement and blur effects.

### Blindsense
- **Definition:** Lesser ability to notice things without seeing, but without blindsight's precision.
- **Checks:** Usually does not need Spot or Listen checks to notice and locate creatures within range, provided line of effect.
- **Concealment:** Opponents the creature cannot see have total concealment (50% miss chance).
- **Movement:** Visibility still affects movement.
- **Dexterity Bonus:** Still denied Dexterity bonus to AC against attacks from unseen creatures.
