# Darkvision

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
The extraordinary ability to see with no light source at all.

## Mechanics
- **Vision:** See with no light source, out to a specified range.
- **Color:** Black and white only (colors cannot be discerned).
- **Limitations:** Does not allow seeing invisible objects or through illusions. Subjects a creature to gaze attacks normally.
- **Light:** The presence of light does not spoil darkvision.
