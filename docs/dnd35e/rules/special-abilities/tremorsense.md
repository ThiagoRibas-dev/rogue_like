# Tremorsense

## Category
Special Ability

## Summary
Automatically senses the location of anything in contact with the ground and within range.

## Mechanics
- **Detection:** Automatically senses the location of anything that is in contact with the ground and within range.
- **Path:** If no straight path exists through the ground from the creature to those it’s sensing, the range defines the maximum distance of the shortest indirect path.
- **Conditions:** Must itself be in contact with the ground, and the creatures must be moving. Creatures taking physical actions (including casting spells with somatic components) are considered moving.
