# Skill: Profession
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Profession skill represents training in a mundane trade or vocation, allowing you to earn a living doing a specific type of work. Unlike Craft (which produces items), Profession covers service-based or ongoing occupations.

*   **Key Ability:** Wisdom (Wis)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

Profession is divided into many subcategories, each treated as a separate skill. Examples include:

*   Profession (Apothecary), Profession (Architect), Profession (Brewer), Profession (Butcher), Profession (Cook), Profession (Driver), Profession (Engineer), Profession (Farmer), Profession (Fisherman), Profession (Gambler), Profession (Herbalist), Profession (Innkeeper), Profession (Merchant), Profession (Midwife), Profession (Miller), Profession (Miner), Profession (Porter), Profession (Sailor), Profession (Scribe), Profession (Shepherd), Profession (Soldier), Profession (Tanner), Profession (Teamster), Profession (Woodcutter).

---

## Mechanics

### Earning Income
During downtime, you can use Profession to earn money through a week's work.

*   **Action:** **1 week** of labor.
*   **Income:** A successful check earns you **half your check result in gold pieces** for the week.
    *   *Example:* A Profession (Sailor) check result of 22 earns 11 gp for the week.

### Taking 10 / Taking 20
*   You can **Take 10** on a Profession check when not in a dangerous or rushed situation (the normal case for routine work).
*   **Taking 20** is allowed but requires 20× the normal time (20 weeks of focused effort for weekly income).

---

## Practical Applications
Beyond earning income, high Profession ranks represent genuine expertise:

*   **Profession (Sailor):** Navigate ships, know weather signs, manage rigging. Provides a **+2 circumstance bonus** on Survival checks at sea.
*   **Profession (Soldier):** Tactical knowledge of military formations. Provides a **+2 circumstance bonus** on initiative checks when acting in a coordinated group.
*   **Profession (Herbalist):** Recognize plants and their properties. Provides a **+2 circumstance bonus** on Survival checks to find food or identify plants.
*   **Profession (Gambler):** Read opponents and calculate odds. Provides a **+2 circumstance bonus** on Sense Motive checks against opponents in games of chance.

> [!NOTE]
> The DM may allow additional circumstance bonuses based on relevant Profession ranks. Discuss with your DM what practical applications your specific subcategory might have.
