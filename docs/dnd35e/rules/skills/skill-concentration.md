# Skill: Concentration
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Concentration skill represents your ability to focus your mind and block out distractions. It is crucial for spellcasters attempting to cast spells or maintain effects under pressure.

*   **Key Ability:** Constitution (Con)
    *   *Exception:* Creatures lacking a Constitution score (such as undead or constructs) use **Charisma** for Concentration checks (see [nonabilities.md](./nonabilities.md)).
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

You must make a Concentration check whenever you are distracted while performing an activity that requires your full attention. Such activities include:
1.  Casting a spell or manifesting a psionic power.
2.  Concentrating to maintain an active spell or power.
3.  Directing a spell or power's effect.
4.  Using a spell-like or psi-like ability.
5.  Using a skill that provokes attacks of opportunity.

### Distraction DC Table

When a distraction occurs, the base DC is modified by the **spell or power level** being cast, maintained, or directed (if applicable):

| Distraction Type | DC | Detail / Example |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Casting/Manifesting Defensively** | 15 + spell/power level | Actively dodging attacks while casting/manifesting. |
| **Injury / Damage** | 10 + damage taken + spell/power level | Damage taken from an attack of opportunity or readied action. |
| **Continuous Damage** | 10 + 1/2 damage last dealt + spell/power level | Ongoing damage such as from *Melf's acid arrow* or fire. |
| **Distracting Spell or Power** | Save DC + spell/power level | Being affected by a spell like *gust of wind* or a nondamaging power. |
| **Entangled** | 15 + spell/power level | Entangled by a net, rope, or spell (e.g., *entangle*). |
| **Grappling / Pinned** | 20 + spell/power level | Wrestled by an opponent (somatic spells are prohibited; see [grapple.md](./grapple.md)). |
| **Vigorous Motion** | 10 + spell/power level | Riding a moving mount, bouncy wagon, or small boat. |
| **Violent Motion** | 15 + spell/power level | Riding a galloping horse, rough carriage, or storm-tossed boat. |
| **Extraordinarily Violent** | 20 + spell/power level | Extreme motion, such as during an earthquake. |
| **Bad Weather** | 10 + spell/power level | High wind carrying blinding rain or sleet. |
| **Severe Weather** | 15 + spell/power level | Wind-driven hail, dust, or heavy flying debris. |
| **Manifest without Display** | 15 + power level | Psionics only. Attempting to hide a power's sensory display. |

*Note: If no spell or power is involved (e.g., making a skill check under distraction), the spell/power level is $+0$.*

---

## Actions

### Casting / Acting Defensively
To avoid provoking attacks of opportunity during combat, you can choose to cast or manifest defensively:
*   **Action:** Part of the casting, manifesting, or activation action.
*   **Resolution:** Make a Concentration check against **DC 15 + spell/power level**.
*   **Success:** You cast the spell or use the ability normally without provoking any attacks of opportunity.
*   **Failure:** You fail to cast the spell or manifest the power, and the slot, preparation, or power points are wasted.

### Maintaining or Directing
*   **Maintaining:** Concentrating to keep a spell or power active is a **Standard Action** (see [spellcasting-mechanics.md](./spellcasting-mechanics.md)).
*   **Directing:** Redirecting an active spell or power is a **Move Action** (see [actions-in-combat.md](./actions-in-combat.md)).

### Gaining Psionic Focus
If you have 1 or more power points available, you can meditate to become psionically focused.
*   **Action:** **Full-Round Action** (provokes attacks of opportunity).
*   **DC:** **20**.
*   **Effect:** You become psionically focused. You remain focused until you expend it, become unconscious, go to sleep, or your power point reserve drops to 0.
*   **Expending Focus:** When focused, you can expend your focus on any single Concentration check to treat the roll as a **15** (like taking 10, but adding 15). You can also expend it to activate many psionic feats.

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You block out the distraction and complete or maintain the activity normally.
*   **Failure:** The activity fails immediately, wasting the action and resources:
    *   *Spells/Powers:* The spell is lost from memory/slot, or power points are wasted.
    *   *Active Spells/Powers:* The effect ends immediately.
    *   *Directing:* The redirect fails, but the effect continues in its previous state.
    *   *Spell/Psi-Like Abilities:* The daily use of the ability is lost.
    *   *Skills:* The skill check fails.

---

## Synergy and Feats

*   **Combat Casting / Combat Manifestation:** Grants a **+4 bonus** on Concentration checks made to cast/manifest defensively or while grappled/pinned.
*   **5+ ranks in Concentration:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Autohypnosis checks.
