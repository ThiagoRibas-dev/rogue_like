# Skill: Survival
## Category
Skill Rules (St-Z) & Cross-Referencing

## Overview
The Survival skill represents your ability to guide travelers through wilderness, find food and water, avoid getting lost, track creatures, and predict or endure extreme weather conditions.

*   **Key Ability:** Wisdom (Wis)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### 1. Foraging and Hunting (Food and Water)
You can sustain yourself and others in wild environments.
*   **Action:** Moved at **half speed** during overland travel.
*   **DC:** **10** (increases by **+5** for inhospitable lands like deserts, and **+10** for barren lands like open ocean for non-aquatic creatures).
*   **Success:** You provide enough food and water for yourself for one day.
*   **Extra Success:** You can support **one additional creature** for every **2 points** by which your check result exceeds the DC.

### 2. Predict Weather
*   **DC:** **15**.
*   **Success:** You accurately predict the weather for the next **24 hours**.
*   **Extra Success:** For every **5 points** by which your check result exceeds 15, you can predict the weather an additional 24 hours into the future.

### 3. Weather Protection
*   **DC:** **15**.
*   **Success:** Grants you and one ally a **+2 Fortitude save bonus** against severe weather while moving up to half your overland speed, or a **+4 bonus** if remaining stationary.
*   **Extra Success:** You can grant this bonus to **one additional creature** for every **1 point** by which your check result exceeds 15.

### 4. Trailblazing (Overland Movement)
You can guide others through difficult terrain (see [dungeon-movement.md](./dungeon-movement.md)).
*   **DC:** **15** (increases to **25** for extreme terrain).
*   **Success:** You improve the terrain movement modifier by **+1/4** (DC 15) or **+1/2** (DC 25), up to a maximum multiplier of ×1.
*   **Capacity:** A trailblazer can guide up to **4 other creatures** without penalty. For every 3 additional creatures, the trailblazer takes a **-2 penalty** on the check.

## Getting Lost and Navigation

If traveling without a clear road or path, a character can get lost:
*   **Secret Check:** The DM rolls a Survival check once per hour of travel. A character with 5+ ranks in Knowledge (geography) or Knowledge (local) gets a **+2 bonus**.

| Terrain Type | Survival DC (Avoid Getting Lost) |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Moor / Plains** | 6 |
| **Forest (Sparse) / Hills** | 10 |
| **Mountain / Swamp / Forest (Dense)** | 15 |
| **Bad visibility** (fog, snow, night) | +5 to DC |
| **Has a map of the area** | -4 to DC |

### Finding North
*   **DC:** **15** (requires 5+ ranks in Survival). Allows you to determine which direction is north.

### Dealing with Being Lost
*   **Recognize Lost:** A lost character can attempt a **DC 20 Survival check** (-1 per hour of random travel) to realize they are lost.
*   **Correcting Direction:** Once lost, regaining your bearings requires a **DC 15 Survival check** (+2 per hour of random travel). Failure means you choose a random direction.

## Tracking (Trained Only / Track Feat)
*   **Track Feat:** You must have the *Track* feat to follow tracks if the DC is higher than 10.
*   **Action:** Finding tracks or following them for 1 mile is a Survival check.
*   **Retry:** If you fail, you can try again after **1 hour** (outdoors) or **10 minutes** (indoors).
*   **Scent Tracking:** Creatures with the *Scent* ability can track by smell (Wisdom or Survival check; see [senses-and-perception.md](./senses-and-perception.md)).

### Base Tracking DCs by Surface

| Surface | Survival DC |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Very Soft Ground** (snow, mud) | 5 |
| **Soft Ground** (dirt, grass) | 10 |
| **Hard Ground** (rock, cobblestone) | 20 |

### Track Modifiers

| Condition | DC Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Every 3 creatures in the group being tracked** | -1 |
| **Every 24 hours since tracks were made** | +1 |
| **Rain or snow fell since tracks were made** | +10 |
| **Tracked creature is hiding its trail** | +5 (or opposed Hide) |

### Identifying Quarry Details
If you have the *Track* feat and succeed on your tracking check:
*   **Success:** You follow the trail and gain basic info (size, foot type).
*   **Exceed by 5+:** You learn creature type, specific species (for animals), and subtler clues (encumbrance, fettered state).
