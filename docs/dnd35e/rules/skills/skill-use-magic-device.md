# Skill: Use Magic Device
## Category
Skill Rules (St-Z) & Cross-Referencing

## Overview
The Use Magic Device (UMD) skill represents your ability to activate magic items, read magical scrolls, and bypass restrictions (such as class, race, alignment, or ability score requirements) that normally limit an item's use.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Restrictions
*   **No Taking 10:** You cannot Take 10 on a Use Magic Device check.
*   **No Aid Another:** Other characters cannot use the Aid Another action to help you make a Use Magic Device check.
*   **Natural 1 Lockout:** If you roll a **natural 1** on your check and the check fails, you cannot attempt to activate that specific item again for **24 hours**.

### Actions and Timing
*   **Activation Check:** Made as part of the action required to activate the item (e.g., standard action for a wand, spell trigger, or command word).
*   **Ongoing Emulation:** If you are emulating an alignment or other quality for an ongoing item effect (like wearing a restricted ring), you must check **once per hour**.

| Task | DC | Notes / Modifiers |

| **Use Wand / Spell Trigger** | 20 | Activate wands, staffs, or other trigger items. |
| **Use Scroll / Spell Completion** | 20 + Caster Level | Decipher first. Also requires ability score emulation if your score is too low. |
| **Emulate Class Feature** | 20 | Effective level in the class equals your check result - 20. |
| **Emulate Ability Score** | — | Effective ability score equals your check result - 15. |
| **Emulate Race** | 25 | Emulate one race at a time (e.g., Elf-only items). |
| **Emulate Alignment** | 30 | Emulate one alignment at a time. |
| **Decipher Written Spell** | 25 + Spell Level | Requires 1 minute of concentration (Spellcraft DC + 5). |
| **Activate Blindly** | 25 | +2 bonus if you have successfully activated it before. |

## Advanced Applications

### 1. Activating Blindly
If you do not know the activation word, thought, or deed for a magic item, you can attempt to force it to activate anyway:
*   **Action:** Standard action.
*   **DC:** **25**.
*   **Failure by 9 or less:** The item does not activate, but nothing else happens.
*   **Failure by 10 or more (Mishap):** Magical energy is released uncontrollably.
    *   *Mishap Effect:* The item targets the wrong creature, or raw magical energy is released, dealing **2d6 points of damage** to you.

### 2. Emulating Ability Scores (Scroll Casting)
To cast a spell from a scroll, you must have an ability score of at least **10 + the spell's level** in the appropriate score (Intelligence for Wizard, Wisdom for Cleric, Charisma for Sorcerer).
*   **Effective Score:** If your score is too low, you emulate it. Your effective score equals your **UMD check result - 15**.
*   *Example:* To cast a 5th-level spell (requires score of 15), you need a UMD check result of at least 30.

## Synergy Bonuses
*   **5+ ranks in Decipher Script:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on UMD checks involving scrolls (see [skill-decipher-script.md](./skill-decipher-script.md)).
*   **5+ ranks in Spellcraft:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on UMD checks involving scrolls (see [skill-spellcraft.md](./skill-spellcraft.md)).
*   **5+ ranks in Use Magic Device:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Spellcraft checks to decipher scrolls.
