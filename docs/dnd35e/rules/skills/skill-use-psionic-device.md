# Skill: Use Psionic Device
## Category
Psionic Skills

## Overview
Use this skill to activate psionic devices—including power stones (crystals storing specific powers) and dorjes (wands charged with powers)—that you normally could not activate due to class, alignment, ability score, or race restrictions.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Restrictions
*   **No Taking 10:** You cannot Take 10 on this skill.
*   **No Aid Another:** You cannot aid another on Use Psionic Device checks; only the user of the item can attempt the check.
*   **Natural 1 Lockout:** If you roll a **natural 1** on an activation attempt and fail, you cannot try to activate that specific item again for **1 day (24 hours)**.

### Emulation Choices
You must consciously choose what to emulate and know what you are trying to emulate when making the check. Ongoing emulations must be re-checked **once per hour**.

## Advanced Applications

### 1. Activate Blindly (and Brainburn)
If you don't know the specific thought or activation method, you can attempt to force the item to activate:
*   **Action:** Requires waving the item or attempting an equivalent activation action.
*   **Success:** The item activates normally.
*   **Failure by 10 or more (Brainburn):** The item fails to activate and you suffer **brainburn**, taking **1d4 points of damage per power level**. (This damage is *in addition* to standard power stone brainburn if applicable).

### 2. Using a Power Stone (Power Completion)
Manifesting a power from a power stone has several requirements:
*   **Address It First:** You must address the stone before using it.
*   **Minimum Ability Score:** You must have a score of at least **10 + the power's level** in the relevant manifesting ability score, or emulate it (Effective Score = Check Result - 15).

## Synergy and Feats

*   **Psionic Affinity Feat:** Grants a **+2 bonus** on Use Psionic Device checks.
*   **5+ ranks in Psicraft:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Use Psionic Device checks related to power stones.
*   **5+ ranks in Use Psionic Device:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Psicraft checks to address power stones.
