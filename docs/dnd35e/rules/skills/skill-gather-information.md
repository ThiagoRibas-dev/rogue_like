# Skill: Gather Information
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Gather Information skill represents your ability to scout rumors, learn local gossip, find out who holds real power in a town, or track down leads by talking to locals.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Time and Cost
*   **Time:** Gathering information requires **1d4+1 hours** of mingling, drinking, and chatting in taverns, markets, or local hubs.
*   **Expenses:** Typically requires spending **1d10 gp** (or more in exclusive areas) on drinks, minor bribes, and tips to grease wheels.

### DCs by Information Type

The check DC depends on how closely guarded or obscure the information is:

| Information Classification | DC | Description / Examples |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **General** | 10 | Local rumors, common knowledge, public figures, directions. |
| **Private** | 15 | Town gossip, private clubs, minor military figures, local politics. |
| **Exclusive** | 20 | High-society scandals, invitation-only clubs, prominent local criminals. |
| **Secret** | 25 | Espionage rings, criminal cartels, hidden guild locations, lost locations. |
| **Unknown** | 30 | Shadow governments, underground cults, ancient forgotten secrets. |
| **Specific Target** | +5 | Applying the search to a highly specific event or target (e.g., "who robbed the vault last night"). |

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You learn the details you were looking for. Higher rolls might reveal extra rumors or context.
*   **Failure:** You learn nothing useful. You can attempt the check again in a different district or by targeting different contacts.

---

## Special Risks
*   **Drawing Attention:** Repeatedly asking questions about a specific secret, group, or person will draw attention. The target or their allies will become aware that someone is asking questions, potentially leading to ambushes, warnings, or shifts in NPC attitudes toward unfriendly/hostile.
