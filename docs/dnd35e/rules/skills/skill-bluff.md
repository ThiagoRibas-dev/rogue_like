# Skill: Bluff
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Bluff skill represents your ability to mislead, con, misdirect, lie, or send secret messages.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

A Bluff check is opposed by the target's **Sense Motive** check (see [skill-sense-motive.md](./skill-sense-motive.md)).

### Believability Modifiers
The target's Sense Motive check is modified based on how believable the lie is and what it demands of the target:

| Bluff Believability | Sense Motive Modifier | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Believable** | +0 | "I dropped my coin over there." |
| **Puts target at risk** | +5 | "Go check that dark alley for me." |
| **Hard to believe / Great risk** | +10 | "The king wants you to give me your sword." |
| **Almost too incredible to consider** | +20 | "I am actually the king's long-lost uncle." |

### Retries
*   **Failure:** A failed Bluff check makes the target suspicious. You cannot try the same or similar bluff again in the same circumstances.

---

## Applications

### 1. General Social Bluffs
*   **Action:** At least a **Full-Round Action** (longer for elaborate schemes).
*   **Requirements:** Requires direct interaction between you and the target. Unaware creatures cannot be bluffed.
*   **Distinguishing Failure types:**
    *   If the target wins the opposed check by **10 or less** because of a risk modifier (+10 or +20), the target did not necessarily see through the lie; they simply proved too reluctant to go along with the risk.
    *   If the target wins by **11 or more**, they have seen through the bluff entirely.

### 2. Feinting in Combat
You can mislead an opponent to bypass their defenses.
*   **Action:** **Standard Action** (no AoO). Reduced to a **Move Action** with the *Improved Feint* feat.
*   **Resolution:** Opposed check: your **Bluff** vs. the target's **Sense Motive** check.
    *   *Target Modifier:* The target adds its **Base Attack Bonus** (BAB) to its Sense Motive check.
    *   *Nonhumanoid Penalty:* You take a **-4 penalty** if feinting a nonhumanoid, and a **-8 penalty** if feinting a creature of animal intelligence (Int 1 or 2). You cannot feint mindless creatures.
*   **Success:** The target is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against your next melee attack. This attack must be made on or before your next turn.

### 3. Delivering Secret Messages
You can pass hidden information to an ally in plain sight.
*   **Action:** None (part of communicating).
*   **Resolution:** Made secretly by the DM. The DC is **15** for simple messages, or **20** for complex messages.
*   **Outcomes:**
    *   *Success:* The ally understands the message.
    *   *Failure by 4 or less:* The message is not understood.
    *   *Failure by 5 or more:* False or misleading information is inferred by the ally or intercepting observers.
    *   *Interception:* Anyone listening can attempt an opposed Sense Motive check to intercept and understand the message (see [skill-sense-motive.md](./skill-sense-motive.md)).

### 4. Heckling Performers
You can disrupt a performer's focus.
*   **Action:** 1 minute.
*   **Resolution:** Opposed check: your **Bluff** vs. the performer's **Concentration** check.
*   **Outcomes:**
    *   *Success:* The performer must make a new Perform check at a **-2 penalty** (plus another -2 for every 5 points by which you won the opposed check).
    *   *Failure:* The audience sides with the performer; the performer gains a **+2 circumstance bonus** on their check, and the audience may become unfriendly to you.
    *   *Limit:* You can heckle only once per performance.

### 5. Looking Innocuous
*   **Action:** Part of movement/stealth.
*   **Resolution:** If spotted tailing someone, make a Bluff check opposed by their Sense Motive check.
    *   *Success:* They see you but assume you are harmless.
    *   *Failure:* They realize you are tailing them.
