# Skill: Swim
## Category
Skill Rules (St-Z) & Cross-Referencing

## Overview
The Swim skill represents your ability to stay afloat, navigate water currents, and move through water.

*   **Key Ability:** Strength (Str)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes — **multiplied by 2** (the ACP of your armor and shield is doubled and applied directly to Swim checks).
*   **Encumbrance:** Carrying a medium or heavy load makes swimming significantly more difficult (see [encumbrance.md](./encumbrance.md)).

---

## Mechanics

### Actions and Movement Speeds
*   **Standard Swim (Move Action):** You move at up to **one-quarter (1/4)** your normal land speed.
*   **Standard Swim (Full-Round Action):** You move at up to **one-half (1/2)** your normal land speed.
*   **Fast Swim (Move or Full-Round Action):** By accepting a **-10 penalty** on your check, you can move at **twice** the standard rate (1/2 speed as a move action, full speed as a full-round action).

### Swim DCs by Water Quality

| Water Condition | Swim DC | Take 10 Allowed? |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Calm water** (pool, slow river) | 10 | Yes |
| **Rough water** (rapids, heavy waves) | 15 | Yes |
| **Stormy water** (ocean storm, flooding) | 20 | No |

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You move the designated distance.
*   **Failure by 4 or less:** You make no progress but stay afloat.
*   **Failure by 5 or more:** You slip below the surface, go underwater, and begin to drown. You must hold your breath (see Suffocation in [natural-hazards.md](./natural-hazards.md)).

---

## Underwater Movement and Jumping

### Underwater Swimming
*   When fully submerged, swimming creatures can move in any direction (including vertically) as if flying with **perfect maneuverability**.

### Jumping in Water
*   **With Natural Swim Speed:** You can leap out of the water. You use your swim speed to determine the distance/height, but you take a **-10 penalty** on high jumps. The final height measures how far your feet or tail clear the water.
*   **Without Natural Swim Speed:** You cannot jump while in water.

---

## Natural Swim Speed
Creatures with a natural swim speed (such as aquatic elves, merfolk, or fish) gain major benefits:
*   **No Checks:** Can move through water at their swim speed without making checks.
*   **Racial Bonus:** Gain a **+8 racial bonus** on any Swim check made to perform a special maneuver or avoid a hazard.
*   **Stress Resistance:** Can always choose to **Take 10** on Swim checks, even if distracted or endangered.
*   **Running:** Can run while swimming, provided they move in a straight line.
