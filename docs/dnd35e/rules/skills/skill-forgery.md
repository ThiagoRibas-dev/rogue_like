# Skill: Forgery
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Forgery skill represents your ability to fake documents, signatures, seals, or records, as well as detect such falsifications.

*   **Key Ability:** Intelligence (Int)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Creating a Forgery
*   **Action:** Fabricating a standard document takes **1d4 $\times$ 10 minutes** of work (more complex documents can take hours or days).
*   **Secret Check:** The DM rolls your Forgery check secretly. This result serves as the DC for anyone trying to detect the forgery.

### Examining a Forgery
*   **Passive Acceptance:** A reader automatically accepts a forged document as genuine unless they have a specific reason to suspect it.
*   **Active Examination:** If a reader is suspicious, they make an opposed **Forgery check** against your original creation check result. 

### Examiner Modifier Table
The examiner's opposed check is modified based on their familiarity with the writing style and document type:

| Examiner's Condition | Check Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Familiar with the handwriting** | +0 |
| **Doesn't know the handwriting** | -2 |
| **Familiar with the document type** | +0 |
| **Doesn't know the document type** | -2 |
| **Highly suspicious** (e.g., contradicts orders, requires sacrifice) | +2 to +4 |

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success (Opposed Check):** The reader is fooled by the document and accepts it as authentic.
*   **Failure (Opposed Check):** The reader detects the forgery.
*   **Single-Roll Rule:** 
    *   You make only one check per forged document. That same result is compared against every examiner who reads it.
    *   An examiner can only attempt to detect a forgery on a specific document once.
    *   If an examiner detects a forgery, you cannot attempt to convince them otherwise, though the document may still fool others who have not examined it.

---

## Special Interactions
*   **Favorable Forgeries:** At the DM's discretion, a successfully presented forged document can grant a **+2 circumstance bonus** on a related Bluff, Diplomacy, or Intimidate check (e.g., forged orders to pass a guard).
    *   *Warning:* If the examiner detects the forgery, the social skill check automatically fails, and the character faces immediate consequences.
