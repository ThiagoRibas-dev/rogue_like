# Skill: Ride
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Ride skill represents your ability to handle mounts in combat and difficult situations. Routine riding (saddling, mounting, dismounting) does not require a check.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Ride checks)

---

## Mechanics

### Baseline Penalties
*   **Ill-suited mount:** -5 penalty on all Ride checks (e.g., riding a camel into water, riding a bear).
*   **No saddle:** -5 penalty on all Ride checks.

### Standard Action Tasks

| Task | DC | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Guide with Knees** | 5 | Free both hands for combat; check at start of turn. Fail = one hand only. |
| **Stay in Saddle** | 5 | When mount rears, bolts, or you take damage. |
| **Fight with a combat-trained mount** | 10 | Non-combat mounts require DC 20 to control in battle. |
| **Soft Fall** | 15 | Take no damage from an unexpected dismount. Failure = 1d6 damage. |
| **Unseat an opponent** | 10 + target's Ride | Opposed check to knock a rider from their mount. |
| **Leap obstacles** | — | Use your Ride modifier *or* the mount's Jump modifier, whichever is lower. |
| **Spur mount** | 15 | Push mount to move faster (details in mount's stat block). |

### Move Action Tasks

| Task | DC | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Control nonwar mount in battle** | 20 | Required each round to keep a nonwar mount moving in combat. |
| **Swim while mounted (calm)** | 10 | Fail = mount swims at half speed + -5 on Swim. Fail by 5+ = unsaddled. |
| **Swim while mounted (rough/underwater)** | 15 | As above. |
| **Swim while mounted (stormy)** | 20 | As above. |
| **Enter water on land mount** | 15 | Failure = mount refuses. Fail by 5+ = mount balks, throws rider. |

### No-Action Tasks
These are performed as part of movement without spending an action:

| Task | DC | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Cover (use mount as cover)** | 15 | Must declare before attack roll. Cannot attack, cast, or charge while using cover. |

### Free Action Tasks

| Task | DC | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Fast Mount or Dismount** | 20 | Quick mount/dismount; must still have a move action available. Failure = it becomes a move action instead. |

---

## Mounted Combat
When mounted, you and your mount function as a single unit:
*   **Your Mount's Actions:** The mount uses its own actions to move. You can direct it to use its natural attacks as part of a full attack or a charge.
*   **Combat-Trained vs. Untrained:** A combat-trained mount can fight in combat without requiring you to make a DC 20 Ride check each round. An untrained mount will not fight and requires the DC 20 check each round just to advance toward enemies.
*   **Mounted Charging:** When you charge while mounted, you move the mount and resolve the charge normally, gaining the +2 bonus on your attack roll and the -2 AC penalty.

## Synergy Bonus
*   **5+ ranks in Handle Animal:** You gain a **+2 synergy bonus** on Ride checks (see [skill-handle-animal.md](./skill-handle-animal.md)).
