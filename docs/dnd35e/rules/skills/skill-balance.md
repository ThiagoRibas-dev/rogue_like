# Skill: Balance
## Category
Skill Rules (A-D)

## Overview
The Balance skill allows you to walk along a narrow, precariously sloped, or slippery surface without falling.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Balance checks)

---

## Mechanics

### Movement
*   **Half Speed:** A successful Balance check allows you to move up to half your speed as a move action along a precarious surface.
*   **Full Speed:** You can move up to your full speed as a move action by accepting a **-5 penalty** on your Balance check.
*   **Running:** You can attempt to run across a narrow surface by taking a **-20 penalty** on your check.

### Surface Width and DCs

| Surface Width | DC | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **7 to 11 inches** | 10 | Wide ledge, stone wall top |
| **2 to 6 inches** | 15 | Rafter, tightrope |
| **Less than 2 inches** | 20 | Thin wire, narrow branch |

### Surface Condition Modifiers (Stacking)

| Surface Condition | DC Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Slippery** (wet, icy, greasy) | +5 |
| **Sloped or Angled** | +5 |
| **Uneven** (rocky, rubble) | +5 |

*Note: If a surface is extremely slippery and sloped, both modifiers apply.*

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You move the designated distance.
*   **Failure by 4 or Less:** You do not move for 1 round, but you maintain your balance.
*   **Failure by 5 or More:** You lose your balance and fall (see [natural-hazards.md](./natural-hazards.md) for falling damage rules).

---

## Special Rules

### Being Attacked while Balancing
*   **Flat-Footed:** You are considered flat-footed while balancing, losing your Dexterity bonus to AC.
    *   *Exception:* If you have **5 or more ranks** in Balance, you retain your Dexterity bonus to AC while balancing.
*   **Taking Damage:** If you take damage while balancing, you must immediately make a new Balance check against the surface's DC. If you fail, you fall prone or fall off the surface.

### Resisting Trip Attacks (Anti-Trip)
*   If you have **10 or more ranks** in Balance, you can choose to make a Balance check at a **-10 penalty** in place of a Strength or Dexterity check to resist being tripped by an opponent (see [trip.md](./trip.md)).
*   If you succeed, you remain standing but you cannot attempt to trip your opponent back.

---

## Special Interactions
*   **Charging:** Charging across a precarious surface requires a Balance check. Failure by 4 or less means you cannot charge, but you can act normally. Failure by 5 or more means you fall.
*   **Hampered Movement:** Balance checks cannot be made on surfaces that prohibit movement. See [tactical-movement.md](./tactical-movement.md).
