# Skill: Spellcraft
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Spellcraft skill represents academic knowledge of magic theory and the ability to analyze, identify, and work with magical effects in real time.

*   **Key Ability:** Intelligence (Int)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Task DCs

| Task | Time | Retry | DC |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Identify a spell being cast**¹ | — | No | 15 + spell level |
| **Identify a spell from its materials/effects**² | — | No | 20 + spell level |
| **Decipher a written spell** (without *read magic*) | Full-Round Action | No | 20 + spell level |
| **Determine the school of a spell aura** (via *detect magic*) | — | No | 15 + spell level |
| **Identify greater glyph of warding** (via *read magic*) | — | No | 16 |
| **Learn a spell from a spellbook or scroll**³ | 8 hours | No | 15 + spell level |
| **Understand a strange or unique magical effect** | Varies | No | 30 or higher |
| **Identify materials created or shaped by magic** | — | No | 20 + spell level |

¹ *You must be able to see or hear the spell's verbal or somatic components.*
² *You must be able to see or detect the effects of the spell.*
³ *No retry for that specific spell until you gain at least 1 additional rank in Spellcraft, even if you find another copy of the spell.*

> [!NOTE]
> Identifying a spell being cast is **not an action** — it happens as a reaction when you witness spellcasting. You either know the spell or you do not; a check determines success.

---

## Secret Checks
Certain Spellcraft checks are made secretly by the DM:
*   **Learning a Spell from a Spellbook/Scroll:** The DM rolls this check secretly, so you do not know until later whether you successfully committed the spell to memory.

---

## Key Interactions
*   **Counterspelling:** You must successfully identify a spell being cast (Spellcraft DC 15 + spell level) before you can counterspell it. Failing this check means you cannot attempt the counterspell that round.
*   **Arcane Spellbook Research:** When scribing new spells into your spellbook, Spellcraft is used to calculate the time and cost of transcription.
*   **Dispel Magic:** Spellcraft does not directly affect *dispel magic* checks, but knowledge of the target spell's caster level (identified via Spellcraft) can inform tactical decisions.
*   **Brew Potion / Scribe Scroll / etc.:** Some item creation feats reference Spellcraft proficiency as a prerequisite.

---

## Synergy Bonuses
*   **5+ ranks in Knowledge (Arcana):** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Spellcraft checks (see [skill-knowledge.md](./skill-knowledge.md)).
*   **5+ ranks in Spellcraft:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Use Magic Device checks to decipher scrolls (see [magic-items-general.md](./magic-items-general.md)).
