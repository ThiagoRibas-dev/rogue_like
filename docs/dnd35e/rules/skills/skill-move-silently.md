# Skill: Move Silently
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Move Silently skill represents your ability to move stealthily without making noise, allowing you to sneak past guards, stalk prey, or move without revealing your location.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Move Silently checks)

---

## Mechanics

### Requirements
*   **Opposed Check:** Your check is opposed by the **Listen** checks of any creatures close enough to hear you (see [skill-listen.md](./skill-listen.md)).

### Speed Penalties
Moving quickly makes it harder to remain quiet:

| Movement Type | Check Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Move up to 1/2 speed** | +0 |
| **Move between 1/2 and normal speed** | -5 |
| **Running or Charging** | -20 |

### Environmental Modifiers
The surface you move across affects the difficulty of moving silently:

| Surface Type | Check Modifier | Examples |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Noisy** | -2 | Undergrowth, shallow water pools, light debris. |
| **Very Noisy** | -5 | Dense undergrowth, deep dry leaves, heavy snow. |

---

## Outcomes

*   **Success:** You move the designated distance without making audible noise.
*   **Failure:** Opponents whose Listen checks exceed your check result hear you. They can determine your direction and approximate distance.
*   **Pinpointing:** If an opponent beats your check result by **20 or more**, they pinpoint your exact square (even if you have total concealment or are invisible).
