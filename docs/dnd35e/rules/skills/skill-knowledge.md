# Skill: Knowledge
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Knowledge skill represents a study of a particular topic, encompassing academic research, memory, and experience. Knowledge is split into several distinct subcategories, each treated as a separate skill.

*   **Key Ability:** Intelligence (Int)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
    *   *Exception:* Untrained characters can make checks to recall very common or easy facts (**DC 10 or lower**), but cannot attempt checks for harder questions.
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Subcategories

1.  **Arcana:** Ancient mysteries, magic traditions, arcane symbols, constructs, dragons, magical beasts.
2.  **Architecture and Engineering:** Buildings, aqueducts, bridges, fortifications, cave-ins (see [natural-hazards.md](./natural-hazards.md)).
3.  **Dungeoneering:** Spelunking, underground hazards, caverns, aberrations, oozes.
4.  **Geography:** Lands, terrain, climate, maps, navigation.
5.  **History:** Wars, colonies, founding of cities, historical figures, legends.
6.  **Local:** Legends, personalities, laws, customs, humanoids (racial types).
7.  **Nature:** Seasons, weather, cycles, flora and fauna, animals, fey, giants, monstrous humanoids, plants, vermin.
8.  **Nobility and Royalty:** Lineage, heraldry, family trees, court etiquette.
9.  **Religion:** Gods, mythic history, ecclesiastical hierarchies, holy symbols, undead.
10. **The Planes:** Astral Plane, Ethereal Plane, Outer Planes, elementals, outsiders.
11. **Psionics:** Ancient mysteries, psionic traditions, psychic symbols, cryptic phrases, astral constructs, and psionic races.

## Mechanics

### Recalling Information
To answer a question or recall details about a topic, the DM assigns a DC based on the question's obscurity:

| Question Difficulty | DC | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Easy** | 10 | The name of the local baron; common folklore. |
| **Basic** | 15 | The layout of a standard fort; local regional laws. |
| **Tough** | 20 | The lineage of a foreign ruler; properties of common monsters. |
| **Extremely Tough** | 30 | Ancient lost ruins; obscure planar layers; long-dead legends. |

### Identifying Monsters
You can use Knowledge checks to identify monsters and learn about their weaknesses, resistances, and special abilities:
*   **DC:** **10 + monster's Hit Dice (HD)**.
*   **Outcome:** 
    *   *Success:* You identify the monster's name and type, and learn one useful piece of information about it.
    *   *Extra Success:* For every **5 points** by which your check beats the DC, you learn another useful piece of information (e.g., immunities, vulnerabilities, DR, special attacks).
*   **Monster Categories by Skill:**
    *   *Knowledge (Arcana):* Constructs, dragons, magical beasts.
    *   *Knowledge (Dungeoneering):* Aberrations, oozes.
    *   *Knowledge (Local):* Humanoids.
    *   *Knowledge (Nature):* Animals, fey, giants, monstrous humanoids, plants, vermin.
    *   *Knowledge (Religion):* Undead.
    *   *Knowledge (The Planes):* Elementals, outsiders.
    *   *Knowledge (Psionics):* Psionic monsters and astral constructs.

## Synergy Bonuses
*   **Knowledge (Arcana):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Spellcraft checks.
*   **Knowledge (Dungeoneering):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Survival checks made in underground environments.
*   **Knowledge (Geography):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Survival checks made to avoid getting lost.
*   **Knowledge (Local):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Gather Information checks.
*   **Knowledge (Nature):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Survival checks made in above-ground natural environments.
*   **Knowledge (Nobility and Royalty):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Diplomacy checks.
*   **Knowledge (The Planes):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Survival checks made on other planes.
*   **Knowledge (Psionics):** 5+ ranks grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Psicraft checks.
