# Skill: Handle Animal
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Handle Animal skill represents your ability to train, command, push, or rear domestic and wild animals.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
    *   *Exception:* Untrained characters can make checks to handle domestic animals (like horses, dogs, or mules) but cannot push them, teach them tricks, train them for purposes, or handle wild animals.
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Handling and Pushing (Active Commands)

During combat or exploration, you can command animals using these actions:

*   **Handle an Animal:** Command an animal to perform a trick it knows.
    *   *Action:* **Move Action** (or **Free Action** for Druids/Rangers directing their Animal Companions).
    *   *DC:* **10** (increases to **12** if the animal is injured or frightened).
*   **Push an Animal:** Direct an animal to perform a task or trick it does *not* know, or to perform a known trick while injured/frightened.
    *   *Action:* **Standard Action**.
    *   *DC:* **25**.

---

## Training Animals (Downtime Tasks)

### 1. Teaching a Trick
An animal can learn a maximum number of tricks based on its Intelligence score (usually 3 tricks per point of Int; Int 1 = 3 tricks, Int 2 = 6 tricks).
*   *Time:* **1 week** of work.
*   *DC:* **15** for standard tricks, **20** for complex tricks.
*   *Standard Tricks:* Attack, Come, Defend, Down, Fetch, Guard, Heel, Perform, Seek, Stay, Track, Work.

### 2. Training for a General Purpose
Instead of teaching individual tricks, you can train an animal for a package of tricks:

| General Purpose | DC | Time Required | Tricks Taught |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Riding** | 15 | 3 weeks | Come, Heel, Stay. |
| **Heavy Labor** | 15 | 2 weeks | Come, Work. |
| **Performance** | 15 | 5 weeks | Come, Fetch, Heel, Perform, Stay. |
| **Guarding** | 15 | 4 weeks | Attack, Defend, Guard, Heel. |
| **Fighting** | 20 | 3 weeks | Attack, Down, Stay. |
| **Hunting** | 20 | 6 weeks | Attack, Down, Fetch, Heel, Seek, Track. |
| **Combat Riding** | 20 | 6 weeks | Attack, Come, Defend, Down, Guard, Heel (mount stands firm in combat). |

### 3. Rearing Wild Beasts
You can raise a wild beast from infancy, domesticating it.
*   *Time:* **1 year** or more of raising.
*   *DC:* **15 + creature's Hit Dice**.
*   *Limit:* You can rear up to three animals of the same species simultaneously.
