# Skill: Hide
## Category
Skill Rules (E-L)

## Overview
The Hide skill is used to conceal yourself from visual detection, move unseen across open ground, or blend into crowds.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** Yes (applied directly to Hide checks)

---

## Mechanics

### Requirements
*   **Cover or Concealment:** You must have cover, concealment, or a special ability (such as *Hide in Plain Sight*) to attempt a Hide check. If you have total cover or total concealment, a check is usually unnecessary.
*   **Observation:** You cannot hide if you are currently being observed, even casually.
*   **Opposed Check:** Your check is opposed by the **Spot** checks of any creatures looking for you (see [skill-spot.md](./skill-spot.md)).

## Advanced Applications

### 1. Sniping
If you are already hidden at least 10 feet away from your target, you can make a ranged attack and immediately hide again:
*   **Action:** Standard action (attack) followed by a **Move Action** (to hide).
*   **Penalty:** You take a **-20 penalty** on your Hide check to conceal yourself after shooting.

### 2. Moving between Cover
If you are already hidden and have **5 or more ranks** in Hide, you can attempt to cross open ground to reach another hiding spot:
*   **Limit:** You can cross up to **5 feet of open ground for every 5 ranks** in Hide you possess.
*   **Penalty:** You take a **-5 penalty** on your Hide check for every 5 feet of open ground crossed. Movement speed penalties apply as normal.

### 3. Sneaking up from Hiding
You can emerge from cover to sneak up on an observer in the open:
*   **Penalty:** You take a **-5 penalty** on your Hide check for every 5 feet of open space between you and the target.
*   **Success:** The target does not notice you until you attack or perform an action, and is treated as **flat-footed** against you.

### 4. Hiding under Distraction
If observers are casually watching you, you can attempt to hide if they are momentarily distracted (such as by an ally's Bluff check to create a diversion):
*   **Action:** Part of movement.
*   **Limit:** You must reach a hiding place within **1 foot per rank** you have in Hide.
*   **Penalty:** You take a **-10 penalty** on the check because you must move incredibly fast.

### 5. Blending into a Crowd
*   You can attempt a Hide check to blend into a crowd of people.
*   *Restriction:* This only hides you from observers scanning the area from a distance. You remain visible to creatures immediately adjacent to you in the crowd.
