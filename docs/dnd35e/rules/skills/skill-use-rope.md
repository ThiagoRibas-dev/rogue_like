# Skill: Use Rope
## Category
Skill Rules (St-Z) & Cross-Referencing

## Overview
The Use Rope skill is used to tie secure knots, splice ropes, bind prisoners, and throw grappling hooks safely.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Time and Action Type
*   **Tie a Knot / Bind yourself:** **Full-Round Action** (provokes attacks of opportunity).
*   **Splice two ropes:** **5 minutes** of work.
*   **Throw a grappling hook:** **Standard Action** (provokes attacks of opportunity).
*   **Bind a creature:** **1 minute** of work.

---

## Task DCs

| Task | DC | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Tie a firm knot** | 10 | Standard secure knot. |
| **Tie a special knot** | 15 | A knot that slips or loosens with a specific tug. |
| **Secure a grappling hook** | 10¹ | Opposed by a secret check. |
| **Bind a creature** | Opposed check | Your check receives a **+10 bonus** (see below). |

¹ *Add **+2 to the DC** for every 10 feet the hook is thrown (maximum DC 20 at 50 feet or more).*

---

## Task Details

### 1. Securing a Grappling Hook
Securing a hook requires a secret Use Rope check made by the DM:
*   **Failure by 4 or less:** The hook fails to catch, falling down. You can attempt to throw it again.
*   **Failure by 5 or more:** The hook catches and appears secure, but it will come loose after **1d4 rounds** of supporting weight.

### 2. Binding a Creature
When you tie up a prisoner with ropes, you make a Use Rope check:
*   **Opposed Escape:** Any Escape Artist check made by the prisoner to escape is opposed by your original Use Rope check result. You gain a **+10 bonus** on this check because it is mechanically easier to bind someone than to escape.
*   **Bursting the Rope:** If the prisoner tries to break the ropes using strength rather than escape, they must exceed the rope's break DC (normally **23** for standard hemp rope).

---

## Synergy Bonuses
*   **5+ ranks in Use Rope:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Escape Artist checks when escaping rope bindings.
*   **5+ ranks in Use Rope:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Sleight of Hand checks to hide small objects (like daggers) on your person using rope wraps.
