# Skill: Open Lock
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Open Lock skill represents your ability to pick mechanical locks using thieves' tools.

*   **Key Ability:** Dexterity (Dex)
*   **Trained Only:** Yes
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

---

## Mechanics

### Requirements
*   **Tools Required:** You must have **thieves' tools** to attempt an Open Lock check. Without them, you take a **-2 penalty** on the check (improvised tools) or cannot attempt it at all (bare hands only).
*   **Trapfinding:** Picking a lock that is also a trap element requires the *Trapfinding* class feature (see [skill-disable-device.md](./skill-disable-device.md)).

### Time
*   **Action:** **Full-Round Action**.
*   **Retry:** Allowed, but each failed attempt alerts anyone listening nearby.

### DCs by Lock Quality

| Lock Type | DC | Examples |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Very Simple** | 20 | Cheap padlock, basic barrel lock. |
| **Average** | 25 | Standard door lock, wagon padlock. |
| **Good** | 30 | Quality door lock, small chest lock. |
| **Amazing** | 40 | Gnomish puzzle lock, fine vault lock. |

> [!NOTE]
> A *knock* spell opens all locks regardless of DC, making the Open Lock skill redundant if the party has access to that spell. Consider investing ranks elsewhere (e.g., Tumble) unless you know you will need reliable lock-picking without magic.

---

## Outcomes
*   **Success:** The lock opens. A locked door, chest, or container becomes accessible.
*   **Failure:** The lock does not open. You may attempt again next round.
