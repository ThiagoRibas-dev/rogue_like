# Skill: Perform
## Category
Skill Rules (M-Sp)

## Overview
The Perform skill represents training and talent in a specific performing art. It is used to entertain an audience, earn a living as a performer, and provides the mechanical backbone for Bard class abilities.

*   **Key Ability:** Charisma (Cha)
*   **Trained Only:** No
*   **Armor Check Penalty:** No

Perform is divided into distinct subcategories, each treated as a separate skill:

1.  **Act** (stage drama)
2.  **Comedy** (buffoonery, wit, clowning)
3.  **Dance**
4.  **Keyboards** (harpsichord, pipe organ, spinet)
5.  **Oratory** (epic, legend, storytelling)
6.  **Percussion** (drums, bells, chimes)
7.  **String Instruments** (fiddle, harp, lute, mandolin)
8.  **Wind Instruments** (flute, horn, recorder, shawm)
9.  **Sing**

## Mechanics

### Earning Income
You can use Perform to earn money by giving a performance. This is primarily a downtime activity (see [downtime-activities.md](./downtime-activities.md)).

| Perform Check Result | Crowd Reaction / Earnings |
| :---: | :--- |
| **10 or lower** | Uninspiring. Earn **3d6 cp** per day of performance. |
| **11–15** | Locally known as a minor talent. Earn **1d6 sp** per day. |
| **16–20** | Comfortable living as a performer. Earn **3d6 sp** per day. |
| **21–25** | Gain the patronage of local nobles. Earn **1d6 gp** per day. |
| **26–30** | Celebrated regionally. Earn **3d6 gp** per day. |
| **31+** | Legendary performer. Earn **1d6 pp** per day. |

---

## Bard-Specific Uses
Bards rely on Perform ranks for their class features:
*   **Bardic Music:** You must have at least **3 ranks** in an appropriate Perform subcategory to use bardic music abilities (Inspire Courage, Inspire Competence, Fascinate, Suggestion, etc.).
*   **Countersong:** A Perform check (whichever subcategory you choose) is made to counter sonic or language-dependent magical effects.
*   **Fascinate:** A Perform check is made opposed by each target's Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 Bard level + Cha modifier).

---

## Synergy Bonus
*   **5+ ranks in Perform:** Grants a **+2 synergy bonus** on Bluff checks (experience with deception through performance) and on Diplomacy checks (experience with reading and working a crowd).
