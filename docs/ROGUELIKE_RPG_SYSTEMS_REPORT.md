# Systemic Roguelike & Interactive RPG Mechanics Report

> **Scope:** Cross-genre research into classic roguelikes (*NetHack*, *Brogue*, *Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup*, *Incursion*, *Caves of Qud*, *Cataclysm: DDA*) and systemic CRPGs (*Baldur's Gate*, *Divinity: Original Sin*, *Stoneshard*, *Tales of Maj'Eyal*), mapped directly onto this project's **Tier 1 Console Engine** + **Tier 2 Ruleset Cartridge (`dnd35e`)** architecture.

---

## 1. Current Baseline Assessment (What Exists Today)

Before recommending new systems, here is the existing interactive surface observed in the codebase so new proposals snap cleanly onto existing primitives instead of duplicating work:

1. **Polymorphic Verb Pipeline Ready for Deeper Wiring ([`src/constants/verbs.constants.ts`](../src/constants/verbs.constants.ts), [`src/systems/apply.system.ts`](../src/systems/apply.system.ts)):**
   - The engine defines 40 `Verb`s (`apply`, `throw`, `kick`, `open`, `close`, `lock`, `unlock`, `search`, `disarm`, `feint`, `heal`, `dip`, `zap`, `ignite`, `read`, `eat`, `drink`, `impact`, trade, social, and companion verbs).
   - Today, D&D skill verbs (`search`, `unlock`, `disarm`, `heal`, `persuade`, `intimidate`, `feint`) and trade/companion verbs have rich handlers, while `dip`, `ignite`, `kick`, `open`, `close`, `lock`, and `read` fall through to campaign-specific `ReactionDefinition`s without generic Console systems behind them.
2. **Spatial Fields & Anchored Auras ([`src/systems/field.system.ts`](../src/systems/field.system.ts)):**
   - Supports decaying, spreading, and entity-anchored fields with `blocksSight` FOV invalidation (`fovNeedsUpdate`). Does not yet evaluate cross-element/field transmutations (oil + fire, water + cold, water + electricity).
3. **Traps, Locks & Light Exposure ([`world_components.types.ts`](../src/types/components/world_components.types.ts), [`light_exposure.ts`](../src/rulesets/dnd35e/spatial/light_exposure.ts)):**
   - `TrapComponent` already includes `canReset` & `resetByEntityId` (used by `dungeon_skill_verbs.ts` when beating disarm DC by `TRAP_RESET_MARGIN = 5`).
   - `LockComponent` already declares `breakable?: boolean` and `jammed?: boolean`.
   - `evaluateActorLightExposure` already checks `lightReading?.isBright` for `trait:light_blindness`, while 81 SRD adventuring items (`torch`, `hooded_lantern`, `bullseye_lantern`, `caltrops`, `marbles`, `flour_pouch`, `oil_flask`, `acid_flask`, `alchemists_fire`, `smokestick`, `thunderstone`, `iron_spike`, `crowbar`, `portable_ram`, `healers_kit`, `disguise_kit`, `manacles`, `wizards_spellbook`, `bandoleer`, `potion_belt`, etc.) exist in data from Milestone 149 waiting for active systemic mechanics.
4. **World Clock & Macro Cadence ([`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](./ARCHITECTURE.md)):**
   - Dual **Turn-Based & RTwP** scheduler, `HungerComponent` (`hunger.system.ts`), `rest.system.ts`, and `Tier 1 / 2 / 3` simulation cadences (`Nemesis`, `Rivalry`, `Schemes`, `World Events`).

## 2. Recommended Systems Matrix (Console vs. D&D 3.5e Cartridge)

To preserve **Rule 16 (Three-Tier Placement)**, **Rule 18 (Universal Actor Symmetry)**, and **Rule 21 (Zero Content-ID Hardcoding)**, every proposed feature is split into **Tier 1 Console primitives** (reusable across `classic`, `dnd35e`, or any future ruleset) and **Tier 2 Cartridge resolvers** (expressing D&D 3.5e skill checks, saves, action costs, and feat/trait modifiers declaratively).

> **Universal Actor Symmetry Mandate (Rule 18):** None of the systems below are "player-only" or "monster-only" special cases. Every mechanic—stealth vs. detection, acoustic investigation, light/darkness penalties, surface chemistry, encumbrance/load tiers, belt vs. backpack action economy, door/lock/barricade manipulation, liquid submersion/breath, gap jumping/climbing, world-owned bridges/ropes, poison stages, and spellbook drops—applies identically to the player, companions, NPCs, and monsters through shared ECS components, `canTraverseTile` evaluations, and Cartridge Stat-DAG / skill resolvers.

### Pillar A — Sensory Stealth, Acoustics & Dynamic Illumination
*Inspirations: Brogue, Thief, Caves of Qud, DCSS, Incursion*

| Layer | Feature & Mechanism | Why It Improves Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **Acoustic Sound Ripple System (`emitSoundEvent`):** Flood-fill sound propagation attenuated by walls and closed doors (`tile.soundAttenuation` / `door.closed`). Heavy footsteps, combat hits, `kick`, failed locks, and `thunderstone`s emit `(x, y, dbLevel)`. Nearby AI stores `lastHeardPos` + `alertnessState: 'unaware' \| 'suspicious' \| 'alerted'` and walks to investigate before gaining LOS; player HUD also receives directional audio cues (`"You hear heavy footsteps to the east"`) from unseen noisy actors. | Eliminates "hive-mind omniscient aggro" and makes doors, stealth, distractions (throwing a coin/stone!), and ambushes genuinely tactical for both player and AI. |
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **Dynamic Light Sources & Fuel/Cone System (`LightEmitterComponent`):** Worn/held/dropped entities with `lightSource: { radiusFt, coneAngleDeg?, fuelTurns?, requiresIgnition? }` project radial or directional (`bullseye_lantern` 60-ft cone) illumination onto the map's `lightGrid` and drain fuel over time. `ignite` / `dip` verbs light torches (`flint_and_steel`, `tindertwig`) and refill lanterns (`oil_flask`). Guard patrols and human bandits carry real light sources that can be snuffed or dropped! | Makes darkness a real tactical resource rather than cosmetic flavor; pairs directly with stealth, ambush, and `oil_flask` throws. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **Full Symmetric Stealth vs. Detection Loop (`Hide` & `Move Silently` vs. `Spot` & `Listen`):** Toggleable *Stealth* mode (`stance`) resolves opposed `Hide` (gated by cover, `light:darkness`, or `light:shadowy` concealment, penalized by size) vs. `Spot`, and `Move Silently` (with Armour Check Penalty; `-5` if moving > half speed, or `-20` if running/charging) vs. `Listen` on every sound pulse—evaluated symmetrically whether the player stalks an orc or a goblin assassin stalks the party. Adds **Darkvision**, **Low-Light Vision**, **Scent** (detects hidden foes within 30 ft regardless of `Hide`), and **Tremorsense** counters. | Directly empowers Rogues, Rangers, Scouts, and stealthy monsters; turns stealth into an active, two-way tactical loop instead of passive stat bloat. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **Counter-Invisibility Utility (`flour_pouch` & Dust/Glitter):** Throwing `flour_pouch` spawns a dust burst applying a `dusted` condition/tag to any actor in the tile that reduces `invisible` / total-concealment miss chance (`50% -> 20%`) and negates `Hide` until cleaned. | Gives martial/non-caster actors smart tactical counters to invisible stalkers or casting enemies using cheap M149 gear. |

### Pillar B — Elemental Surface Chemistry & Dungeon Hazard Physics
*Inspirations: Brogue, Divinity: Original Sin, NetHack, Dungeons of Dredmor*

| Layer | Feature & Mechanism | Why It Improves Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **Tag-Driven Field & Surface Transmutation Engine:** Extend `field.system.ts` with declarative `FieldInteractionRule`s (`incomingTags` × `existingFieldOrTileTag` → `spawnField / extinguish / chainSpread`):<br>• `oil` + `fire` → **Ignites connected oil slick** into roaring flames.<br>• `water` + `cold` → **Ice patch / Frozen surface** (slippery traversal hazard; also bridges deep water!).<br>• `water` + `electricity` → **Conductive shock** across all orthogonally connected water tiles.<br>• `fire` + `water` → **Dense Steam Cloud** (`blocksSight: true`).<br>• **Smoke/Fog Obscurement (`smokestick`, `obscuring_mist`):** Dynamically toggles `blocksSight` on `FieldComponent` so any actor can block line-of-sight from archers/casters. | Turns combat rooms into reactive chemistry sandboxes where positioning and throwables beat raw DPS checks. |
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **`Dip` & Coat Pipeline (`dip` verb):** Generic Console mechanic letting any actor apply a liquid/vial entity (`toolEntityId`) onto a weapon, stack of ammo, or container (consuming 1 charge and attaching a timed/hit-limited `ItemCoatingOverlay` with `onHit` status/damage tags). | Unlocks poisoning arrows, blessing bolts with `holy_water`, coating blades in oil to ignite on hit, and refilling lanterns cleanly through one Console primitive. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **SRD `Caltrops`, `Marbles` & `Grease` Field Resolution:**<br>• **Caltrops:** Any actor entering or fighting in a caltrop tile triggers a BAB `+0` attack ignoring armor/shield/deflection (footwear adds `+2` AC); hit deals `1` piercing damage and halves speed until `Heal DC 15` or `1+ HP` magical healing (halting both player and monster charges cleanly!).<br>• **Marbles / Ice / Grease:** Entering requires `Balance DC 10–15` (or Reflex save) or the actor falls `prone`, provoking AoOs when standing up. | Gives immediate mechanical teeth to M149's `caltrops`, `marbles`, `oil_flask`, and terrain control spells for all combatants. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **Poison Risk & `Craft (Alchemy)` Identification:** Applying injury poison to a weapon triggers a 5% natural-1 mishap check unless the actor has the `poison_use` feature; `Craft (Alchemy)` checks identify potions without drinking (`DC 15`) and brew `antitoxin` / `alchemists_fire` / `acid_flask` at alchemy benches/camps. | Elevates `Craft (Alchemy)` from a dead character-sheet skill into high-utility dungeon survival. |

### Pillar C — Centralized Spatial Displacement Kernel, Explicit Charge, Universal Terrain Traversal & Destructible Architecture
*Inspirations: Brogue, NetHack, Cataclysm: DDA, Baldur's Gate, Divinity: Original Sin*

| Layer | Feature & Mechanism | Why It Improves Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **Centralized Spatial Displacement Kernel (`displaceEntityStep` / `displaceEntityAlongVector`):** Consolidates **every** in-map actor position change (`voluntary_walk`, `charge_step`, `forced_push`, `forced_pull`, `teleport`, `swap`) into one SSOT pipeline. Enforces multi-tile footprints & squeezing (`resolveMovementFootprintPlacement`), `spatialIndex` & `fovNeedsUpdate` sync, anchored field sync, traps, `TileEntered` events, and mode-aware `runActorMovementStep(..., displacementContext)` across all callers—eliminating the fragmented silos in `bull_rush_resolver.ts`, `immediate_reactions.ts` (Abrupt Jaunt), and friendly swaps. | Guarantees 100% spatial consistency: pushing an ogre preserves its 2×2 footprint, triggers traps/fields/sunlight on every crossed tile, distinguishes solid wall-slams (Dungeon Crasher) from open ledges, and prevents teleports from skipping traps or anchored auras. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **Explicit Triggered Charge Action (Removing Passive 10-ft Charge):** Replaces passive `distanceMovedFt >= 10` detection in `dnd35e_combat_adapter.ts` with an explicit targeted **Charge** action (shared symmetrically by players and AI via `displaceEntityAlongVector` with `mode: 'charge_step'`). Enforces straight-line unobstructed traversal (blocked by difficult terrain, hazards, or occupied squares unless overridden by declarative `RuleOverride`s like *Nimble Charge* / *Woodland Stride*), grants `+2` attack + Pounce/Charging Smite riders, and cleanly applies the SRD **`-2` AC penalty** until the charger's next turn without ever penalizing ordinary walking steps. | Faithful to PHB p. 154 and prevents players or AI from accidentally taking `-2` AC penalties just by walking 10 ft before a normal melee attack. |
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **Three-Tier Passability & A* Cost Heuristics (`evaluateTileTraversal`):** Upgrade `canTraverseTile` (shared between the Displacement Kernel and AI/companion `computeNextStepToward`) from a binary wall check into a structured evaluation returning `passability: 'unimpeded' | 'difficult' | 'hazardous' | 'solid_obstacle'`, `moveCostMultiplier`, `triggersSolidCollision`, `blocksCharge`, and declarative `hazardKind`:<br>1. **Unimpeded:** Standard movement cost (walkable floor, or native `fly > 0` / `swim > 0` / `burrow > 0` matching `tile.traversableBy`).<br>2. **Difficult / Hazardous:** Passable with elevated cost (`2×` shallow water/bog) or risk (`deep_water`, `chasm_gap`, `pit`), evaluated symmetrically on entry (and weighted in AI A* pathfinding!).<br>3. **Solid Obstacle:** Solid walls, closed doors, or pillars (`triggersSolidCollision: true`). | Ensures players, companions, and monsters obey the exact same spatial physics, charge-line rules, forced-push ledge drops, and pathfinding trade-offs. |
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **World-Owned Crossing Entities (Bridges, Ropes, Ladders, Ice Sheets):** Instead of an actor-internal "has rope = ignore holes" bypass, deploying `grappling_hook` + `hempen_rope`/`silk_rope`, laying a `ladder_10ft`, or freezing water stamps a real world entity/surface (`bridge`, `rope_line`, `frozen_surface`) onto the tile.<br>• **Full Tactical Symmetry:** Monsters, companions, and the player can all traverse a placed rope or ice bridge—and any actor can cut/untie the rope, smash the plank, or melt the ice (`ignite` / *Fireball*) while foes are mid-crossing! | Creates emergent "cut the bridge behind you" or "melt the ice under the armored knight" moments without bespoke scripting. |
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **Unified Forced-Movement & Edge/Submersion Hazards (`BreathComponent`):** Because `forced_push` / `forced_pull` runs through the Displacement Kernel, bull-rushing an actor into `deep_water` or a `chasm` automatically enters the hazard (`triggersSolidCollision: false`) instead of bouncing off air, and stops the attacker's follow-up safely at the ledge. Any breathing actor (lacking `trait:water_breathing`, `trait:undead`, or `trait:construct`) tracks remaining breath turns while submerged before taking drowning damage. | Rewards tactical shoves, Bull Rush builds, and positioning near ledges/water for both player and AI bruisers. |
| **Console (Tier 1)** | **Structural Integrity & Door/Container Agency (`StructuralComponent`, `open`/`close`/`kick`/`lock`):** Give doors, chests, barricades, bridges, altars, and crumbling walls `{ materialTag, hardness, hp, maxHp, breakDc }`. Wire `close` (seal behind you), `kick` (force stuck/locked doors), `lock` (seal with key), **Wedging/Spiking (`iron_spike` + `hammer`)** to block doors, and `pole_10ft` range-2 trap probing. Intelligent monsters can likewise bash or kick down wedged doors to reach their quarry! | Doors and bridges become active, destructible tactical barriers for all actors rather than static decorations. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **Water Depth Tiers (`shallow_water` vs. `deep_water`) & `Swim` (`2× ACP`):**<br>• **Shallow Water / Bog:** Walkable at `2×` movement cost, `-2` stealth/tumble penalty, extinguishes non-magical fires, conducts electricity.<br>• **Deep Water:** Native swimmers (`swim > 0`) move freely; all other actors roll `Swim` (`DC 10` calm / `DC 15` rough) on entry and each turn, applying **`2×` Armour Check Penalty**! Failing actors in heavy armor sink and burn `2 × CON` breath rounds unless aided, under *Water Walk*, or crossing via boat/rope/ice. | Turns Armor Check Penalty and creature swim speeds into decisive environmental factors; makes flooding or freezing a room lethal to heavy infantry. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **Gaps, Holes & Pits (`Jump`, `Climb`, `Use Rope`, `Balance`):**<br>• **Jumping Gaps (1–2 Tile Voids):** Any actor can attempt a `Jump` check (`DC 5` per 5-ft gap with running start, doubled from standing) to leap across a gap; failure triggers a Reflex ledge-catch save or pit fall (`1d6` per 10 ft + `prone`). High-Athletics monsters can leap gaps to chase the party!<br>• **Pit Climbing & Anchored Ropes:** `Climb` (`DC 10–15`, `+2` with `climbers_kit`) lets actors climb into or out of pits; anchoring a `grappling_hook` (`Use Rope DC 10`, `+2` with `silk_rope`) creates a shared line crossable via `Climb` / `Balance`. | Integrates `Jump`, `Climb`, `Balance`, `Use Rope`, `climbers_kit`, `grappling_hook`, `ladder_10ft`, and `pole_10ft` into a cohesive dungeoneering loop. |
| **Cartridge (D&D 3.5e)** | **SRD Hardness, Sunder & Break Checks:** Damage against objects/doors/bridges subtracts `hardness`, applies energy multipliers (sonic/acid vs. wood/stone; fire halved unless flammable), and lets `adamantine` ignore hardness `< 20`. `crowbar` grants `+2 circumstance` to Strength break checks; `portable_ram` adds `+2` (plus `+2` per adjacent aiding ally) while emitting a loud sound ripple. | Realizes D&D 3.5e's *Strike an Object* rules and distinguishes special weapon materials beyond monster DR. |

## 4. Finalized Design Decisions (Locked)

1. **Stealth & Unified 6-Second Round Clock (TB & RTwP):**
   - **Unified 6-Second Modulation:** Modulate actions, attacks, movement pacing, and stealth check cadence around continuous **6-second round blocks** across both Turn-Based and RTwP (NWN / Pathfinder model) for seamless TB ↔ RTwP switching and zero "death by a thousand rolls".
   - **Stealth Speed:** Entering Stealth defaults to **half-speed** (avoiding the `-5` `Move Silently` penalty), with a **Fast Stealth** toggle for full-speed movement.
   - **Check Cadence & Break Behavior:** Roll opposed stealth once per 6-second round, forcing an immediate re-check only on state changes (brighter light band, new observer LOS, opening a door, loud noise). Breaking stealth in RTwP uses **Option A — Strict Instant Break without Auto-Pause**; re-entering Stealth is manual via the Stealth skill (gated by unobserved/cover/concealment + 1-round cooldown).
2. **Rest Pressure / "Doom Clock" (Tier 1 Per-Area Configurable):**
   - Lives at Tier 1 Console as campaign defaults (`rules.json`) with **per-area overrides** (`AreaDefinition.restPressure`) exposing tunable parameters: `safeRest`, `ambushChancePerWatch`, `schemeTickMultiplier`, `respawnRateMultiplier`, and `alarmDecayTurns`.
   - Bulk time skipping (`world_time_skip.ts`) advances `processSchemeTurn` alongside Nemesis/Rivalry/World Events according to `schemeTickMultiplier`.
3. **Equipment & Object Durability Baseline (SRD *Smashing an Object* + Opt-In Console Wear):**
